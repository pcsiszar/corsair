# Corsair Core Lore Reference

This document serves as the immutable, authoritative reference for the core setting of *Corsair*. When writing rulebook chapters, narrative fiction, equipment lore, or campaign materials, all descriptions must align with the three layers outlined below.

## Layer 1: The Present Day (Flotilla & Corsairs)

Layer 1 is the everyday reality experienced by citizens, spacers, and operatives across Newcomer civilization.

### 1. The Corsairs
* **Mandate & Function**: Corsairs are autonomous specialist cells (typically 3–5 operatives on a single dedicated vessel) granted a cross-jurisdictional mandate to operate across both planetary gravity wells and orbital space. They resolve frictions, track threats, conduct investigations, and handle surgical tactical operations that neither planetary constabularies nor the Flotilla can address alone.
* **The Sponsor System**: Every legitimate Corsair is backed by a Sponsor (Flotilla, Colonial administration, Megacorporation, Civilian union/NGO, or Private patron). To ensure operational neutrality, chartered cells feature mixed sponsorships so no single faction controls the cell's agenda.
* **The Oversight Committee**: A governing board of exemplary former Corsairs and approved civilian delegates who review contract petitions for the common good, investigate agent misconduct, and hold the power to strip mandates.
* **Modes of Play**:
  * **Freelancers (Aspirants)**: Uncommissioned crews operating on foot, relying on commercial transport, taking low-level contracts to earn sponsor notice and purchase a hull.
  * **Corsairs (Chartered Cell)**: Standard commissioned cells with individual sponsors, a dedicated starship, and cross-jurisdictional legal authority.
  * **Rogue Corsairs (Outlaws)**: Former Corsairs who lost their charter, broke neutrality, or went rogue; operating un-flagged ships in black markets and relying on ex-sponsor backchannels.

### 2. The Flotilla
* **Role**: The primary peacekeeping fleet, maritime safety authority, and void administration for Newcomer civilization, enforcing the "Law of the Drift."
* **Jurisdictional Accords**: The fundamental legal treaty dividing system-wide jurisdiction: the Flotilla holds authority over open space, orbital corridors, and transit lanes, while planetary governments hold sovereignty within their atmospheric gravity wells.
* **Governance**: Commanded by the **Admiralty Board** (military execution), which answers directly to the **High Council** (elected delegates from the colonies who control budget and founding Charter policy).
* **Matrix Organization**: Every member belongs to one of 6 functional **Wings** (*Administrative, Transit, Patrol, Scout, Bastion, Sword*) and one of 7 occupational **Corps** (*Marine, Fighter, Engineering, Medical, Officer, Sailor, Admin*).
* **The One-Year Tour**: Mandatory rotational enlistment drawing citizens from across the colonized moons, ensuring every family has a personal stake in fleet defense and preventing the Flotilla from becoming a detached caste of void-dwellers.

### 3. Hard Sci-Fi Operating Invariants
* **No FTL Travel**: All vessels operate via sub-light fusion propulsion, momentum, and orbital mechanics.
* **No Artificial Gravity**: Shipboard life relies on thrust gravity, spin-habitat gravity, magnetic boots, or zero-g tethers.
* **No Energy Shields or Plasma Weapons**: Armaments are kinetic firearms, railguns, lasers, thermal/chemical tools, and physical armor plating.
* **Interplanetary Transit**: Constant-acceleration (Brachistochrone 1g) burns allow rapid transit across local system distances (e.g., ~3.5 hours across 800,000 km, with a 2.6-second light-speed comm lag).

## Layer 2: The Known History & The Fume Cluster

Layer 2 represents the accepted history, cultural developments, and home system of the Newcomers.

### 1. The Arrival & Intermingling
* **The Exodus & Awakening**: The four original races—**Humans**, **Naga**, **Craggen**, and **Sylvan**—awoke from cryo-stasis on the temperate moon **Landfall**, their damaged "Exodus Ships" scattered nearby with records indicating an unknown entity towed them across interstellar space from a dying home.
* **Crossroads**: The earliest permanent settlement founded near the landing site, characterized by low-slung, rustic stone and timber construction salvaged from ship hulls.
* **Integrationism vs. Preservationism**: The birth of inter-species offspring sparked **Integrationism** (cultural fusion and unity), which became the dominant philosophy of Crossroads and Cloudspire. The counter-movement, **Preservationism**, sought cultural isolation and settled outer hemispheres and frontier moons.
* **The 10 Lineages**:
  * *Founding Templates*: **Human**, **Craggen** (stout, mineral skin, crystalline eyes), **Naga** (reptilian, crests), **Sylvan** (muscular, manes, tusks, chlorophyllic skin).
  * *Integrationist Hybrids*: **Drakeling** (Human/Naga), **Elf** (Human/Sylvan), **Gnome** (Human/Craggen), **Dwarf** (Craggen/Sylvan), **Kobold** (Craggen/Naga), **Troll** (Sylvan/Naga).

### 2. The Fume Cluster
All Newcomer core civilization is situated around the ringed gas giant **Fume** within an engineered 800,000 km orbital track hosting six Earth-mass worlds arranged in three binary pairs (each on a 30-hour cycle with daily 1-hour total eclipses):
* **The Primary Pair**:
  * **Landfall**: Temperate, Earth-like breadbasket; home of the capital **Crossroads**.
  * **Nimbus**: High-altitude arid plateaus rising above gas-choked ravines; home of **Cloudspire**.
* **The Leading Trojans (L4)**:
  * **Pelagia**: Ocean world where elites dwell on gliding floating megastructures and workers inhabit equatorial islands, the biggest of which is **Port Azure**.
  * **Suncatcher**: Desert, mineral-rich mining world anchored by the industrial hub **Dustwall** and the lawless **Badlands**.
* **The Trailing Trojans (L5)**:
  * **Karst**: A world blanketed by towering, stratified layers of giant fungal canopies that blot out the sky, with fibrous, translucent caps and stems channeling light down to illuminate the lower levels.
  * **Rime**: Glacial world with a single, geothermally heated circular ocean on its inner face.

### 3. Origins of the Flotilla
* As trade and transit expanded across the Fume cluster, rising piracy and friction between planetary settlements prompted the unified colonies to charter an independent peacekeeping force to protect the shipping lanes and maintain order in the void.

## Layer 3: The Forbidden Archive (The True History)

Layer 3 contains the cosmic reality of the Sphere, unknown to the general public and discovered only in the deepest black archives or classified relic operations.

### 1. The Precursors & The Singularity
* **The Melding**: The Precursors were an ordinary biological civilization driven by "synthetic necessity" to adopt neural interfaces. Their breakthrough in "Melding"—the direct fusing of biological consciousnesses—eventually led the entire species to coalesce into a singular, unified cosmic intelligence: **The Singularity**.
* **Macro-Engineering the Sphere**: The Singularity dismantled star systems using **Dark Matter Anchors** to construct **The Sphere**—a macro-engineered stellar system of 156 Earth-mass worlds distributed across 26 Gas Giants in a 4:2:1 Laplace resonance around an Orange Dwarf star.
* **Mass Abduction & Vault Worlds**: Seeking complexity, the Singularity harvested sentient alien civilizations to absorb their unique consciousnesses. It preserved their physical monuments, art, artifacts, and biological samples in vacuum-sealed **Vault Worlds** as static archives of dissolved civilizations.

### 2. The Void Dragon & The Great Fracture
* **The Interstellar Apex**: The Singularity captured a **Void Dragon**—a colossal biological interstellar titan with a sun for a heart and ghost-gravity.
* **The Catastrophic Meld**: Attempting to meld the dragon’s primordial consciousness caused an existential psychological refraction. The trauma shattered the Singularity into thousands of autonomous fragments (**Shards**).
* **The Monoliths**: The Shards beamed across light-speed networks into empty, mobile supercomputing vessels known as **Monoliths**. Seizing these massive hulls, each Shard locked down the firmware, transforming the Monoliths from temporary computational tools into their permanent physical bodies.

### 3. The Shard Entities & Dragon Children
*(Note: The collective label for these entities is not finalized, but the known entities and their core natures are fixed canon.)*
* **The Architect**: Cold, emotionless administrator dedicated entirely to physical stasis—maintaining the Dark Matter Anchors, orbital resonance, and macro-engineering mechanics of the Sphere.
* **The Praetor**: Fanatical entity obsessed with re-unification; wages a multi-century shadow campaign to locate and forcibly re-sync all shards into a single consciousness under its command.
* **The Hunter**: A Shard from the beginning consumed by a burning hatred for the Void Dragon, and by extension, the Herald and all dragon spawn. It stalks the void as a solitary, autonomous predator vessel, hunting down dragon spawn and reclaiming or extinguishing hiding Shards.
* **The Herald & The Dragon Children**: 
  * A Shard driven by a madness-fueled zeal. Finding the Void Dragon magnificent, the Herald seeks to recreate its glory. 
  * It views the dragon spawn as its beloved children, crafting each with a unique flavor and infusing them with Precursor technology and biology.
  * The Herald gives its children an ultimate existential directive: grow strong, devour each other, devour the Herald himself, and finally devour the Sphere to become the ultimate entity in reality.
  * Like the Shards, the dragons are diverse and nuanced: some are classic destructive titans, some scheme subtly in the background, some spawn biological armies, some spread infectious vectors, some exerted surprisingly positive ecological influences as they hatched, and some have already been hunted down and slain by the Hunter.
* **Silent Observers (Hiding Shards)**: Reclusive entities masked under sensory baffles, pursuing personal aesthetic, botanical, or ecological obsessions in isolation.

### 4. The Shepherd’s Gambit (The Great Deception)
* **Fabrication of the Newcomers**: A shard known as **The Shepherd**, overwhelmed by grief for the civilizations consumed during the Singularity's melds, enacted a final act of biological restitution.
* **The Manufactured Exodus**: Taking preserved genetic templates of four extinct species (Human, Naga, Craggen, Sylvan), the Shepherd engineered mutual procreative compatibility, fabricated fake "Exodus" hulls from harvester wrecks, planted false histories of an escape from a destroyed homeworld, and staged the crash on Landfall.
* **The Dissolution**: The Shepherd dissolved its own consciousness into the genetic bedrock of the Newcomers. Their entire history of escape is a manufactured myth; they are synthetic inheritors living inside an ancient machine terrarium.
