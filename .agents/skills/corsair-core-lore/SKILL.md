# Corsair Core Lore Reference

This document serves as the immutable, authoritative reference for the core setting of *Corsair*. When writing rulebook chapters, narrative fiction, equipment lore, or campaign materials, all descriptions must align with the three layers outlined below.

## Layer 1: The Present Day (518 AA — The New Wave)

Layer 1 is the everyday reality experienced by citizens, spacers, and operatives across Newcomer civilization in **518 AA** (exactly one decade after the Fall of the Evergaol).

### 1. The Corsairs & The New Wave
* **Mandate & Function**: Corsairs are autonomous specialist cells (typically 3–5 operatives on a single dedicated vessel) granted a cross-jurisdictional mandate to operate across both planetary gravity wells and orbital space.
* **The New Wave (518 AA)**: Following the devastating Frontier War with the Orkish Empire and the apocalyptic crash of the *Evergaol* in 508 AA, the Flotilla’s capital fleet was decimated and hundreds of veteran Corsair crews were lost. In 518 AA, the Admiralty Board and colonial sponsors have issued emergency charters to recruit a **New Wave of Corsairs**—scrappy, hungry crews flying refitted gunboats and surplus hulls to maintain order in a fraying frontier.
* **The Sponsor System**: Every legitimate Corsair is backed by a Sponsor (Flotilla, Colonial administration, Megacorporation, Civilian union/NGO, or Private patron). To ensure operational neutrality, chartered cells feature mixed sponsorships so no single faction controls the cell's agenda.
* **The Oversight Committee**: A governing board of exemplary former Corsairs and approved civilian delegates who review contract petitions for the common good, investigate agent misconduct, and hold the power to strip mandates.
* **Modes of Play**:
  * **Freelancers (Aspirants)**: Uncommissioned crews operating on foot, relying on commercial transport, taking low-level contracts to earn sponsor notice and purchase a hull.
  * **Corsairs (Chartered Cell)**: Standard commissioned cells with individual sponsors, a dedicated starship, and cross-jurisdictional legal authority.
  * **Rogue Corsairs (Outlaws)**: Former Corsairs who lost their charter, broke neutrality, or went rogue; operating un-flagged ships in black markets and relying on ex-sponsor backchannels.
* **MANTLE Chassis & The Freelancer Network**: MANTLE rigs (*Modular Adaptive Neural Transmission Linked Equipment*) are classified as Tier-1 Restricted Military Hardware, legally manufactured and controlled by the Flotilla for specialist strike corps. While commissioned Corsairs receive officially sanctioned frames underwritten by their sponsors, Freelancers acquire their required starting MANTLE either through competitive **Sponsor Candidate Pipelines** (training programs pending formal charter confirmation) or via **The Freelancer Network**—an underground fraternity of retired Corsair veterans, mentors, and dockside armorers who distribute repaired, surplused, or salvaged frames to promising aspirants.

### 2. The Flotilla
* **Role**: The primary peacekeeping fleet, maritime safety authority, and void administration for Newcomer civilization, enforcing the "Law of the Drift."
* **The Skyline Accords (335 AA)**: The fundamental constitutional treaty: the Flotilla holds supreme authority over open space, transit corridors, and orbital lanes, while planetary governments hold sovereignty within their atmospheric gravity wells.
* **Current Posture (518 AA)**: The Flotilla is stretched razor-thin. While it maintains naval hubs in Fume, a consulate in X'ara, and frontier taskforces in Wanabo, its veteran dreadnoughts are scarred or decommissioned, making the fleet heavily reliant on Corsair cells for reconnaissance and crisis response.
* **Governance**: Commanded by the **Admiralty Board** (military execution), which answers directly to the **High Council** (elected delegates from the colonies who control budget and founding Charter policy).
* **Matrix Organization**: Every member belongs to one of 6 functional **Wings** (*Administrative, Transit, Patrol, Scout, Bastion, Sword*) and one of 7 occupational **Corps** (*Marine, Fighter, Engineering, Medical, Officer, Sailor, Admin*).
* **The One-Year Tour**: Mandatory rotational enlistment drawing citizens from across the colonized moons, ensuring every family has a personal stake in fleet defense and preventing the Flotilla from becoming a detached caste of void-dwellers.

### 3. Hard Sci-Fi Operating Invariants
* **No FTL Travel**: All vessels operate via sub-light fusion propulsion, momentum, and orbital mechanics.
* **Brachistochrone Transit Realities**: At 1g continuous thrust, transit between co-orbital Trojans (800,000 km) takes **3.5 hours** (2.6-second comm lag). Transit between neighboring gas giants (90 million km, e.g. Fume to X'ara) takes only **2.2 days** (5.1-minute comm lag). The clusters form a tightly connected interstellar corridor.
* **No Artificial Gravity**: Shipboard life relies on thrust gravity, spin-habitat gravity, magnetic boots, or zero-g tethers.
* **No Energy Shields or Plasma Weapons**: Armaments are kinetic railguns, coilguns, autocannons, chemical explosives, lasers, and physical armor plating.

## Layer 2: The Known History & The Sphere Corridors (0 – 518 AA)

### 1. The Genesis & The Four Arks (0 AA – 60 AA)
* **The Four Arks & Touchdown (-4 BA)**: Four separate seed-hulls (Human, Naga, Craggen, Sylvan) gestated Cohort Zero in isolated crèches, touching down in Crossroads Valley on Landfall to finish maturation.
* **The Clandestine Breakouts (-3 to -1 BA)**: Restless teenage troublemakers bypassed autotutors, discovered the other arks in the valley, and formed cross-lineage bonds under the secret protection of **Goldheart** (an adolescent Void Dragon turned by the Shepherd to benevolence).
* **0 AA (Graduation Day)**: The cohorts formally matured and bulkheads opened. Cohort Zero emerged already bound by three years of secret friendships, slang, and romances, founding Crossroads (1 AA).
* **Crossroads (1 AA)**: Built with an intentionally low-slung, rustic footprint of stone and timber salvaged from Arks, designed to keep a low profile against orbital sensors.
* **Stormdrinker & The Covenant (14 AA)**: Stormdrinker, the Scholar Dragon of Nimbus, formed an alliance with the Newcomers to explore Precursor Vaults. Together with Goldheart and the Council, they discovered the truth of the Sphere and founded **The Covenant** to contain forbidden knowledge and prevent cosmic beacons from firing.
* **The Draco Fusion Drive (35–39 AA)**: Stormdrinker shared bio-fusion principles with the SRC $\rightarrow$ first interplanetary flight of the *Birdy*. Cloudspire founded in 45 AA beneath his perch.

### 2. The 10 Lineages & Ideological Divide
* **Founding Templates**: **Human**, **Craggen** (stout, mineral skin, crystalline eyes), **Naga** (reptilian, crests), **Sylvan** (muscular, manes, tusks, chlorophyllic skin).
* **Integrationist Hybrids**: **Drakeling** (Human/Naga), **Elf** (Human/Sylvan), **Gnome** (Human/Craggen), **Dwarf** (Craggen/Sylvan), **Kobold** (Craggen/Naga), **Troll** (Sylvan/Naga).
* **Integrationism vs. Preservationism**: Integrationism embraces cultural and genetic blending at Crossroads and Cloudspire. Preservationism is driven by filial reverence—the vow to keep ancestral bloodlines and cultures pure to honor dead homeworlds.

### 3. The Trojan Worlds & Local Dragons (120 – 200 AA)
* **Pelagia & Suncatcher**: Colonized in L4. Ballistic water-ice railguns linked them across the void.
* **Sandwyrm & Leviathan (175 AA)**: Mining awakened the voracious Sandwyrm on Suncatcher. When it flew to Pelagia, the **Leviathan**—a deep-sea schemer secretly puppet-mastering Pelagia’s corporate oligarchs—drowned it in the abyss to protect its shadow empire. The destroyed railguns caused the **Inter-Binary Famine**.

### 4. Corporate Feuds & Scavenger Wars (200 – 340 AA)
* **The Drift Wars**: Bitter He-3 proxy wars over Fume's rings shattered early joint peacekeeping.
* **The Scavenger Incursions & Genesis of MANTLEs (315–320 AA)**: Raider armadas of **Scavs** (bio-engineered children of The Collector) invaded Fume and sacked Cloudspire. Facing biologically augmented raiders in hand-to-hand boarding actions, Commander **Velen Redmane** commissioned Fleet-Engineer **Kira Abe** at the **Port Zenith Orbital Engineering Yards** to reverse-engineer inert Precursor Vault neural conduits. This created the first closed-loop **MANTLE** (*Modular Adaptive Neural Transmission Linked Equipment*) prototypes, giving strike teams the reflex-speed telemetry needed to win the **Battle of Fume Rings** (320 AA) and birthing the Flotilla and Skyline Accords (335 AA). MANTLE architecture continuously evolved over subsequent centuries, incorporating dragon biology research, new tech from Precursor vaults, and continuous iterative development.
* **The L5 Boundary (350–380 AA)**: The primitive **Lithari** discovered on fungal moon Karst $\rightarrow$ **Non-Interference Protocol** enacted. Expansion redirected to glacial **Rime**.

### 5. Multi-System Expansion & Foreign Empires (400 – 518 AA)
* **X’ara & The Ancestrals (425 AA)**: Contact with pre-Meld Precursors ruled by **The Elder** (a Shard in an avatar body enforcing the "Sacred Vigil"). Binary pair **Avon** is split: Avon 1 (Ancestral, home to the rebellious Gilded Youth and militant Pure Blade hawks) and Avon 2 (Newcomer festival hub).
* **Wanabo & The Orkish Empire (450–475 AA)**: Expansion into the buffer system Wanabo met the **Orkish Empire**—the brutal, might-makes-right sole survivors of Garden Node B.
* **The Frontier War & The Fall of the Evergaol (485–508 AA)**: War between the Flotilla and Orks was interrupted in 508 AA when the Praetor's automated Monolith dreadnought, the ***Evergaol***, attacked both fleets. Flotilla warships, Ork heavy rams, Goldheart, and Stormdrinker united to drag the burning titan down into Landfall's crust (**Evergaol Crash Site**).
* **The Truce of Iron and Ash (509 AA)**: Signed ten years ago; both empires are heavily scarred, setting the stage for the New Wave in **518 AA**.

## Layer 3: The Forbidden Archive (The Cosmic Truth)

### 1. The Precursors & The Singularity
* **The Melding**: Biological civilization adopted neural interfaces, eventually fusing into a unified cosmic intelligence: **The Singularity**.
* **The Sphere**: Engineered stellar system of more than a hundred Earth-mass worlds across 26 Gas Giants in a 4:2:1 Laplace resonance around an Orange Dwarf.
* **Harvesting Civilizations**: The Singularity harvested and melded alien species (Humans, Naga, Craggen, Sylvan, Orks, etc.) into its god-mind, storing biological samples in Vault Worlds.

### 2. The Great Fracture & The Shards
* **The Void Dragon**: A cosmic biological interstellar titan shattered the Singularity during an attempted Meld, splitting it into thousands of autonomous **Shards** that seized mobile supercomputing vessels (**Monoliths**).
* **The Known Shard Pantheon**:
  * **The Praetor**: Seeks total, forced re-synchronization of all Shards. Commands an arsenal of Monolith dreadnoughts (including the *Evergaol*).
  * **The Hunter**: Autonomous predator vessel obsessed with exterminating dragons and reclaiming Shards.
  * **The Elder**: Nostalgist Shard embedded on Irul, masquerading as Archon over pre-Meld Ancestrals under the "Sacred Vigil."
  * **The Collector**: Lesser Shard of Greed whose cloned **Scav** clans compete for his favor by plundering Precursor relics.
  * **The Sculptor & The Menagerie Keeper**: Reclusive aesthetic Shards residing on Oran 1 and Oran 2.
  * **The Herald & The Dragon Children**: The Herald bred dragons with the directive to "grow strong and devour." The dragons adapted their hunger in unique ways (Goldheart devours conflict; Stormdrinker devours knowledge; Leviathan devours political agency; Sandwyrm devoured raw matter).

### 3. The Shepherd’s Gambit & Fate
* **Garden Node 01 (Landfall)**: The Shepherd shed the melded essences of Humanity, Naga, Sylvan, and Craggen into Cohort Zero, bestowing a Divine Spark, and entrusted them to Goldheart before departing.
* **The Tragedy of Garden Node B**: The Shepherd attempted a second resurrection, but was ambushed and captured by **The Hunter**. Abandoned mid-genesis, the resurrected species tore each other apart until only the **Orks** survived, forging their autocratic empire.
