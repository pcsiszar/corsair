# Style Guide

Always keep in mind that the setting follows hard science fiction rules. No artificial gravity, no FTL, no space magic.

The rules phrasing should be direct. When writing lore, the style should emulate Terry Pratchett.

When writing paragraphs for lore sections, do not over-fragment it. Prefer longer sections.

The theme of the setting should be a mix of Expanse, Mass Effect, Guardians of the Galaxy and Cowboy Bebop.

When coming up with new lore content, ALWAYS go into /grill-me mode, meaning ask questions from the user to align.
