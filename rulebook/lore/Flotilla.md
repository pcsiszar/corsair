# The Flotilla: Guardians of the Void

While the two hundred worlds of **the Sphere** provide the soil and air for life, the **Flotilla** provides the thread
that binds them. A massive, space-bound organization, the Flotilla is an entity in the system that claims no
territory and answers to no single planet.

## The Mandate of the Stars

The Flotilla operates under a strict non-interference treaty known as the **Cloud Line**. Their authority is
absolute in the vacuum, but it vanishes the moment they breach a planet’s gravity well.

* **Interstellar Security:** The Flotilla is the primary defense against "Outside Threats".
* **The Freedom of the Sphere:** They ensure that the trade lanes between the colonized planets remain open and free.
  They do not collect taxes, but they do enforce "Safety Protocols" and "Navigational Standards."
* **Neutral Ground:** Flotilla stations and massive Hive-Ships serve as the system’s primary meeting grounds, where
  representatives from all worlds can gather without fear of local planetary laws.

## The Service Structure

The Flotilla is not a hereditary empire or a traditional military; it is a volunteer-driven meritocracy that draws from
the entire  population.

* **The Tour of Duty:** The vast majority of the Flotilla is composed of citizens from the various planets who sign on
  for **One-Year Tours**. This "revolving door" ensures that the Flotilla remains deeply connected to the people it
  protects.
* **The Career Track:** Higher-level positions—Commanders, Magistrates, Admirals—are reserved for those
  who have completed multiple tours and demonstrated a commitment to the Flotilla’s neutral ideals.
* **Codependency:** The Flotilla relies on the planets for food, raw materials, and fresh recruits. In exchange, the
  planets rely on the Flotilla for protection, satellite maintenance, and the orbital arrays that keep the engineered
  climates stable.
