### The Unredacted Chronicle: The Cosmic History of the Sphere

*Note: This classified document preserves the true, unredacted historical record of the Sphere. It cross-references the public timeline of Newcomer civilization with the classified revelations of the Precursor Singularity, the Shard Pantheon, the Dragon Children, and the Shepherd’s Gambit.*

---

### Era I: The Era of Genesis & The Primary Pair (0 AA – 60 AA)

#### ? BA — The Staged Exodus

According to curated ship databanks, the four great ancestral lineages—Humanity, Naga, Craggen, and Sylvan—launched their colossal automated seed arks to escape the imminent death of their home systems. In truth, this exodus was an engineered act of biological restitution by the **Shepherd**, an empathetic Fragment of the fractured Precursor Singularity. Having un-melded these four templates and endowed them with a synthetic **Divine Spark**, the Shepherd housed their genetic code within four separate automated seed arks, programming them with genuine cultural, philosophical, and scientific libraries alongside a manufactured tragedy of planetary loss.

#### -4 BA — Silent Touchdown in Crossroads Valley

Under sub-light automated navigation, the four arks enter the atmosphere of the temperate moon **Landfall** and touch down silently within a single verdant, sheltered valley. While the hulls rest on alien soil, the ships' automated nurseries remain sealed; the onboard autotutors are programmed to keep their adolescent charges sequestered within simulated biodomes until they reach full biological and social maturity at age twenty.

#### -3 to -1 BA — The Clandestine Breakouts

Confinement proves impossible against restless, inquisitive youth. Bored by looping historical lectures, synthetic nutrition paste, and sterile nursery corridors, teenage troublemakers (aged 14–16) begin prying open ventilation shafts, overriding maintenance bulkheads, and slipping out into the misty valley under the cover of night.

In the untamed alien ferns of the valley floor, runaway humans, nagas, craggens, and sylvans collide. Although their databanks had never disclosed the existence of the other three hulls resting mere kilometers away, they discover to their shock that they all speak the same engineered Common Tongue. 

Waiting for them in the valley is **Goldheart**, an adolescent Void Dragon turned by the Shepherd to benevolence. Starved for companionship himself, Goldheart does not report the runaways or herd them back into their cradles. Instead, he becomes their secret guardian, protector, and older brother. He shields the teenagers from Landfall's apex predators, teaches them to avoid dangerous sensor hazards, and conceals their illicit excursions from the arks' external scanners. Around clandestine campfires beneath the dragon's folded wings, the teenagers trade smuggled rations, share homeworld myths, and forge a fierce, cross-species camaraderie that transcends the sterile boundaries of their individual ships.

#### 0 AA — Graduation Day

The automated gestation and education cycles reach scheduled completion. Ship sirens wail, floodlights activate, and the primary nursery airlocks swing wide to formally release **Cohort Zero**—an initial generation of young adults (aged 18–20) meant to step onto an alien moon for the first time. 

The ship autotutors anticipate bewildered, solemn survivors clutching ancestral archives. Instead, the breakout veterans burst back into their respective arks, laughing and dragging their wide-eyed, obedient peers out into the sunlight. Rather than four frightened alien factions encountering each other in defensive isolation, the cohorts step into a valley where shared jokes, deep friendships, rivalries, and first loves have already been quietly flourishing for three years. Goldheart greets the assembled multitudes, playfully reinforcing the myth that he pulled their drifting ships from the void and watched over them.

#### 1 AA — Foundation of Crossroads

Cohort Zero begins building their first permanent settlement in the valley where the four arks landed. Naming it **Crossroads**, they construct low-slung, rustic dwellings from local stone, timber, and salvaged ark hulls. Influenced by Goldheart's gentle warnings to avoid high-altitude electromagnetic emissions that might draw predatory eyes from the void, the settlement establishes an intentionally horizontal footprint that eschews massive vertical spires. The four lineages form a provisional council, electing one representative from each ark.

#### 6 AA — Birth of Integrationism

The birth of the first cross-lineage child sparks a profound cultural transformation. Natural romances, having taken root during the secret midnight gatherings of the breakout years, blossom openly across Crossroads. This gives rise to **Integrationism**—a socio-political movement advocating total cultural synthesis, mutual adaptation, and shared destiny as the moral core of the Newcomer civilization.

#### 14 AA — The First Vault & The Covenant

Expeditions venturing into Landfall’s crags discover the First Precursor Vault—a humming subterranean complex of crystalline lattices. The anomaly attracts **Stormdrinker**, a massive dragon roosting in the violent electrical storms of Landfall's binary twin, **Nimbus**. Driven by an insatiable instinct to devour cosmic secrets and knowledge, Stormdrinker descends upon the site. 

Goldheart steps in to mediate, forging an unprecedented alliance between mortals and dragons: the Newcomers provide the nimble hands and fine tools to navigate human-sized maintenance conduits, while Stormdrinker and Goldheart provide the sensory depth and brute energy to crack heavy exterior bulkheads. 

Inside the Vault, the expedition leaders discover the true macro-engineered nature of the Sphere and the terrifying reality of the Singularity. Recognizing that public knowledge of this cosmic reality would cause civilizational despair—and that activating ancient broadcast relays could draw predatory Shards—the expedition leaders, council elders, Goldheart, and Stormdrinker swear an eternal oath. They form **The Covenant**: an ultra-classified circle dedicated to containing forbidden knowledge, sanitizing Vaults, and protecting the young civilization.

#### 15 AA — The Preservationist Counter-Movement

As Integrationism sweeps the urban heart of Crossroads, an ideological counter-current emerges. Led primarily by the "dutiful students" of Cohort Zero—those who had remained inside the arks until Graduation Day, absorbing the unadulterated poetry, tragedy, and philosophies of Earth, the Naga swarms, the Craggen deep-hives, and the Sylvan groves—these traditionalists view the freewheeling hybrid culture as an irreverent betrayal of their sacred ancestral heritage. Adopting the philosophy of **Preservationism**, they begin migrating toward Landfall's outward-facing hemisphere to establish insular, single-lineage settlements dedicated to keeping their bloodlines and traditions pure.

#### 35 AA — The Nimbus Rendezvous

Guided by data shared through The Covenant, researchers at the Sphere Research Center (SRC) construct Landfall’s first deep-space manned rocket. Commanded by a daring multi-species crew, the vessel braves the turbulent electrical storm-wells of Nimbus to rendezvous with Stormdrinker at his high-altitude roost. In exchange for historical texts, mathematical proofs, and cultural data, Stormdrinker allows SRC physicists to study his unique bio-fusion biology and gravitic flight mechanics.

#### 38 AA — The Draco Fusion Breakthrough

Synthesizing telemetry from Stormdrinker’s biological dynamo with Precursor magnetic shielding principles, the SRC develops the **Draco Fusion Drive**. Operating on continuous-thrust fusion physics, the drive makes rapid sub-light interplanetary transit safe, reliable, and commercially viable.

#### 39 AA — Maiden Flight of the *Birdy*

The transport vessel *Birdy*, equipped with the prototype Draco Drive, successfully crosses the 61,000-kilometer binary gap between Landfall and Nimbus in under two hours, formally opening the space age for Newcomer civilization.

#### 45 AA — The Rise of Cloudspire

A permanent research outpost on the high-altitude arid plateaus of Nimbus rapidly expands into a metropolis of gleaming towers, neon arrays, and lightning catchers: **Cloudspire**. Stormdrinker becomes the public patron of the city, revered by citizens as the "Lightning Sage," circling the upper atmosphere above the clouds while maintaining quiet council with Covenant leaders in restricted subterranean bays.

### Era II: The Trojan Expansion & The Great Beasts (60 AA – 200 AA)

#### 62 AA — Waypoint Station at L1

Civil engineering reaches a monumental milestone with the deployment of **Waypoint Station** at the gravitational L1 null-point, precisely 30,500 kilometers between Landfall and Nimbus. Waypoint serves as the primary customs terminal, logistics clearinghouse, and data relay coordinating transit across the primary pair.

#### 80–110 AA — The Binary Century

Crossroads and Cloudspire mature into dense, industrial city-states. The facing hemispheres of both moons become the brilliant, hyper-connected heart of Integrationist civilization. Meanwhile, corporate charters and Preservationist settlements dominate the outward hemispheres, funding private prospecting into the wider Fume system.

#### 120 AA — The Rush to the L4 Trojans

With the primary pair heavily colonized, ambitious mining conglomerates and independent pioneers look outward to Fume's co-orbital L4 Lagrange point, 800,000 kilometers ahead. Colony fleets launch toward the oceanic water-world of **Pelagia** and the hyper-arid, mineral-rich desert moon of **Suncatcher**.

#### 125 AA — The Ballistic Logistics Corridors

Because neither Trojan world is self-sustaining alone, an audacious engineering marvel is established: massive planetary railguns are constructed on the equatorial island chains of Pelagia to launch sealed water-ice canisters across the 61,000-kilometer gap directly into Suncatcher’s catchment basins. In return, Suncatcher fires heavy ores and refined structural metals back to Pelagia.

#### 130–150 AA — Rise of the Trojan Megacities

* **Port Azure** on Pelagia evolves from a humble aquatic landing pad into a sprawling network of floating pontoon arcologies, dominated by shipping dynasties and maritime fishing syndicates.
* **Dustwall** on Suncatcher becomes the iron-bound capital of heavy industry and smelting, surrounded by the untamed, canyon-cut expanse known as the **Badlands**.

#### 165 AA — Awakening of the Sandwyrm

Heavy deep-core tectonic fracking in Suncatcher's Badlands awakens a gargantuan subterranean horror: the **Sandwyrm**. A colossal draconic predator driven by the Herald’s primal imperative to consume matter, the beast begins swallowing mining crawlers, drilling platforms, and frontier rail lines whole, turning the Badlands into a lethal wasteland.

#### 170 AA — The Shadow of the Abyss

Deep-sea acoustic hydrophones deployed along Pelagia's oceanic trenches detect an ultra-massive biological entity slumbering in the abyss: the **Leviathan**. Unknown to the populace, the Leviathan is a calculating Dragon Child that devours through psychological manipulation and covert influence, subtly whispering into the neural networks and biometric telemetry of Pelagia's corporate oligarchs.

#### 175 AA — The Clash of the Titans & The Inter-Binary Famine

Desperate to eliminate the Sandwyrm, Suncatcher authorities re-aim Pelagia’s planetary water railguns to execute kinetic orbital bombardments against the beast's nest. Enraged and grievously wounded, the Sandwyrm sprouts massive membranous wings, breaks atmosphere, and flies across the void toward Pelagia to annihilate the railgun complexes. 

As the airborne titan tears through Pelagia's coastal defense arrays, the **Leviathan** violently breaches the ocean surface. Refusing to let an uncouth sibling destroy its carefully nurtured puppet-empire, the abyssal dragon coils around the Sandwyrm and drags it thrashing into the black depths of the trench, drowning it. 

The collateral damage levels Pelagia’s launch arrays. The severed water logistics corridor plunges both worlds into catastrophic drought and industrial collapse: the **Inter-Binary Famine**.

#### 180 AA — The First Flotilla Charter

With famine ravaging the Trojans and piracy surging along the disrupted shipping routes, the Landfall Council and the newly formed Trade Alliance charter a joint emergency peacekeeper force: the **Flotilla**. Governed by a provisional Admiralty Board, the fleet mobilizes all available freighters to escort emergency food and water convoys across the system.

### Era III: The Corporate Feuds & The Scavenger Wars (200 AA – 340 AA)

#### 205 AA — Resolution of the Famine & The Deep-Sea Farms

Pelagia reconstructs its launch platforms while deploying bio-engineered kelp and aquaculture farms, permanently ending the famine. However, the political divide between the central governments of the primary pair and the corporate oligarchies of the Trojans becomes permanently entrenched.

#### 220–255 AA — The Drift Wars

Fierce competition over helium-3 fuel and heavy isotopes around Fume’s radiation-swept inner moons sparks the **Drift Wars**. Planetary governments refuse to fund the joint Flotilla, choosing instead to arm private corporate security navies. Proxy skirmishes, commerce raiding, and state-sponsored piracy turn the space lanes into a treacherous free-fire zone.

#### 270 AA — The Collector’s Gaze

Attracted by the electromagnetic clutter, radio noise, and wreckage of the Drift Wars, the **Scavs**—a bio-engineered nomadic warrior race bred by the lesser Shard **The Collector**—enter the Fume system. Competing for their patron deity's favor by plundering Precursor relics and high technology, dozens of Scav raider clans launch coordinated incursions against mining outposts and orbital platforms.

#### 285 AA — The Sack of Cloudspire

In the most brazen raid of the century, a massive Scav vanguard breaches Nimbus's atmosphere. Their objective: looting the SRC's deep Precursor relic vaults and harvesting the living bio-fusion wealth of Stormdrinker. 

Stormdrinker unleashes his fury across the sky, incinerating dozens of boarding craft with plasma lightning, while Cloudspire's ground batteries fight room-by-room in the arcologies. The defenders successfully repel the invaders, but the upper districts suffer catastrophic damage and several vital Vault relics are stolen.

#### 290–310 AA — The Fractured System & Redmane's Remnants

Terrified planetary governments withdraw their remaining warships into low-orbit defense grids, leaving open space completely unguarded. Piracy and Scav raids skyrocket. Refusing to abandon the shipping lanes, veteran Flotilla commander **Velen Redmane** gathers a loyal coalition of spacers, corporate defectors, and volunteer crews to operate as an independent, non-aligned defense fleet: **Redmane’s Coalition**.

#### 320 AA — The Battle of Fume Rings

A massive Scav armada gathers within Fume's inner ring system to launch a coordinated planetary assault against Landfall. Redmane’s Coalition intercepts the raiders in a brutal, multi-day fleet engagement among the icy ring debris. Utilizing tight Brachistochrone maneuvers and point-blank railgun volleys, Redmane decisively obliterates the Scav fleet, securing the void lanes and saving the colonies from collapse.

#### 335 AA — The Skyline Accords & Creation of the Corsairs

Hailed as saviors, Redmane's coalition forces the planetary governments to the negotiating table. The landmark **Skyline Accords** are signed:
* **The Flotilla** is permanently established as the sovereign, neutral naval and peacekeeper authority over all open space, orbital lanes, and transit hubs.
* **Planetary Governments** retain absolute legal sovereignty within their atmospheric gravity wells.
* **The Corsairs**: Recognizing that criminals, smugglers, and rogue corporate agents exploit the jurisdictional seam between planetary skies and open orbit, the Flotilla authorizes independent, multi-party sponsored privateer cells granted cross-jurisdictional mandates: the **Corsairs**.

### Era IV: The L5 Boundary & The Ancestral Encounter (340 AA – 450 AA)

#### 350 AA — Turn to the L5 Trojans

With Fume's primary and leading worlds stabilized, colonization fleets look to the trailing Lagrangian pair: the dense fungal world of **Karst** and the glacial ice-world of **Rime**.

#### 365 AA — Discovery of the Lithari

Deep subterranean survey teams on Karst discover vast bioluminescent cavern biomes inhabited by a primitive, tool-using sentient species: the tribal hunters known as the **Lithari**. 

#### 368 AA — The Non-Interference Protocol

Recognizing the moral weight of their own origin, the Council and the Flotilla ratify the **Non-Interference Protocol**. Karst is placed under permanent naval quarantine, enforced by a heavily armed Flotilla picket station at the L1 point to protect the Lithari until they achieve independent spaceflight. All colonial expansion is redirected to **Rime**, where subterranean cities are carved around its geothermally heated circular ocean.

#### 410 AA — The Inter-System Probes

Long-range autonomous survey drones burning at 1g cross the 90-million-kilometer void to the neighboring gas giant system, **X’ara**. They return startling telemetry: a second, engineered six-world binary track mirroring Fume's architecture, inhabited by a hyper-advanced, uniform civilization. Soon after, the worlds of Fume receive an enigmatic, formal transmission inviting them to open diplomatic talks.

#### 425 AA — First Contact with the Ancestrals

A high delegation of Newcomer diplomats aboard the Flotilla cruiser *Endeavour* arrives at X'ara, meeting the **Ancestrals**. The Ancestrals are un-melded, biological Precursors resurrected into an idealized golden age by **The Elder**—a nostalgist Shard living secretly among them in a synthetic avatar body as the "Archon." 

The Elder has enforced a strict theological dogma: the **Vigil of Reverent Stillness**, preaching that expansion is a sin of hubris. The sudden arrival of noisy, multi-species, fusion-powered Newcomers terrifies the Elder and shocks Ancestral society.

#### 432 AA — The Ancestral Treaty

To prevent conflict and quarantine Newcomer influence away from the sacred capital worlds of **Irul 1 & 2**, the Elder negotiates the **Ancestral Treaty**:
* **The Avon Partition**: The L4 Trojan pair of X'ara is divided. **Avon 1** remains under Ancestral control, while **Avon 2** is opened to Newcomer settlement, quickly evolving into a vibrant hub of art, trade, and cultural festivals.
* **The Avon 1 Schism**: Looking across the narrow 61,000-kilometer gap at the bustling, colorful night-skies of Avon 2, Ancestral society on Avon 1 fractures into the **Gilded Movement** (restless youth captivated by Newcomer vitality) and the **Pure Blade** (zealous military hawks who despise Newcomer vulgarity).
* **The Oran Quarantine**: The sacred L5 worlds of **Oran 1 & 2** (containing the singing stone monuments of the Sculptor and the great beasts of the Menagerie Keeper) are permanently closed to industrial colonization.
* **The Flotilla Consulate**: A single, fortified consulate on Irul 1 serves as the sole legal port of entry into core Ancestral space.

### Era V: The Frontier War, The Evergaol, & The Present Day (450 AA – 518 AA)

#### 455 AA — Expansion into Wanabo

Pushing outward along the Middle Ring of the Sphere, Newcomer prospectors and corporate pioneers reach the third gas giant: **Wanabo**. The moon of **Kazahn** becomes an unregulated frontier of subterranean boomtowns, black markets, and outlaw claims, while neighboring **Karak** remains a perilous wilderness.

#### 465 AA — First Contact with the Orkish Empire

In the void beyond Wanabo, Newcomer explorers encounter the battle-cruisers of the **Orkish Empire**. The Orks are the sole surviving lineage of **Garden Node B**—the Shepherd's tragic second resurrection attempt, which was aborted when the **Hunter** ambushed and captured the Shepherd. Left without guidance, the resurrected species tore each other apart in an apocalyptic survival war until only the iron-willed, brutal Orks remained.

#### 485–505 AA — The Great Frontier War

Tensions over Wanabo’s rich helium-3 fields boil over into a grinding, 20-year interstellar war. The Flotilla's disciplined kinetic doctrine clashes with the massive, armored ram-prows and overwhelming assault doctrine of the Orkish Warlords. Decisive battles across the trenches of Kazahn and the orbital corridors of Wanabo grind both militaries down to near-exhaustion.

#### 508 AA — The Evergaol Incursion & The Great Last Stand

As the battered fleets of the Flotilla and the Orkish Empire clash in the outer reaches of the Fume system, a nightmare from Layer 3 awakens: the ***Evergaol***, a colossal automated Monolith dreadnought from the **Praetor’s** armada, warps into the battlespace. Seeking to forcibly harvest biological minds for the Praetor's re-synchronization crusade, the dreadnought begins systematically obliterating and abducting warships from both sides.

Facing total annihilation, Flotilla battlecruisers and Orkish dreadnoughts forge an unthinkable, desperate battlefield alliance. In an earth-shattering clash across the upper atmosphere of Landfall, Flotilla railguns, Ork boarding torpedoes, **Goldheart**, and **Stormdrinker** unite. The two dragons grapple directly with the Monolith’s drive arrays, tearing open its heat manifolds and allowing combined kinetic salvos to break its orbital anchors. 

The burning *Evergaol* crashes violently into the surface of Landfall, gouging a continent-scale scar known as the **Evergaol Crash Site**. Boarding teams led by veteran Flotilla commanders sacrifice their lives inside the burning wreckage to disable the primary automated matrix.

#### 509 AA — The Great Truce

Horrified by the cosmic power of the Monolith and hollowed out by casualties, the Flotilla Admiralty and the Orkish Empire sign **the Great Truce**. The treaty establishes an uneasy, heavily militarized border across Wanabo, with both sides retreating to lick their wounds.

#### 518 AA — The Present Day: The New Wave of Corsairs

Exactly ten years have passed since the Fall of the Evergaol:
* **The Flotilla in Crisis**: The navy is stretched to the breaking point. Its capital fleet is a fraction of its pre-war strength, struggling to decommission scarred warships and maintain patrols across Fume, X'ara, and Wanabo.
* **The Evergaol Exclusion Zone**: The crash site on Landfall remains a smoldering, highly restricted wasteland of lethal automated Precursor defenses, picked at by illegal Scavs, corporate black-ops units, and daring relic hunters.
* **The Smoldering Frontier**: The Orkish Empire watches the border with growing impatience, while Ancestral hawks on Avon 1 push for a preemptive strike against the weakened Newcomers.
* **The New Wave**: Unable to police the void alone, the Flotilla and colonial sponsors have thrown open the doors to a **New Wave of Corsairs**. Fresh crews—flying refitted gunboats, surplus corvettes, and salvaged hulls—are stepping forward to take on the most dangerous contracts in the Sphere, standing as the thin line between civilization and total collapse.
