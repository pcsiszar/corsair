The gas giant **Fume** dominates the local heavens, a colossal tapestry of swirling amber and violet bands that serves
as the gravitational anchor for four distinct worlds. Each moon is a testament to the Shepherd’s diverse genetic
seeding, yet they are bound together by the thin silver threads of the Flotilla’s trade routes. At the heart of this web
sits **Redmane Station**, the massive, rotating hub of the void-born and the silent arbiter of the system’s peace.

---

### The Moons of Fume

The four habitable satellites of Fume represent a spectrum of biological and industrial evolution, each contributing
uniquely to the survival of the Sphere-born.

* **Landfall (The Ancestral Heart):** The site of the original "Colony Ship" landings and the city of **Crossroads**. It
  remains a world of rolling greens, temperate forests, and the most stable ecosystem in the system. While it is the
  cultural soul of the species, its influence has waned as its technology became "traditional" compared to the lunar
  frontiers.
* **Skyreach (The Industrial Engine):** A jagged, low-gravity moon once considered a barren rock. The discovery of
  Precursor vaults beneath its crust transformed it into a high-tech powerhouse of neon-lit hab-domes and
  electromagnetic refineries. It is the source of the system’s advanced alloys and graviton-shunts.
* **Raindrop (The Oceanic Cradle):** A world of endless, shimmering blue, where scattered island chains provide the only
  dry land. Its rich aquatic biome and specialized kelp-farms provide the majority of the system’s organic nutrients and
  medicinal compounds, though its volatile storms make it a challenge for the Patrol Wing to monitor.
* **Suncatcher (The Resource Frontier):** The outermost moon, defined by vast savannahs and sun-scorched deserts. It is
  the primary source of raw minerals and heavy elements. Life here is rugged and decentralized, populated by hard-bitten
  mining collectives who rely on the Flotilla for every liter of recycled water.

### Redmane Station: The Spire in the Dark

Positioned at the L1 Lagrange point between Fume and Landfall, **Redmane Station** is a gargantuan feat of engineering—a
sprawling complex of interconnected hulls, rotating rings, and docking spires that serves as the permanent headquarters
of the Flotilla.

* **The Hub of Neutrality:** Within the station’s pressurized corridors, the "Law of the Drift" is absolute. It is the
  only place in the system where a Landfall diplomat and a Skyreach CEO can meet on neutral ground, overseen by the *
  *Administrative Wing** and the **Flotilla High Court**.
* **The Matrix Center:** Redmane serves as the primary coordination node for all Wings and Corps. The **Sword Wing**
  maintains a permanent carrier presence in the "Lower Bays," while the **Scout Wing** utilizes the station’s massive
  sensor arrays to peer into the deep dark beyond the gas giant’s rings.
* **The Transit Nexus:** Every major cargo haul and passenger ferry in the system eventually passes through the "Central
  Ring." The station’s vast docking arms are constantly crowded with vessels from all four moons, creating a vibrant,
  multilingual bazaar where the "One-Year Tour" recruits mingle with seasoned void-sailors.

More than just a military installation, Redmane Station is the physical manifestation of Velen Redmane’s vision: a
shield that stands between the internal squabbles of the moons and the terrifying, unknown potential of the outer void.
