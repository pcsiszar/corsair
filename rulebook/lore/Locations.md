The gas giant **Fume** is the gravitational anchor for four habitable moons, characterized by its distinct amber and
violet atmospheric bands. These worlds are connected by primary Flotilla trade routes and overseen by 
**Redmane Station**, a large-scale orbital facility that serves as the system's administrative and logistical hub.

---

### The Moons of Fume

The four habitable satellites of Fume provide the majority of the system's resources and population centers.

* **Landfall:** The site of the original "Colony Ship" landings and the city of **Crossroads**. It maintains a stable,
  temperate ecosystem with extensive forests. As the primary cultural center for the Sphere-born, it focuses on
  agriculture and traditional industries.
* **Skyreach:** A high-gravity moon housing the system's primary industrial and technological facilities. Following the
  discovery of Precursor vaults, it was developed into a network of pressurized hab-domes and refineries. It is the
  leading producer of advanced alloys and graviton-shunts.
* **Raindrop:** An oceanic moon with limited dry land confined to scattered island chains. It hosts specialized
  kelp-farms that provide organic nutrients and medicinal compounds.
* **Suncatcher:** The outermost moon, consisting primarily of savannahs and deserts. It is the primary source of raw
  minerals and heavy elements. The moon is populated by decentralized mining collectives who rely on imported water
  supplies managed by the Flotilla.

---

### Redmane Station

Positioned at the L1 Lagrange point between Fume and Landfall, **Redmane Station** is the permanent headquarters of the
Flotilla. It consists of interconnected hulls and rotating rings designed for long-term orbital habitation.

* **Administrative Center:** The station serves as the neutral ground for interplanetary diplomacy and legal
  arbitration, overseen by the **Administrative Wing**.
* **Military Logistics:** Redmane is the primary coordination node for all Flotilla operations. The **Sword Wing**
  maintains a presence in the lower bays, while the **Scout Wing** operates the station's long-range sensor arrays.
* **Transit Nexus:** As the central hub of the system, all major cargo and passenger traffic passes through Redmane's
  docking rings, facilitating trade between the four moons.

Redmane Station serves as the primary defensive barrier between the moons and the outer regions of the Sphere.

---

### The Moons of X'ara

X'ara is a neighboring gas giant containing two binary moon pairs.

* **Avon'rea & Avon'roa:** A pair of binary moons that serve as a diplomatic and cultural interface between Spherite
  colonies and the Ancestrals. They are known for hosting large-scale artistic and musical competitions.
* **Irul'loa & Irul'lia:** Binary moons claimed by the Ancestrals. Access is strictly regulated by the Consulate on
  Irul and is generally closed to Spherite personnel.

---

### The Moons of Wanabo

Wanabo is a distant gas giant at the edge of the Flotilla's operational range.

* **Kazahn:** A moon classified as hazardous due to atmospheric instability. It is populated by independent mining
  operations and fringe groups operating outside Admiralty Board jurisdiction.
* **Karak:** Kazahn’s sister moon, currently under scientific quarantine due to aggressive genetically modified fauna.
  Rumors of Precursor vaults in the region continue to attract unsanctioned exploration.
