# The Fume Cluster: Orbital Dynamics and Planetary Topography

The Fume cluster is a hyper-dense, engineered orbital node within The Sphere. Centered on the Gas Giant "Fume," this
cluster hosts three distinct binary pairs—a total of six terrestrial worlds—within a single radiation-shielded orbital
track at 800,000 kilometers. Each pair is locked in a 30-hour cycle, resulting in a system defined by extreme tidal
stress, consistent eclipses, and profound atmospheric diversity.

---

## I. The Central Anchor: Fume

Fume is a massive, ringed Gas Giant characterized by a modified metallic hydrogen core that minimizes high-energy
radiation output. Its atmosphere is a turbulent expanse of deep amber and violet clouds, illuminated by the internal
heat of its formation and the reflected light from its twelve sibling Gas Giants. Fume’s magnetosphere is the vital
life-support mechanism for the six worlds in its local track, acting as a buffer against stellar wind while producing
spectacular, permanent aurorae that illuminate the night skies of its orbiting moons.

---

## II. The Binary Pairs

### 1. The Primary Pair (Barycenter): Landfall and Nimbus

Situated at the base of the orbital track, these two worlds provide the system’s baseline terrestrial conditions.

* **Landfall:** Landfall is the most Earth-like of the cluster. It features a balanced distribution of continents and
  oceans, with moderate plate tectonics driven by the tidal pull of its twin. The geography consists of temperate
  forests, mountain ranges, and large, stable basins. Its climate is the most temperate in the Fume cluster, with the
  eclipse serving as a mild seasonal shift.
  The planet’s geopolitical landscape is anchored by two critical locations. **Crossroads**, the oldest city established
  by the Spherites and the current capital, retains a distinct, historical character. It features low-slung stone and
  timber engineering salvaged from early colony ships, maintaining a rustic, spread-out layout that deliberately avoids
  supermassive vertical structures. In stark contrast lies the **Evergaol Crash Site**, a massive, scarred exclusion
  zone where the hull of the disabled Precursor invasion fortress sits permanently embedded into the crust, serving as a
  bleak monument to the invasion of 146 AA and a continuous site for dangerous salvage operations.
* **Nimbus:** This world’s geography is dominated by high-altitude plateaus separated by deep, vertical ravines. Because
  of the planet’s unique atmospheric density, heavy, inert gases collect in these low-lying ravines, creating "
  atmospheric lakes" that possess a liquid-like viscosity. The high-altitude plateaus are relatively thin-aired and
  arid, while the ravines are sheltered, pressurized micro-environments choked with heavy fog.
  Perched across the high plateaus is **Cloudspire**, a massive, hyper-dense metropolis that serves as the technological
  counter-weight to Crossroads. The city is a vertical labyrinth of towering arcologies, constantly washed in the glare
  of multi-colored plasma advertisements and artificial localized illumination networks to pierce the permanent
  atmospheric haze. Beneath this affluent, brightly lit surface layer lies a volatile criminal underworld; the dense,
  fog-ridden ravines below the city lines are completely unpoliced, serving as decentralized hideouts for heavily armed
  syndicates and rogue smuggler factions.

### 2. The Leading Pair (L4 Trojans): Pelagia and Suncatcher

These worlds occupy the leading Lagrangian point, forming the most climatically polarized binary in the cluster.

* **Pelagia:** Pelagia is a world composed almost entirely of water. Shallow, interconnected island chains span the
  equator, but the poles are marked by massive, deep-sea trenches. The planet possesses high thermal inertia, remaining
  largely indifferent to the hourly solar eclipse. Its atmosphere is thick, humid, and rich in oxygen, supporting
  expansive, semi-aquatic flora.
  Societally, Pelagia is defined by a rigid class divide. The planet's elite reside on massive, floating
  megastructures—luxurious, self-sustaining artificial islands that glide gracefully across the open ocean, operating
  with an insulated, aristocratic elegance. The common working populace is restricted to the natural, equatorial island
  chains, which are heavily urbanized, densely packed, and largely dedicated to the harvesting of marine resources and
  chemical refining for the upper tiers.
* **Suncatcher:** Pelagia’s twin is a brutal contrast: a planet of low albedo, dominated by vast, heat-absorbing desert
  basins and savannah-like equatorial plateaus. Its surface features very low thermal inertia; it experiences rapid
  thermal spikes during solar exposure and steep cooling during the eclipse. Its landscape is defined by vast,
  wind-scoured plains and permanent dust storms.
  While surface agriculture is nearly impossible, the arid crust is rich in highly concentrated heavy mineral and
  crystalline deposits, driving a massive, volatile mining industry. The primary civil hub is **Dustwall**, a heavily
  fortified, industrial frontier settlement built directly around a massive crater mine. Beyond the hub lies the *
  *Badlands**, a vast, lawless expanse of wind-carved canyons where corporate law enforcement cannot penetrate, leaving
  the territory entirely to cutthroat outlaws, independent prospectors, and opportunistic bounty hunters operating out
  of rustic, makeshift outposts.

### 3. The Trailing Pair (L5 Trojans): Karst and Rime

This pair showcases the extreme effects of gravitational tidal friction on planetary crusts.

* **Karst:** Karst’s surface is a landscape of collapse. Centuries of tidal flexing have shattered the crust into a
  network of massive cenotes and sinkholes. The surface is essentially a barren, razor-sharp plateau. However, the true
  geography of Karst lies beneath; the planet’s interior is hollowed into massive, cavernous networks filled with
  geothermally heated inland seas and bioluminescent forests.
  This subterranean ecosystem is home to the **Lithari**, a primitive intelligent species of tribal hunters. Following
  their discovery, the Flotilla enacted the **Non-Interference Protocol**, declaring Karst a non-interference zone. All
  Sphereborn presence is strictly prohibited, and the Flotilla maintains a permanent orbital blockade to ensure the
  Lithari remain undisturbed until they achieve spaceflight capability.
* **Rime:** Rime is defined by its extreme albedo. The Outer Face is locked under a permanent, kilometers-thick ice
  sheet that reflects the majority of incoming stellar radiation. The planet’s geography is a gradient of temperature:
  the outer hemisphere is a frozen wasteland, while the Inner Face features a single, geothermally maintained circular
  ocean. This coastal ring is the only habitable region, pinned between the encroaching glacial walls and the
  tidal-warmed sea.
