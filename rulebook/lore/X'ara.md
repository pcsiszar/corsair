# The X’ara Cluster: Relic Repositories and Systemic Anchors

The X’ara cluster is an engineered orbital node within The Sphere. In terms of macro-environmental conditions,
magnetospheric protection, and its core baseline, X’ara closely mirrors the Fume system. It hosts three distinct binary
pairs—six terrestrial worlds in total—within a single radiation-shielded orbital track at 800,000 kilometers, locked in
a synchronized 30-hour cycle.

Unlike the radical geographical extremes seen in the Fume system (such as worlds composed entirely of water or ice), the
X’ara cluster features highly uniform, Earth-mass planets. They closely match the temperate climates, continental
distributions, and atmospheric densities of Landfall, varied primarily by localized soil chemistry, ancient Precursor
infrastructure, and distinct cultural or historical landmarks.

In the Ancestral language, the suffixes *-ria/-lia`and`-oa` denote the first and second worlds of each binary pair (
representing 1 and 2).

---

## I. The Central Anchor: X’ara

X’ara is a massive, ringed Gas Giant characterized by a modified metallic hydrogen core that minimizes high-energy
radiation output. Its atmosphere is a turbulent expanse of deep amber and violet clouds, structurally identical in its
baseline physics to Fume. X’ara’s stable magnetosphere serves as the vital life-support mechanism for the six worlds in
its local track, acting as a buffer against stellar wind while producing permanent, brilliant aurorae across the night
skies of its orbiting moons.

---

## II. The Binary Pairs

### 1. The Primary Pair (Barycenter): Irul’lia and Irul’oa (Irul 1 & 2)

Situated at the base of the orbital track, these twin Earth-mass worlds serve as the sovereign, heavily fortified home
territory of the Ancestrals.

* **Irul’lia (Irul 1):** The first world of the primary pair mirrors Landfall’s baseline geography, though high
  atmospheric concentrations of xenon and iodine compounds give its massive northern pine and taiga forests a dark
  purple hue. The planet serves as the administrative core of the Ancestral civilization. Its Inner Face is dominated by
  sprawling, highly advanced spires that tap directly into the planetary lithospheric dynamo. While the society here is
  almost entirely closed off to outsiders, the Flotilla is permitted to maintain a single highly secured diplomatic
  consulate on the surface—the only enclave where Spherites are legally allowed to clear transit protocols and interface
  directly with Ancestral officials.
* **Irul’oa (Irul 2):** The second world is a protective and industrial counterpart. While its geography matches
  Irul'lia's temperate baseline, high concentrations of surface silicates cause its equatorial savannas to grow stiff,
  glassy, golden-orange grasses. Its Outer Face is a barren, glassed desert, constantly scorched by the radiation
  leaking through the edges of the cluster's magnetic shield. It houses hyper-advanced, subterranean manufacturing bays
  and automated cloning facilities dating back millions of years—monumental assets that are fiercely guarded by the
  reclusive Ancestral military.

### 2. The Leading Pair (L4 Trojans): Avon’ria and Avon’oa (Avon 1 & 2)

Occupying the leading Lagrangian point, this binary pair acts as an orbital graveyard for Precursor debris. It has
evolved into a unique cultural convergence zone, famous across the system for its massive cultural festivals.

* **Avon’ria (Avon 1):** This world features temperate forests and broad oceans, but its soil is pocketed by
  hyper-velocity impacts from mechanical detritus. The flora here is radiotrophic, jet-black, and silicon-based. Avon 1
  is primarily populated by the Ancestrals. It plays host to solemn, highly ritualistic festivals where the Ancestrals
  commemorate their history and lineage, celebrating their proximity to the relics of their "Gods."
* **Avon’oa (Avon 2):** The second Avon world features high surface copper deposits, staining its extensive moss-like
  ground cover an intense, metallic green, with rivers of liquid mercury cutting through the plains. This moon is
  heavily Spherite-leaning, populated mostly by Preservationist communities. The cultural festivals here are vibrant,
  chaotic, and artistic, serving as a hub where Spherite subcultures display their unique traditions. The Ancestrals
  find these cultural displays deeply endearing, making Avon 2 a rare point of genuine, friendly interaction between the
  two civilizations.

### 3. The Trailing Pair (L5 Trojans): Oran’ria and Oran’oa (Oran 1 & 2)

Situated at the trailing Lagrangian point, this pair is designated as sacred ground by the Ancestrals. The entire binary
system operates as a massive, strictly enforced wildlife and historical protection zone where permanent settlement is
strictly forbidden.

* **Oran’ria (Oran 1):** Oran'ria is a temperate world with massive global ocean basins. The 30-hour rotation forces
  500-meter-tall tidal waves across these oceans, churning up deep-sea iron that feeds massive, blood-red kelp networks
  along the coastlines. The world is famous for an ancient Precursor visitor who sculpted the landscape into impossible,
  artistic landmarks. Pilgrims from all across the Sphere travel here to see mirror-polished mountain faces that reflect
  the amber starlight in dazzling patterns, a field of black flowers filled with geometric stone monuments that sing
  resonant chords when the wind blows through them, and a gargantuan tree that grew completely around a massive
  waterfall, its hollowed branches and trunk perfectly guiding the torrent downstream.
* **Oran’oa (Oran 2):** Locked in a tight gravitational dance with Oran'ria, the second world handles the cluster's
  tidal energy through its lithosphere, resulting in extensive turquoise-blue wetlands rich in cobalt and sulfur. This
  misty landscape is home to massive, unique beasts of undocumented origin that defy standard evolutionary
  classification. According to Ancestral lore, this planet is the resting place of a living, ancient Precursor. It is
  said she dwells deep within the shifting heart of the world, surrounded by these Great Beasts that she watches over,
  and in return, they stand vigil as her perpetual guardians.
