# Welcome to the Sphere

It has been over 500 years since the four great races—Humanity, Naga, Craggen, and Sylvan—fled a dying home to awaken in
the **Sphere**, an engineered solar system of 156 habitable worlds. What began as a struggle for survival on
**Landfall** has developed into a civilization organized around binary moons and gas giants.

Today, the **Flotilla**, a massive independent fleet, maintains security in the void between worlds, while planetary
colonies govern themselves under the Skyline Accords. When jurisdictional or security issues cross the boundary between
ground and space—such as corporate espionage, piracy, or the discovery of Precursor technology—the system utilizes *
*Corsairs**.

## The Age of Corsairs

You are a member of a **Corsair Cell**, a team of specialists operating aboard a high-performance vessel. Vouched for by
the major powers of the Sphere—the military, the colonies, and the corporations—you hold a mandate to operate across
different jurisdictions.

You are authorized to investigate and use force to maintain stability in the Sphere. Your status as a Corsair is a
privilege granted by your Sponsors and can be revoked based on your performance and adherence to the mandate.
