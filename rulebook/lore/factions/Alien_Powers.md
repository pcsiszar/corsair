# Faction Dossier: Alien Powers & Foreign Empires

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| JURISDICTION: NEIGHBORING STAR SYSTEMS, DEMILITARIZED ZONES, & QUARANTINED MOONS  |
| AUTHORITY: THE SACRED VIGIL, IMPERIAL DECREES, THE GREAT TITHE, & FLOTILLA TREATIES|
| PRIMARY POWERS: THE ANCESTRALS OF X'ARA, THE ORKISH EMPIRE, THE SCAVENGER CLANS,   |
|                 THE LITHARI OF KARST                                              |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

To the average citizen living on Landfall or Nimbus, the universe ends at the edge of the Fume cluster. They know there are twenty-six gas giants suspended in the cosmic clockwork of the Sphere, because their high school astronomy teachers made them memorize the orbital resonances, but the sixty-million-kilometer void between planetary tracks is so vast that foreign civilizations feel like distant myths—until an Orkish battlecruiser's transponder lights up on an outer sensor buoy, or an Ancestral diplomatic barge slides silently into an orbital docking ring like an ivory ghost.

The Newcomers are not alone in the Sphere. They were merely the last to arrive.

To the east across the void lies **X'ara**, where an ancient, biologically uniform civilization has lived in unchanging, crystalline stillness for millions of years under the rule of an immortal prophet. To the west, across the disputed buffer moons of **Wanabo**, lies the iron-bound martial autocracy of the **Orkish Empire**, whose scarred dreadnoughts still point their forward ram-prows across the demilitarized zone established by the Great Truce. Drifting through the lightless gaps between systems are the nomadic, cloned armadas of the **Scavs**, driven by an insatiable religious greed to strip copper and Precursor tech from living hulls. And tucked away in the subterranean fungal grottoes of **Karst**, mere hours from Landfall by fusion burn, a primitive species of tribal hunters chips stone hand-axes in total ignorance of the starships passing sixty kilometers above their heads.

Foreign powers do not operate by Newcomer rules. They do not care about municipal council ordinances, they view corporate patent law with utter contempt, and they treat the Flotilla’s Skyline Accords as a quaint local custom.

Below are the dossiers on the four alien and foreign civilizations that share the engineered heavens.

---

## 1. The Ancestrals of X'ara

### Civil Profile & The Sacred Vigil
The **Ancestrals** are the oldest surviving civilization in the mapped Sphere. Residing across the hyper-uniform, engineered binary worlds of the neighboring **X'ara cluster** (a six-world system mirroring Fume’s orbital track at ninety million kilometers), the Ancestrals are a humanoid species of serene, ascetic elegance. They possess tall, slender frames, pale opalescent skin, and large, dark eyes adapted to the soft amber glow of the dwarf star.

Unlike the noisy, multi-species, ever-expanding chaos of Newcomer society, Ancestral civilization is defined by absolute, frozen permanence. For uncounted millennia, they have lived under the strict theological dogma of the **Sacred Vigil** (or the *Vigil of Reverent Stillness*). Ancestral scripture teaches that the universe was perfected by the ancient "Makers," and that mortal expansion, reckless curiosity, and technological tinkering are grotesque sins of hubris. 

Their cities on the capital world of **Irul'lia (Irul 1)** are monumental, geometric spires of self-repairing lithospheric composite that tap directly into planetary geothermal dynamos without producing a whisper of industrial smoke. Ancestrals do not innovate; they maintain. They wear flowing, radiotrophic silks that absorb ambient radiation, speak in low, melodic cadences, and regard Newcomers with a mixture of polite fascination, condescending pity, and deep philosophical horror—viewing them as hyperactive toddlers who have somehow been given the keys to a loaded fusion reactor.

### Leadership & The Great Avon Schism
At the apex of Ancestral society sits the **Archon**—an enigmatic, revered, and seemingly immortal ruler who has reigned from the Great Spire of Irul'lia since before Newcomer history began. The Archon speaks with absolute spiritual and civil authority, interpreting the will of the Makers and guarding the quiet stasis of Ancestral civilization.

However, contact with the Newcomers in 425 AA cracked this monolithic stasis wide open, creating the **Great Avon Schism** across the binary worlds of Avon 1 and Avon 2:

* **The Gilded Movement:** Centered among the restless youth of Avon 1 who looked across the narrow sixty-thousand-kilometer gap at the colorful, bustling night-skies of Newcomer-settled Avon 2. Captivated by Newcomer vitality, vibrant art, raucous music, and the shocking discovery that life could change, thousands of young Ancestrals have embraced the Gilded Movement. They discard traditional grey robes for colorful tourist vests, slip past monastic checkpoints, and travel to Fume as enthusiastic "Space Tourists"—marveling at slag heaps, attending baseball games in Dustwall, and drinking bitter coffee while dreaming of reform.
* **The Pure Blade:** In violent opposition stand the Pure Blade—an ascetic, militant faction of hawks, temple inquisitors, and military commanders who view Newcomer culture as a virulent biological contagion. The Pure Blade believe that mortal contact is a mortal sin that will bring ruin upon the Sphere. Armed with electromagnetic lances and sleek, high-acceleration interceptors, the Pure Blade constantly lobbies the Archon for a holy crusade to cleanse the Newcomer moons before their noise spreads further.

### Territory & Signature Assets
The Ancestrals govern the six worlds of the X'ara cluster: the primary twins **Irul'lia & Irul'oa**, the festival worlds of **Avon'ria & Avon'oa**, and the sacred, uninhabited wildlife preserves of **Oran'ria & Oran'oa**.

* **The Consulate of Irul'lia:** A heavily fortified, isolated diplomatic enclave on the Ancestral capital world, representing the only square kilometer in core Ancestral space where Newcomers are legally permitted to stand.
* **The Avon 2 Convergence:** The vibrant sister moon where Newcomer Preservationists and Ancestral pilgrims mingle in massive seasonal art festivals, trading silk, poetry, and copper handicrafts.
* **Ancestral Spires & Void-Cutters:** Geometric, needle-thin starships constructed from non-magnetic crystalline composites, powered by high-efficiency ion drives and armed with precision electromagnetic pulse arrays.

### Relationship with Corsairs
Official diplomatic relations between the Flotilla and the Ancestrals are governed by the strict **Ancestral Treaty of 432 AA**. The Flotilla maintains a quiet, nervous consulate on Irul 1, desperate to avoid provoking the reclusive empire into war.

However, the Archon’s court and the Gilded Movement frequently interact with Corsairs behind closed doors. Because the Archon recognizes that official naval incursions by either side could trigger a systemic conflagration, Ancestral magistrates quietly issue **Discreet Petitions** to neutral Corsair cells. Corsairs are hired to recover stolen sacred relics from Newcomer black-market cartels, track down rebellious young Ancestral aristocrats who have run away to join rock bands on Nimbus, or discreetly intercept Pure Blade strike craft before they can execute unauthorized terror raids against Newcomer border stations.

## 2. The Orkish Empire

### Civil Profile & The Martial Ascendancy
If the Ancestrals represent frozen, meditative grace, the **Orkish Empire** represents raw, roaring industrial survival and unyielding imperial tyranny. Emerging from the outer void beyond the buffer system of **Wanabo**, the Orks are a massive, muscular, green-to-grey-skinned humanoid species built with dense bone structures, dual circulatory systems, and an astonishing tolerance for physical trauma.

The Orks are not mindless barbarians; they are a proud, deeply disciplined, and brutally efficient martial civilization. Their society does not believe in councils, parliamentary debates, or democratic compromises. The Orkish Empire is built on absolute autocratic hierarchy, institutional slavery, and normalized violence. The empire's lower castes—conquered populations, enslaved debtors, and disgraced warriors—labor in the crushing dark of subterranean ore pits and orbital foundry galleys, while the martial warrior castes maintain iron order.

Yet, despite this internal brutality, the Empire functions with fearsome unity. The entire war machine is held together by an insatiable appetite for conquest and a cultural conviction that strength of arms is the only objective arbiter of sovereignty in the void.

### Leadership & Emperor Vrakketh
The absolute master of the Orkish Empire is **Emperor Vrakketh**. 

Vrakketh is a legendary, terrifying figure across the Wanabo frontier. A towering, scarred warlord who fought his way through decades of civil bloodfeuds to claim the Imperial Throne, Vrakketh manages to steer the violent, volatile frenzy of dozens of rival clans in whatever direction he points his finger. He rules through sheer force of will, ruthless intelligence, and an uncompromising code of martial fealty. 

Beneath the Emperor, Clan Warlords command regional battle fleets and planetary garrisons across the outer rim, competing fiercely for his favor. Imperial law is brief, absolute, and executed on the spot: treason, cowardice, and insubordination carry the mandatory penalty of public execution; disputes between officers are settled in ritual melee combat before their assembled troops.

### Territory & Military Doctrine
The Empire holds sovereign dominion over the outer systems beyond Wanabo, maintaining heavily fortified garrisons across the scarred frontier moons of **Kazahn** and **Karak**.

* **The Iron Rams:** Orkish naval doctrine rejects delicate, long-range skirmishing. Their warships—colossal, heavy-displacement battlecruisers encased in meters of crude rolled-steel armor—are built around massive, solid-tungsten **Forward Ram-Prows**. In combat, Ork captains burn their oversized fusion drives directly toward the enemy at maximum acceleration, absorbing point-blank kinetic fire before literally ramming through the opponent's hull, followed immediately by dozens of armored **Boarding Torpedoes** that vomit squads of howling, heavy-armored shock troopers into the enemy ship's corridors.
* **Bipedal Combat Walkers:** On planetary surfaces, the Orks deploy heavy two-legged battlemechs—armored, diesel-fusion walking fortresses equipped with pneumatic siege hammers and heavy rotary autocannons that pulverized Flotilla trench lines during the war.
* **The Wanabo Demilitarized Zone:** A tense, heavily mined buffer sector negotiated ten years ago under the Great Truce, separating Newcomer and Orkish space, patrolled by nervous pickets from both sides.

### Relationship with Corsairs
Formally, the Orkish Empire is bound to the Flotilla by **the Great Truce (509 AA)**—signed by Emperor Vrakketh and the Flotilla Admiralty after the apocalyptic crash of the *Evergaol*, where Orkish heavy rams and Flotilla battlecruisers were forced into a desperate battlefield alliance alongside Goldheart and Stormdrinker to drag the ancient dreadnought down. 

While the truce holds, border tensions in 518 AA are at a fever pitch. Emperor Vrakketh watches the battered Flotilla with calculating patience, keeping his younger, hot-headed warlords on a short leash while his shipyards rebuild their ram-prow fleets.

Because imperial naval divisions cannot cross the DMZ without shattering the truce, Orkish warlords frequently engage in proxy warfare—quietly contracting independent Newcomer Corsair cells as "Honor-Bound Auxiliaries." The Orks respect Corsairs who demonstrate raw courage, high-speed tactical aggression, and direct speech. If a Corsair crew stands their ground against an Ork boarding party without flinching, an Orkish commander is just as likely to offer them a flagon of fermented grain-mash and a lucrative bounty contract as they are to throw them out the airlock.

## 3. The Scavs (Scavenger Raider Clans)

### Biological Profile & The Great Tithe
If the Orks are an iron-clad army, the **Scavs** are a locust swarm. 

Drifting through the deep, lightless void between planetary tracks in jury-rigged, scrap-welded hulls that resemble tumbling junk heaps, the Scavs are a nomadic, cloned raider species. Biologically, they are horrifyingly adapted to zero-gravity existence: standing two meters tall with lean, sinewy frames, leathery grey-green hide, and **four fully functional arms** terminating in triple-jointed, clawed digits. They possess multi-faceted optical clusters that see clearly in pitch-black vacuum, and their respiratory systems can endure minutes of explosive decompression without fatal trauma.

The entire Scav civilization operates as a fanatical, holy pyramid scheme governed by the dogma of **The Great Tithe**. To spacers across the Sphere, the Scavs are driven by an obsessive compulsion to strip starships of rare copper wiring, high-efficiency fusion coils, intact electronics, and above all, ancient Precursor relics. 

Scav clans believe that biological life is an ongoing spiritual debt owed to a shadowy, mythical master they revere as **The Collector**, who dwells in a fabled deep-space repository known as "The Hoard." To earn the right to reproduce, to receive vital genetic stability treatments, and to buy fuel for their ships, every clan must continuously deliver fresh scrap and relics to the Tithe. A clan that fails to meet its quota faces violent repurposing; a clan that delivers an intact alien focusing core is elevated to supremacy.

### Leadership & Clan Structure
Scav society is divided into hundreds of nomadic, fiercely competitive **Raider Clans** (such as the *Rust-Weevils*, the *Void-Leeches*, and the *Iron-Gnawers*). Clans live aboard sprawling "Hulk-Nests"—derelict commercial freighters and station modules lashed together with industrial tow-cables, unpressurized and tumbling through the dark on ballistic drift vectors to avoid radar detection.

Leadership belongs to the **Warmasters**—massive, heavily cybernetically augmented veterans whose four arms have been reinforced with hydraulic actuators and cutting tools. However, daily clan logistics are managed by **Tithe-Counters**: fanatical, multi-armed scribes who obsessively weigh, categorize, and catalog every scrap bolt, coil of wire, and canned food ration seized during raids, calculating precisely how many grams of copper are required to pay off the clan’s spiritual debt.

### Territory, Tactics & Signature Assets
The Scavs hold no permanent moons or planetary bases; their territory is the "Deep Drift"—the unpatrolled voids between Fume, X'ara, and Wanabo.

* **Scrap-Welded Strike Craft:** Erratic, high-acceleration boarding boats assembled from stripped civilian hulls, equipped with overpowered surplus thrusters, unshielded reactors, and crude forward drills.
* **The Great Tithe Fleet:** Massive, hollowed-out metallic asteroids where hundreds of raider crews dock to trade scrap, repair hulls, and settle blood feuds before burning outward to pay the Tithe.
* **Four-Armed Boarding Doctrines:** In close-quarters combat, Scavs are terrifying: they cling to zero-g bulkheads with two hands while firing high-rate-of-fire scrap-throwers and pneumatic harpoons with the other two, crawling through ventilation conduits and maintenance chases that human-sized defenders cannot enter.

### Relationship with Corsairs
To the average commercial hauler, the Scavs are the absolute boogeymen of the void: silent, ruthless raiders who will vent a ship’s air supply simply to strip the copper piping out of the galley walls. 

For Corsairs, however, the Scavs are the most frequent, dangerous, and lucrative combat contract in the system. The Flotilla and Trade Alliance continuously issue **Scavenger Interdiction Bounties**, paying bounties for every destroyed raider hull and rescued freighter. 

Yet, veteran Corsairs know that Scavs can occasionally be bargained with. Scavs are deeply terrified of small, elite Corsair cells—they know from bitter experience that Corsairs carry mil-spec MANTLE chassis, combat experience, and precision weapons that can butcher an entire boarding clan in narrow corridors. If a Corsair crew captures a Scav raider and offers them a cache of high-purity refined copper or broken Precursor scrap, the Tithe-Counters will happily trade valuable navigation data, reveal the locations of lost derelicts, or sell out rival clans without a single pang of conscience.

## 4. The Lithari of Karst

### Civil Profile & The Primitive Cradle
While the Ancestrals, Orks, and Scavs command starships and weapons of war, the fourth foreign power in the Sphere possesses no engines, no metal armor, and no concept that space exists.

They are the **Lithari**.

Discovered in 365 AA on **Karst**—the tidal-flexed, cenote-shattered binary twin of the ice world Rime at Fume's trailing L5 Lagrangian point—the Lithari are an indigenous intelligent species of tribal hunters. While Karst’s surface is a razor-sharp, radiation-swept plateau of limestone sinkholes, its interior is hollowed into massive, cavernous subterranean networks. Here, warmed by geothermal volcanic vents and illuminated by towering bioluminescent fungal forests, the Lithari live around vast, underground freshwater seas.

Biologically, the Lithari are uniquely adapted to their subterranean world: compact, muscular humanoids with smooth, slate-grey skin, wide feline pupils capable of seeing in near-total darkness, and sensitive acoustic antennae along their jawlines that detect micro-seismic vibrations through the stone. They are an early tool-using, stone-age civilization. They fashion spears from volcanic obsidian, weave waterproof garments from fungal fiber, hunt massive subterranean aquatic beasts, and paint sacred, glowing murals on cavern walls depicting the rhythmic rumble of the planet’s tectonic heartbeat.

### The Non-Interference Protocol (368 AA)
The discovery of the Lithari sparked the greatest moral and philosophical debate in Newcomer history. Corporate mining cartels—particularly Caldera Foundry and early pharmaceutical trusts—immediately lobbied to colonize Karst, eager to strip-mine its rare-earth limestone deposits and harvest its unique bioluminescent flora for commercial enzymes.

However, the memory of the Newcomers' own origin—waking up in the dark of seed arks with no knowledge of their homeworlds—was still fresh in the cultural consciousness. Led by the Crossroads Council and passionately championed by **Goldheart**, Newcomer leadership declared that to exploit a young, dreaming species would be a moral sin that would stain Newcomer civilization forever.

In 368 AA, the Flotilla High Council formally ratified the **Non-Interference Protocol**:
* Karst was declared a **Total Quarantine Zone**. All civilian settlement, resource prospecting, and commercial transit within the moon's gravity well are strictly prohibited under penalty of life imprisonment.
* The Lithari must be allowed to develop naturally, without external contamination, until they achieve independent orbital spaceflight capability through their own efforts.

### The Karst Picket & Naval Enforcement
To enforce the protocol, the Flotilla maintains a permanent naval presence: **The Karst L1 Picket Station**. A heavily armed orbital sentinel platform continuously sweeps the moon’s upper atmosphere, maintaining an automated radar and interdiction grid. Any vessel attempting to enter Karst’s orbital envelope without unanimous high-clearance diplomatic authorization is issued an immediate vector warning—and if they fail to turn back, the picket’s kinetic railguns open fire without hesitation.

### Relationship with Corsairs
To the legitimate Corsair, Karst is a sacred, forbidden sanctuary; entering its airspace is an unforgivable breach of the Charter.

However, Karst is also an irresistible siren song for the Sphere’s worst elements. Because Karst's subterranean ecosystems are rich in unharvested biochemical enzymes, rare geothermal crystals, and undiscovered Precursor ruins, unscrupulous corporate black-ops teams, bioprospecting cartels, and underworld smugglers are constantly attempting to breach the blockade. Smugglers slip through the sensor grid using masked drive vectors, landing on the surface and descending into the sinkholes to poach rare species, steal Lithari sacred relics, or abduct living specimens for corporate laboratories.

The Flotilla’s Patrol Wing regularly employs trusted, high-reputation Corsair cells under **Quarantine Interdiction Writs**. Corsairs are tasked with running down poacher vessels before they can reach the surface, tracking illicit expeditions into the perilous, unmapped subterranean caverns of Karst, and extracting intruders without exposing the primitive Lithari to starship engines, firearms, or modern technology.
