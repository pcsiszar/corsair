# Faction Dossier: The Civilian Institutions

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| JURISDICTION: CROSS-SYSTEM CIVIL LAW, TREATY OVERSIGHT, LABOR, SCIENCE, & RELIEF  |
| AUTHORITY: THE SKYLINE ACCORDS (335 AA), INTER-COLONIAL CHARTERS, & UNION PACTS   |
| PRIMARY BODIES: THE COLONIAL FORUM, INTERPLANETARY CRIMINAL COURT (ICC),          |
|                 SPHERE RESEARCH CENTER (SRC), UNION OF UNIONS (U2), IPRO          |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

If you listen solely to the admirals of the Flotilla and the board directors of the great industrial cartels, you might easily conclude that the Sphere is held together entirely by railgun trajectories and titanium rivets. 

In truth, railguns are terrible at resolving municipal water tariffs, and titanium rivets have very little to say when a freighter captain registered on Pelagia defaults on a grain contract signed on Landfall while docked in orbit above Nimbus. Between the localized sovereignty of the planetary councils down in the mud and the cold martial authority of the Flotilla up in the high void sits a sprawling, indispensable ecosystem of interplanetary civilian institutions.

These are the engines of civil civilization: the legislators who control the navy's purse strings, the magistrates who try cross-border criminals without starting a planetary war, the ivory-tower physicists who keep ancient Precursor machinery from eating the local atmosphere, the labor stewards who remind trillion-credit corporations who actually turns the wrenches, and the relief doctors who fly unarmed hospital ships into smoking disaster zones. They possess no dreadnought fleets and claim no planetary soil, yet without their ledgers, subpoenas, containment vaults, picket lines, and triage tents, the eight billion souls of the Sphere would have strangled each other in an airlock centuries ago.

Below are the five paramount interplanetary civilian institutions that govern, study, unionize, and mend the modern Sphere.

---

## 1. The Colonial Forum

### Civic Profile & Institutional Ethos
The **Colonial Forum** is the supreme interplanetary legislature of Newcomer civilization and the ultimate civilian check upon the military power of the Flotilla. Established under the constitutional framework of the **Skyline Accords of 335 AA**, the Forum was designed to solve a problem as old as government itself: how to fund a heavily armed peacekeeping navy capable of fighting off alien raiders without allowing the admirals to declare themselves emperors the moment the shooting stops.

To prevent any single moon from claiming territorial jurisdiction—or subtly threatening to turn off the parliamentary heating pipes during a heated tariff vote—the Colonial Forum is seated permanently in neutral orbital space aboard the **Civic Rotunda Spindle of Redmane Station**, complemented by diplomatic annexes aboard **Waypoint Station** at Fume L1. Here, rotating at a comfortable, egalitarian 0.8g of spin gravity, elected delegations from the five sovereign moons of Fume convene alongside territorial representatives from **Avon 2** in the X'ara cluster and frontier observers from **Kazahn** in Wanabo.

The prevailing ethos of the Forum is *Loud, Expensive Peace*. The chamber is a perpetual, cantankerous storm of competing planetary interests: Suncatcher delegates demanding subsidies for heavy ore transport, Pelagian barons arguing over maritime export duties, Cloudspire technocrats lobbying for avionics patent protections, and Rime burgomasters grumbling that everyone else talks too much. It is slow, theatrical, and drowned in procedural subcommittees, yet it has successfully prevented both inter-colonial civil war and military dictatorship for nearly two centuries.

### Leadership & Internal Power Structure
The Forum is presided over by the **Speaker-General of the Forum** (currently **Chancellor Maera Vance-Korin**, a sharp-tongued Gnome constitutional scholar from Crossroads who wields her parliamentary gavel with the precision of a sniper rifle). Delegates are apportioned through a complex constitutional compromise balancing planetary population with industrial and agricultural output, ensuring that densely populated Landfall and Nimbus cannot simply outvote the resource-critical outer moons of Pelagia, Suncatcher, and Rime.

While the Flotilla’s Admiralty Board exercises day-to-day operational command over warships and patrols, the Admiralty answers directly to the Forum. The Forum controls the **Interplanetary Appropriations Ledger**—meaning that if an Admiral wishes to lay down a new strike cruiser or requisition extra fusion torpedoes, they must endure six months of open committee hearings while a municipal delegate from Rime questions the fuel expenditure of last year's training exercises. Furthermore, the Forum holds the sole constitutional authority to ratify foreign treaties (such as the **Non-Interference Protocol of 368 AA** and **the Great Truce of 509 AA**) and to amend the founding **Corsair Charter**.

### Jurisdiction, Operations & Signature Assets
The Forum claims no physical territory beyond its leased orbital chambers, but its legislative mandates bind all signatory governments, Flotilla commands, and chartered sponsors across the three systems.

* **The Grand Rotunda Spindle (Redmane Station):** A kilometer-wide, terraced parliamentary amphitheater suspended within Redmane Station's primary civilian ring, complete with pressurized gallery boxes for press syndicates, corporate lobbyists, and labor stewards.
* **The Appropriations & Audit Bureau:** An army of relentless civilian forensic accountants who scrutinize every Flotilla procurement voucher, customs tariff, and Sovereign Wealth Fund disbursement.
* **The Diplomatic Courier Corps:** A fleet of fast, unarmed, high-visibility diplomatic pinnaces carrying sealed treaty dispatches and senatorial legations between Fume, the X'ara Consulate, and the Wanabo neutral zone.
* **The Charter Oversight Delegation:** The civilian wing of the **Corsair Oversight Committee**, responsible for vetting new Sponsor applications and ensuring Corsair cells operate in the public interest.

### Relationship with Corsairs
The Colonial Forum is the constitutional parent of the Corsair system. Ten years after the Frontier War and the fall of the *Evergaol*, it was the Forum that passed the emergency appropriations bill authorizing the **New Wave of Corsairs** to fill the void left by the Flotilla’s shattered battlecruisers.

Individual Forum committees and multi-moon coalitions regularly act as direct Sponsors, issuing **Senatorial Investigative Writs** and **Diplomatic Protection Mandates**. When a planetary government and a megacorporation deadlock over a border dispute, when a high-ranking diplomatic envoy requires discreet armed escort across the Wanabo demilitarized zone, or when rumors surface of unauthorized black-budget naval activity, Forum subcommittees hire independent Corsair cells to uncover the truth and report directly back to the Rotunda floor.

## 2. The Interplanetary Criminal Court (ICC)

### Civic Profile & Institutional Ethos
Under the Skyline Accords, a criminal who robs a payroll vault in Crossroads is tried by a Landfall magistrate, and a pirate caught boarding a freighter in deep space is court-martialed by a Flotilla tribunal. This tidy arrangement lasted roughly forty-eight hours after the treaty was signed, until an enterprising syndicate financier embezzled forty million credits from a Cloudspire bank, laundered the bearer bonds through an orbital clearinghouse at L1, hired a Suncatcher mercenary crew to burn the evidence in deep space, and retired to a floating pontoon villa on Pelagia. Five separate courts claimed jurisdiction, three refused to extradite, and two threatened to impound each other's grain ships.

To prevent the legal seam between gravity wells from turning into an outlaw paradise, the **Interplanetary Criminal Court (ICC)** was chartered as the supreme cross-jurisdictional judiciary of the Sphere. Seated in neutral orbital territory aboard the **High Tribunal Ring of Waypoint Station**—hanging precisely at the L1 gravitational null-point between Landfall and Nimbus—the ICC occupies a unique legal bubble. A fugitive delivered to Waypoint's tribunal docks has not entered any planetary atmosphere, allowing suspects from any moon or orbital lane to be arraigned, tried, and sentenced without triggering municipal extradition loops.

The Court’s philosophy is *The Long Ledger*: orbital mechanics may allow a criminal to outrun a local sheriff's hopper, but no sub-light engine in the universe can outrun a properly stamped cross-system warrant.

### Leadership & Internal Power Structure
The ICC is governed by the **High Bench of Magistrates**, a panel of fifteen senior jurists appointed for life by the Colonial Forum to insulate them from planetary election cycles and corporate lobbying. The Chief Magistrate—currently **High Justice Orin Vael**, an austere, intimidatingly well-read Elf jurist who has memorized the statutory codes of seven moons—presides over a legal synthesis that blends Crossroads civil statutes, Cloudspire contract law, Pelagian maritime code, and Suncatcher resource equity.

Indictments and investigations are driven by the **Office of the Procurator-General**, a fearless corps of interplanetary prosecutors, forensic data-miners, and witness-protection specialists. The Procurator’s office handles crimes that cross atmospheric boundaries, multi-moon syndicate conspiracies, systemic corporate fraud, violations of the Karst Non-Interference Protocol, and war crimes arising from the Frontier War.

### Jurisdiction, Operations & Signature Assets
By deliberate constitutional design, the ICC possesses sweeping judicial authority over cross-border felonies—**but commands zero warships and no standing police force of its own** outside its courtroom bailiffs and prison wardens. The signatories of the Skyline Accords were willing to create an independent court, provided the judges could never march a battalion of troops down a planetary street.

* **The High Tribunal Ring (Waypoint Station L1):** A solemn, steel-and-glass judicial concourse housing twelve high courtrooms, sequestered jury habitats, and encrypted evidence vaults.
* **The Central Warrant Registry:** The system-wide judicial beacon network that broadcasts active indictments, asset freezes, and bounty valuations across every customs buoy from Rime to Kazahn.
* **The Detention Hulk *Justiciar*:** A decommissioned Flotilla heavy cruiser permanently moored four hundred kilometers off Waypoint Station, stripped of its main drives and converted into a high-security, escape-proof orbital penitentiary for syndicate bosses, rogue warlords, and war criminals.
* **The Witness Sanctuary Network:** A classified web of orbital safehouses and identity-scrubbing clinics used to shield whistleblowers testifying against megacorporations or underworld cartels.

### Relationship with Corsairs
Because planetary police cannot legally cross the cloud ceiling and Flotilla battlecruisers cannot kick down doors in a Pelagian reef shanty, **the Corsairs serve as the de facto marshals and long arm of the Interplanetary Criminal Court**. Without Corsairs, an ICC indictment is merely an eloquent piece of stationary; with Corsairs, it is a breach charge on the suspect's airlock.

The Office of the Procurator-General is one of the most active issuers of high-paying contracts in the Sphere. The Court issues the iconic **Red-Seal Extradition Warrants** and **Cross-System Subpoenas**, authorizing chartered cells to pursue indicted fugitives across any atmospheric or orbital boundary, seize frozen corporate hardware, and protect star witnesses from syndicate hit squads. There is only one non-negotiable catch: the ICC is a court of law, not an assassination bureau. Red-Seal warrants pay their full bounty only when the defendant is delivered to Waypoint Station breathing, coherent, and fit to stand in the dock.

## 3. The Sphere Research Center (SRC)

### Civic Profile & Institutional Ethos
While megacorporations maintain proprietary weapons labs and skunkworks, the **Sphere Research Center (SRC)** stands apart as an independent, interplanetary academic and scientific consortium chartered for the advancement—and survival—of all mortal civilization. Headquartered in the sprawling stone-and-glass campuses of Crossroads on Landfall and maintaining high-altitude storm observatories in Cloudspire on Nimbus, the SRC is the paramount intellectual institution of the Sphere.

The SRC’s historic prestige is unmatched: in 38 AA, working in direct scientific partnership with **Stormdrinker**, the Scholar Dragon of Nimbus, SRC physicists unraveled the bio-fusion principles that led to the invention of the **Draco Fusion Drive**—the foundational propulsion breakthrough that opened the interplanetary space age. Through **The Covenant** forged in 14 AA alongside Stormdrinker and Goldheart, the SRC's innermost circle has served for five centuries as the quiet scientific custodian of the Sphere's deepest truths.

Today, the SRC operates on a dual philosophy of insatiable scholarly curiosity and extreme existential caution. To the public, its universities and orbital observatories map the 4:2:1 Laplace resonance of the twenty-six gas giants, calibrate system-wide atomic clocks, develop drought-resistant crops, and study Precursor architectural ruins. Behind sealed vault doors, however, the Center enforces ruthless containment protocols. Remembering the apocalyptic awakening of the automated dreadnought *Evergaol* in 508 AA, the SRC works tirelessly to quarantine active Precursor lattices and prevent reckless corporate prospectors or Scav raiders from triggering ancient, system-sterilizing defense networks.

### Leadership & Internal Power Structure
The Center is governed by the **Council of Curators**, an elected senate of senior physicists, xeno-archaeologists, xenobiologists, and propulsion engineers presided over by **Arch-Curator Varisia Lin**, an elderly, razor-sharp Sylvan metallurgist. Lin is a master of institutional survival: she keeps the SRC financially independent through licensing royalties from the **Draco Drive Patent Foundation**, allowing her to say "no" to both corporate defense contractors demanding weapons schematics and Flotilla admirals demanding exotic telemetry.

While the SRC is a civilian academic body, it maintains an elite, specialized internal security and containment branch: the **Archive Wardens**. Rather than heavy military artillery, Wardens deploy with electromagnetic Faraday cages, acoustic disruptors, radiation-shielded containment crates, and neural-dampening countermeasures designed to secure volatile Precursor relics and sanitize contaminated excavation sites before dangerous schematics can leak onto the black market.

### Jurisdiction, Operations & Signature Assets
Under interplanetary treaty, the SRC holds statutory quarantine authority over designated Precursor ruins, anomalous scientific phenomena, and deep-space research stations across Fume and X'ara.

* **The Crossroads Grand Institute:** The sprawling flagship university campus on Landfall, housing the system’s largest historical libraries, deep-core computing arrays, and xeno-archaeology laboratories.
* **The Cloudspire Storm Observatories:** High-altitude research platforms anchored directly to Nimbus’s upper spires, studying high-energy atmospheric physics and bio-fusion harmonics under the wing of Stormdrinker.
* **The Draco Drive Patent Foundation:** The public-trust endowment that holds the foundational patents on continuous-thrust fusion propulsion, funding all SRC universities and grants through commercial licensing fees.
* **The Vault Isolation Repositories:** Heavily shielded, subterranean and zero-g containment facilities where active Precursor transducers and hazardous non-baryonic relics are locked away from the world.

### Relationship with Corsairs
The SRC is one of the most prolific, generous, and occasionally nerve-wracking patrons of Corsair cells. While Archive Wardens guard the Center's domestic campuses, the Curators lack the starships and cross-jurisdictional warrants to chase smugglers across the Drift or mount armed expeditions into contested frontier ruins.

The Council of Curators regularly issues **Academic Recovery Petitions** and **Quarantine Containment Writs**. These contracts task Corsairs with intercepting Jailer smugglers carrying live Precursor cores out of the Evergaol Exclusion Zone, recovering stolen research slates from corporate industrial spies, escorting eccentric field xeno-biologists into the hostile wilderness of Karak, or investigating newly awakened Vault anomalies before the Iron Fang syndicate can strip them for parts. In return, the SRC pays in crisp universal credits, priority access to experimental sensor suites, and bespoke starship tuning that money cannot legally buy.

## 4. The Union of Unions (U2)

### Civic Profile & Institutional Ethos
In the glossy recruitment brochures printed by Caldera Foundry and Ker'Satz Korp, the industrial expansion of the Sphere is depicted as a heroic triumph of visionary executives and gleaming machinery. If you ask anyone who has ever spent a twelve-hour shift scraping frozen ammonia slush off a docking gantry at Port Zenith or breathing recycled silica dust three kilometers beneath Suncatcher, you will get a very different history lesson.

**The Union of Unions—universally known as U2—**is the grand interplanetary labor confederation that unites hundreds of planetary trade guilds, orbital dockworker brotherhoods, deep-core miners' syndicates, and atmospheric scrubber crews into a single, immovable bloc. Forged in the grease, soot, and bloody wildcat strikes of the **200s and 300s AA**, when unchecked megacorporations routinely paid workers in non-transferable company scrip and threatened to turn off habitat oxygen valves to break wage protests, U2 established a simple, ironclad truth: *No ship burns, no smelter pours, and no airlock cycles unless a working hand pulls the lever.*

The culture of U2 is gritty, fiercely egalitarian, and bound by a quasi-religious devotion to mutual solidarity across all ten lineages. In a U2 hiring hall, nobody cares whether your grandparents came off the Human ark or the Craggen ark; they care whether you check your partner's air seal before stepping into the lock and whether you hold the picket line when corporate security shows up with shock batons.

### Leadership & Internal Power Structure
U2 is governed democratically from the bottom up. Every local shop floor, cargo berth, and mining shaft elects a **Shop Steward**; those stewards elect regional councils, who in turn send delegates to the **General Congress of Stewards**. The Congress convenes in the noisy, oil-scented amphitheater of **Solidarity Hall**, situated in the working-class orbital docks of **Port Zenith** above Landfall, with major chapter citadels in Dustwall and the mid-decks of Cloudspire.

The confederation is currently led by **General Secretary Brannick "Bolts" O'ul**, a scarred, barrel-chested Craggen zero-g structural welder who lost two fingers to a faulty corporate crane forty years ago and has spent every day since making corporate boardrooms regret it. O'ul does not wear tailored suits; he wears a high-visibility canvas union vest covered in local chapter pins and negotiates collective bargaining agreements with the calm, terrifying certainty of a man who can freeze forty million tons of interplanetary freight with a single radio call.

### Jurisdiction, Operations & Signature Assets
U2 claims no sovereign territory and fields no armed paramilitary divisions. Instead, it wields the most feared economic weapon in the Sphere: **The System-Wide General Strike**—colloquially known on the docks as *"Dropping the Spanner."* If a megacorporation or the Trade Alliance crosses U2's red lines on life-support safety or wage theft, U2 locks the gantry cranes at Cross-Drift Central, walks off the drydocks at Port Zenith, and banks the smelting furnaces on Suncatcher until the executives come back to the table.

* **Solidarity Hall (Port Zenith):** The bustling orbital headquarters of U2, serving simultaneously as a congress hall, a transit hostel for traveling spacers, a vocational training academy, and the loudest tavern in low orbit.
* **The Mutual Strike & Widow's Relief Fund:** A massive, fiercely guarded credit reserve funded by modest weekly dues from two billion union workers, used to feed striking families, pay medical bills for injured spacers, and underwrite Corsair sponsorships.
* **The Stewards' Safety Inspectorate:** Teams of independent, veteran structural and life-support engineers who conduct unannounced safety audits on corporate mining rigs, freighters, and habitats, publishing public "Red-Tag Warnings" on death-trap vessels.
* **The Blue-Ticket Legal Network:** A pugnacious corps of labor attorneys who battle corporate lawyers in planetary courts and before the ICC over workplace deaths and indenture contracts.

### Relationship with Corsairs
While megacorporations sponsor Corsairs to protect their property, **U2 sponsors Corsairs to protect the people who built it**. By pooling small contributions from millions of dockworkers, miners, and mechanics, U2 holds multiple voting seats on the Corsair Sponsor Board, ensuring that working-class operatives have a path into the ranks without bowing to a corporate boardroom.

Whenever a corporate mining outpost suffers a suspicious "geological collapse" right after workers report a safety violation, whenever a rogue freight cartel locks a crew inside a radiation-leaking hull to save salvage fees, or whenever corporate Pinkertons hire underworld thugs to ambush union organizers in the Cloudspire ravines, U2 issues **Worker Protection & Grievance Petitions**. Corsairs taking U2 contracts rarely get paid in luxury yachts, but they receive honest union credits, free priority overhauls and discounted parts at every union-run drydock in the Sphere, and the priceless operational advantage that wherever they land, the local bartender, crane operator, and fuel-line mechanic is quietly on their side.

## 5. The Interplanetary Relief Organization (IPRO)

### Civic Profile & Institutional Ethos
Space is an unforgiving neighbor, and war makes it infinitely worse. When a corporate ice-hauler suffers a catastrophic fusion containment breach in deep orbit, when a seasonal typhoon tears a working-class reef flotilla loose on Pelagia, or when a continent-sized alien dreadnought crashes into Landfall's crust and leaves hundreds of thousands of frontier citizens choking on ash and stray radiation, local municipal clinics are overwhelmed in minutes.

Enter the **Interplanetary Relief Organization (IPRO)**—the premier neutral humanitarian, disaster-triage, and medical relief agency of the Sphere. Originally founded as an ad-hoc charity coalition during the **Inter-Binary Famine of 175 AA**, IPRO was transformed into a permanent, system-spanning institution during the twenty-year **Frontier War** in Wanabo and the catastrophic aftermath of the ***Evergaol* Crash in 508 AA**.

Operating under the universally recognized emblem of the **Amber Lifeline on Stark White**, IPRO’s volunteer surgeons, trauma medics, radiation-scrubbing engineers, and relief pilots fly directly into the worst places in the heavens. Their charter is governed by the *Oath of the Open Airlock*: IPRO treats the bleeding, feeds the starving, and evacuates the stranded regardless of lineage, planetary citizenship, corporate employment, or criminal record. During the height of the Frontier War, IPRO field hospitals on Kazahn famously stitched up wounded Flotilla marines in one tent and stabilized abandoned Orkish conscripts in the next, staring down armed commanders from both sides who tried to cross the triage line.

### Leadership & Internal Power Structure
IPRO is administered by the **Humanitarian Relief Directorate**, headquartered aboard **Mercy Anchorage**—a massive, rotating orbital medical station moored in high Landfall orbit directly above the Evergaol Exclusion Zone, supported by forward logistics depots at Cloudspire and the Wanabo border. Funded through a combination of Colonial Forum humanitarian grants, charitable endowments from Crossroads guilds, U2 worker donations, and mandatory disaster-insurance levies paid by commercial shipping lines, IPRO guards its political neutrality with ferocious vigilance.

The Directorate is led by **Director-General Dr. Seleni Vash**, a tireless, sharp-voiced Naga trauma surgeon who spent fifteen years operating in zero-g combat hospital ships along the Wanabo front. Dr. Vash is famous across three systems for having zero fear of anyone wearing medals or carrying a briefcase; she has publicly shamed Pelagian Land Barons into donating freshwater barges on live comms feeds and once threatened to quarantine a Flotilla Admiral's flagship for failing a sanitation inspection.

### Jurisdiction, Operations & Signature Assets
Under an annex to the Skyline Accords, vessels and field stations bearing the verified transponders and white-and-amber livery of IPRO enjoy protected diplomatic and humanitarian immunity across all signatory space. By strict internal charter, **no IPRO vessel or facility may mount offensive naval weaponry** or carry military ordnance.

* **The White Hull Mercy Fleet:** A flotilla of converted bulk transports and modular hospital ships painted in high-visibility stark white with amber emergency strobes, led by the 400-meter flagship trauma vessel ***HMS Solace***, equipped with spin-gravity surgical rings and hyperbaric burn wards.
* **Rapid-Deploy Triage Domes:** Drop-capable, self-pressurizing inflatable medical habitats that can be parachuted into planetary disaster zones or bolted onto breached space stations within minutes of arrival.
* **Mobile Scrubbing & Decontamination Barges:** Heavy engineering vessels deployed around the Evergaol Exclusion Zone and war-torn Kazahn basins to filter heavy-metal fallout, toxic propellant spills, and Precursor energetic contamination from local water tables.
* **The Displaced Persons & Missing Spacers Registry:** The central humanitarian clearinghouse that tracks war refugees, disaster survivors, and lost crewmen across Fume, X'ara, and Wanabo.

### Relationship with Corsairs
In a polite universe, the Amber Lifeline painted on the hull of an unarmed hospital ship would be all the armor a doctor ever needed. In the actual Sphere of 518 AA, Scav raider clans cannot read interplanetary treaties, desperate Jailer gangs view radiation-scrubbing barges as free salvage, and underworld syndicates like the Iron Fang see a medical transport loaded with surgical-grade anesthetics, synthetic blood plasma, and refined fusion fuel as the easiest payday in the drift.

Because IPRO's charter strictly forbids its own ships and personnel from carrying heavy weapons, **IPRO relies heavily on chartered Corsair cells as its armed shield and rapid-extraction vanguard**. As an accredited NGO Sponsor on the Corsair Board, IPRO regularly issues **Mercy Convoy Escort Writs**, **Hot-Zone Medical Extraction Petitions**, and **Contraband Recovery Warrants** when pirate cartels hijack vaccine or food-relief shipments bound for frontier clinics. IPRO cannot offer corporate executive bonuses, but they pay reliable universal credits, provide free top-tier surgical trauma care and cybernetic rehabilitation at any Mercy Fleet berth, and give even the most cynical, battle-scarred Corsair crew the rare satisfaction of knowing that, for at least one thirty-hour cycle, they were undeniably the good guys.
