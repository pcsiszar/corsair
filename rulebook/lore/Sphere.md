# The Sphere: An Engineered Solar System

The Sphere is an engineered solar system consisting of 156 habitable worlds. Centered around a stable Orange Dwarf star,
the system utilizes complex gravitational mechanics to maintain orbital stability across multiple planetary rings.

## The Hearth: The Primary

The central star is a stable Orange Dwarf. Its light is shifted toward the amber spectrum, providing consistent solar
radiation to the habitable zones. Because it is cooler than a yellow sun, the habitable rings are positioned closer to
its surface.

## The Three Rings

The habitable space is organized into three concentric rings. To maintain orbital stability, the density of planets
increases in the outer orbits:

The Inner Ring (0.5 AU): Contains 6 Gas Giants. Orbital period: 120 days.

The Middle Ring (0.75 AU): Contains 8 Gas Giants. Orbital period: 240 days.

The Outer Ring (1.1 AU): Contains 12 Gas Giants. Orbital period: 480 days.

## The Resonance Chain

The rings are locked into a Laplace Resonance with exact orbital ratios (4:2:1). For every one orbit of the Outer Ring,
the Mid Ring completes two orbits, and the Inner Ring completes four. This mathematical synchronization prevents
gravitational interference and maintains orbital paths.

## The Anchors: Gas Giants

The 26 Gas Giants serve as the primary gravitational hubs of the Sphere. They provide magnetic shielding for their moons
and stabilize the smaller worlds within their respective rings.

## The Binary Pairs: The Sister Worlds

The majority of habitable worlds exist as Binary Pairs—two Earth-sized planets orbiting a shared center of gravity,
which in turn orbits a Gas Giant. These worlds are tidally locked at a distance of 40,000 km, meaning they face each
other permanently. Every 30 hours, the planets align with the sun, causing a total solar eclipse known as the Daily
Shadow. The constant gravitational pull results in a Permanent Tidal Bulge, leading to flooded conditions on the
inner-facing hemisphere and shallower seas on the outer-facing hemisphere.

## The Trojans

Trojan Worlds occupy the L4 and L5 Lagrange points of each Gas Giant, positioned 60 degrees ahead and behind in the same
orbit. These worlds generally lack a binary partner.

## The Watchers

A limited number of worlds occupy Polar Orbits, traveling vertically relative to the system's ecliptic plane. From their
orbital apex, the entire 156-world system is visible.
