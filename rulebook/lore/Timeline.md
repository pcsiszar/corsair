# Timeline of the Reborn Era

### The Waking Era

The history of the Reborn does not begin in a gleaming city, but in the raw, untamed wilderness of Genesis Prime. The
entity known as the Shepard awoke the First Generation from their stasis pods in a simple clearing, surrounded by
supplies and basic tools. He walked among them not as a god, but as "The Architect," a weathered and knowledgeable elder
who offered guidance rather than commandments. Under his tutelage, the Reborn learned to fell trees, quarry stone, and
forge metal. They built "Cradle" stone by stone, a labor of sweat and solidarity that forged a deep bond between the
diverse species.

There were no drones to pamper them, no fabricated history to lull them into complacency. Every building in Cradle was
raised by Reborn hands, every law debated in their councils. The Architect taught them how to survive, how to govern,
and how to resolve their differences, but he always stopped short of doing the work for them. He was a teacher who
refused to hold the chalk, insisting that his students find the answers themselves.

### The Warden Blockade

Generations passed, and Cradle grew from a settlement into a thriving metropolis. The Reborn looked to the sky, their
ambitions outgrowing the horizon. But when they launched their first unmanned orbital probe, a precision energy beam
swatted it from the heavens. Simultaneously, the ground shook as the "Wardens"—colossal, worm-like robotic
constructs—emerged from the earth. These machines did not attack the city. They simply coiled around the launch sites
and patrolled the upper atmosphere, enforcing a strict planetary quarantine.

The Wardens were a silent wall. They ignored ground vehicles and pedestrians, only reacting to attempts at spaceflight.
The Architect, when pressed for answers, simply pointed to the wilderness, hinting that the keys to the sky lay buried
beneath the earth in ancient Vaults.

### The Proving and the Drip-Feed

The Reborn formed small, specialized teams to infiltrate these underground Vaults. These "Worm Hunters" found that the
Vaults were defended by complex logic puzzles, non-lethal containment systems, and tactical challenges. It was a
dangerous game, but one designed to teach rather than kill.

With every Vault breached and every command node disabled, the Hunters uncovered fragments of data—encrypted logs and
video files. These were not grand speeches, but pieces of a puzzle. One Vault contained records of a lost
galaxy-spanning civilization. Another held the schematics for the Reborn's own genetic codes. The Architect confirmed
the authenticity of these finds but offered no further context, forcing the Reborn to piece together their own history.
They learned of the Singularity, of the destruction of their ancestors, and of their own nature as salvaged
consciousnesses given a second chance.

### The Vanishing

The final piece of the puzzle lay in the Omega Vault, deep beneath the planet's crust. When the Worm Hunters finally
breached its core and uploaded the last command code, the Wardens across the planet powered down in unison, their eyes
dimming as they returned to the earth.

The Reborn rushed to the Architect's dwelling to celebrate their victory and demand the final answers, but they found it
empty. There was no note, no grand farewell, and no magical ascension. The Architect had simply vanished. His work was
done; his students had surpassed the master. He left them with the keys to the stars and the truth of their origins,
trusting that they were now strong enough to forge their own path without his hand on the tiller.

### The Slow Drift

The Architect's disappearance left a void, but not immediate chaos. The unity forged during the construction of Cradle
and the Proving years held strong for a time. The Reborn expanded into the solar system, colonizing the other planets
and moons using the technology mastered in the Vaults.

However, as the memory of the Architect faded into legend, the old unity began to fray. Without his mediating presence,
philosophical differences that had once been settled over dinner began to harden into dogma. It took generations, but
eventually, the descendants of the Reborn drifted apart. The **Harmonists**, who revered the Architect's memory and
sought to preserve Cradle, clashed with the expansionist **Seekers**, who believed their destiny lay in conquering the
void. These rifts would eventually widen into the chasms that separate the modern Starguilds and Factions, but the seeds
were sown in the silence left by the Shepard's departure.
