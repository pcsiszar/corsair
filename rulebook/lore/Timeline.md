## Timeline of Newcomer Civilization

This chronicle documents the recorded history of Newcomer civilization across the Sphere, from the arrival of the four ancestral seed arks on Landfall to the present day in 518 AA.

---

### Era I: The Era of Genesis & The Primary Pair (0 AA – 60 AA)

#### ? BA — The Great Exodus

According to curated ship archives and sacred oral tradition, the four ancestral lineages—Humanity, Naga, Craggen, and Sylvan—fled catastrophic stellar collapse in their home systems aboard four colossal automated seed arks. Guided across the lightless interstellar void by automated navigational systems, these massive colony vessels safeguarded the genetic repositories, cultural libraries, philosophies, and scientific knowledge of their dying homeworlds, seeking a habitable system where civilization could begin anew.

#### -4 BA — Silent Touchdown in Crossroads Valley

Under sub-light automated navigation, the four arks entered the atmosphere of the temperate moon **Landfall** and touched down silently within a single verdant, sheltered valley. While the hulls rested on alien soil, the ships' automated nurseries remained sealed; the onboard autotutors were programmed to keep their adolescent charges sequestered within simulated biodomes until they reached full biological and social maturity at age twenty.

#### -3 to -1 BA — The Clandestine Breakouts

Confinement proved impossible against restless, inquisitive youth. Bored by looping historical lectures, synthetic nutrition paste, and sterile nursery corridors, teenage troublemakers (aged 14–16) began prying open ventilation shafts, overriding maintenance bulkheads, and slipping out into the misty valley under the cover of night.

In the untamed alien ferns of the valley floor, runaway humans, nagas, craggens, and sylvans collided. Although their databanks had never disclosed the existence of the other three hulls resting mere kilometers away, they discovered to their shock that their autotutors had taught them all the same engineered Common Tongue. 

Waiting for them in the valley was **Goldheart**, a gentle, solitary young dragon who nested among the crags. Rather than alarming the ships' security sensors or herding the runaways back into confinement, Goldheart became their secret guardian, protector, and older brother. He shielded the teenagers from Landfall's native predators, taught them to avoid natural hazards, and concealed their illicit excursions from the arks' external scanners. Around clandestine campfires beneath the dragon's folded wings, the teenagers traded smuggled rations, shared homeworld myths, and forged a fierce cross-species camaraderie that transcended the sterile boundaries of their individual ships.

#### 0 AA — Graduation Day

The automated gestation and education cycles reached scheduled completion. Ship sirens wailed, floodlights activated, and the primary nursery airlocks swung wide to formally release **Cohort Zero**—an initial generation of young adults (aged 18–20) meant to step onto an alien moon for the first time. 

The ship autotutors anticipated bewildered, solemn survivors clutching ancestral archives. Instead, the breakout veterans burst back into their respective arks, laughing and dragging their wide-eyed, obedient peers out into the sunlight. Rather than four frightened alien factions encountering each other in defensive isolation, the cohorts stepped into a valley where shared jokes, deep friendships, rivalries, and first loves had already been quietly flourishing for three years. Goldheart greeted the assembled multitudes, playfully reinforcing the beloved cultural belief that he had pulled their drifting ships from the void and watched over them.

#### 1 AA — Foundation of Crossroads

Cohort Zero began building their first permanent settlement in the valley where the four arks landed. Naming it **Crossroads**, they constructed low-slung, rustic dwellings from local stone, timber, and salvaged ark hulls. Influenced by Goldheart's gentle warnings to avoid high-altitude electromagnetic emissions that might draw unknown dangers from the void, the settlement established an intentionally horizontal footprint that eschewed massive vertical spires. The four lineages formed a provisional council, electing one representative from each ark.

#### 6 AA — Birth of Integrationism

The birth of the first cross-lineage child sparked a profound cultural transformation. Natural romances, having taken root during the secret midnight gatherings of the breakout years, blossomed openly across Crossroads. This gave rise to **Integrationism**—a socio-political movement advocating total cultural synthesis, mutual adaptation, and shared destiny as the moral core of the Newcomer civilization.

#### 14 AA — The First Vault & The Covenant

Expeditions venturing into Landfall’s crags discovered the First Precursor Vault—a humming subterranean complex of crystalline lattices. The anomaly attracted **Stormdrinker**, a massive dragon roosting in the violent electrical storms of Landfall's binary twin, **Nimbus**. Driven by an insatiable curiosity for ancient technology and energy phenomena, Stormdrinker descended upon the site. 

Goldheart stepped in to mediate, forging an unprecedented alliance between mortals and dragons: the Newcomers provided the nimble hands and fine tools to navigate human-sized maintenance conduits, while Stormdrinker and Goldheart provided the sensory depth and brute energy to crack heavy exterior bulkheads. 

Inside the Vault, the expedition leaders encountered active automated defenses and energetic systems of incomprehensible power. Recognizing that reckless tampering with Precursor ruins or broadcasting energetic distress signals into open space could trigger devastating planetary catastrophes, the expedition leaders, council elders, Goldheart, and Stormdrinker swore an eternal oath. They formed **The Covenant**: a classified circle dedicated to containing dangerous technologies, sanitizing active Vault sites, and shielding the young civilization from cosmic hazards.

#### 15 AA — The Preservationist Counter-Movement

As Integrationism swept the urban heart of Crossroads, an ideological counter-current emerged. Led primarily by the "dutiful students" of Cohort Zero—those who had remained inside the arks until Graduation Day, absorbing the unadulterated poetry, tragedy, and philosophies of Earth, the Naga swarms, the Craggen deep-hives, and the Sylvan groves—these traditionalists viewed the freewheeling hybrid culture as an irreverent betrayal of their sacred ancestral heritage. Adopting the philosophy of **Preservationism**, they began migrating toward Landfall's outward-facing hemisphere to establish insular, single-lineage settlements dedicated to keeping their bloodlines and traditions pure.

#### 35 AA — The Nimbus Rendezvous

Guided by data shared through The Covenant, researchers at the Sphere Research Center (SRC) constructed Landfall’s first deep-space manned rocket. Commanded by a daring multi-species crew, the vessel braved the turbulent electrical storm-wells of Nimbus to rendezvous with Stormdrinker at his high-altitude roost. In exchange for historical texts, mathematical proofs, and cultural data, Stormdrinker allowed SRC physicists to study his unique bio-fusion biology and gravitic flight mechanics.

#### 38 AA — The Draco Fusion Breakthrough

Synthesizing telemetry from Stormdrinker’s biological dynamo with Precursor magnetic shielding principles, the SRC developed the **Draco Fusion Drive**. Operating on continuous-thrust fusion physics, the drive made rapid sub-light interplanetary transit safe, reliable, and commercially viable.

#### 39 AA — Maiden Flight of the *Birdy*

The transport vessel *Birdy*, equipped with the prototype Draco Drive, successfully crossed the 61,000-kilometer binary gap between Landfall and Nimbus in under two hours, formally opening the space age for Newcomer civilization.

#### 45 AA — The Rise of Cloudspire

A permanent research outpost on the high-altitude arid plateaus of Nimbus rapidly expanded into a metropolis of gleaming towers, neon arrays, and lightning catchers: **Cloudspire**. Stormdrinker became the public patron of the city, revered by citizens as the "Lightning Sage," circling the upper atmosphere above the clouds while maintaining quiet council with Covenant leaders in restricted subterranean bays.

### Era II: The Trojan Expansion & The Great Beasts (60 AA – 200 AA)

#### 62 AA — Waypoint Station at L1

Civil engineering reached a monumental milestone with the deployment of **Waypoint Station** at the gravitational L1 null-point, precisely 30,500 kilometers between Landfall and Nimbus. Waypoint served as the primary customs terminal, logistics clearinghouse, and data relay coordinating transit across the primary pair.

#### 80–110 AA — The Binary Century

Crossroads and Cloudspire matured into dense, industrial city-states. The facing hemispheres of both moons became the brilliant, hyper-connected heart of Integrationist civilization. Meanwhile, corporate charters and Preservationist settlements dominated the outward hemispheres, funding private prospecting into the wider Fume system.

#### 120 AA — The Rush to the L4 Trojans

With the primary pair heavily colonized, ambitious mining conglomerates and independent pioneers looked outward to Fume's co-orbital L4 Lagrange point, 800,000 kilometers ahead. Colony fleets launched toward the oceanic water-world of **Pelagia** and the hyper-arid, mineral-rich desert moon of **Suncatcher**.

#### 125 AA — The Ballistic Logistics Corridors

Because neither Trojan world was self-sustaining alone, an audacious engineering marvel was established: massive planetary railguns were constructed on the equatorial island chains of Pelagia to launch sealed water-ice canisters across the 61,000-kilometer gap directly into Suncatcher’s catchment basins. In return, Suncatcher fired heavy ores and refined structural metals back to Pelagia.

#### 130–150 AA — Rise of the Trojan Megacities

* **Port Azure** on Pelagia evolved from a humble aquatic landing pad into a sprawling network of floating pontoon arcologies, dominated by shipping dynasties and maritime fishing syndicates.
* **Dustwall** on Suncatcher became the iron-bound capital of heavy industry and smelting, surrounded by the untamed, canyon-cut expanse known as the **Badlands**.

#### 165 AA — Awakening of the Sandwyrm

Heavy deep-core tectonic fracking in Suncatcher's Badlands awakened a gargantuan subterranean predator: the **Sandwyrm**. A colossal serpentine beast of terrifying proportions, the creature began swallowing mining crawlers, drilling platforms, and frontier rail lines whole, turning the Badlands into a lethal wasteland.

#### 170 AA — The Shadow of the Abyss

Deep-sea acoustic hydrophones deployed along Pelagia's oceanic trenches detected an ultra-massive biological entity slumbering in the abyss: the **Leviathan**. Whispers spread through maritime crews and corporate corridors of an ancient, intelligent titan resting in the fathomless deep, exerting an eerie psychological presence over the oceanic world.

#### 175 AA — The Clash of the Titans & The Inter-Binary Famine

Desperate to eliminate the Sandwyrm, Suncatcher authorities re-aimed Pelagia’s planetary water railguns to execute kinetic orbital bombardments against the beast's nest. Enraged and grievously wounded, the Sandwyrm sprouted massive membranous wings, broke atmosphere, and flew across the void toward Pelagia to annihilate the railgun complexes. 

As the airborne titan tore through Pelagia's coastal defense arrays, the **Leviathan** violently breached the ocean surface. Coiling around the Sandwyrm, the abyssal dragon dragged the thrashing monster down into the black depths of the trench, drowning it. 

The collateral damage leveled Pelagia’s launch arrays. The severed water logistics corridor plunged both worlds into catastrophic drought and industrial collapse: the **Inter-Binary Famine**.

#### 180 AA — The First Flotilla Charter

With famine ravaging the Trojans and piracy surging along the disrupted shipping routes, the Landfall Council and the newly formed Trade Alliance chartered a joint emergency peacekeeper force: the **Flotilla**. Governed by a provisional Admiralty Board, the fleet mobilized all available freighters to escort emergency food and water convoys across the system.

### Era III: The Corporate Feuds & The Scavenger Wars (200 AA – 340 AA)

#### 205 AA — Resolution of the Famine & The Deep-Sea Farms

Pelagia reconstructed its launch platforms while deploying bio-engineered kelp and aquaculture farms, permanently ending the famine. However, the political divide between the central governments of the primary pair and the corporate oligarchies of the Trojans became permanently entrenched.

#### 220–255 AA — The Drift Wars

Fierce competition over helium-3 fuel and heavy isotopes around Fume’s radiation-swept inner moons sparked the **Drift Wars**. Planetary governments refused to fund the joint Flotilla, choosing instead to arm private corporate security navies. Proxy skirmishes, commerce raiding, and state-sponsored piracy turned the space lanes into a treacherous free-fire zone.

#### 270 AA — Arrival of the Scavs

Attracted by the electromagnetic clutter, radio noise, and wreckage of the Drift Wars, the **Scavs**—a brutal, nomadic alien raider species driven by an insatiable hunger for high technology and ancient relics—entered the Fume system. Operating in fast, scrap-welded strike crafts, dozens of Scav raider clans launched coordinated incursions against frontier mining outposts and orbital fuel platforms.

#### 285 AA — The Sack of Cloudspire

In the most brazen raid of the century, a massive Scav armada breached Nimbus's atmosphere. Their objective: looting the SRC's deep Precursor relic vaults and harvesting high-output power arrays. 

Stormdrinker unleashed his fury across the sky, incinerating dozens of boarding craft with plasma lightning, while Cloudspire's ground batteries fought room-by-room in the arcologies. The defenders successfully repelled the invaders, but the upper districts suffered catastrophic structural damage and several vital research vaults were ransacked.

#### 290–310 AA — The Fractured System & Redmane's Remnants

Terrified planetary governments withdrew their remaining warships into low-orbit defense grids, leaving open space completely unguarded. Piracy and Scav raids skyrocketed. Refusing to abandon the shipping lanes, veteran Flotilla commander **Velen Redmane** gathered a loyal coalition of spacers, corporate defectors, and volunteer crews to operate as an independent, non-aligned defense fleet: **Redmane’s Coalition**.

#### 320 AA — The Battle of Fume Rings

A massive Scav armada gathered within Fume's inner ring system to launch a coordinated planetary assault against Landfall. Redmane’s Coalition intercepted the raiders in a brutal, multi-day fleet engagement among the icy ring debris. Utilizing tight Brachistochrone maneuvers and point-blank railgun volleys, Redmane decisively obliterated the Scav fleet, securing the void lanes and saving the colonies from collapse.

#### 335 AA — The Skyline Accords & Creation of the Corsairs

Hailed as saviors, Redmane's coalition forced the planetary governments to the negotiating table. The landmark **Skyline Accords** were signed:
* **The Flotilla** was permanently established as the sovereign, neutral naval and peacekeeper authority over all open space, orbital lanes, and transit hubs.
* **Planetary Governments** retained absolute legal sovereignty within their atmospheric gravity wells.
* **The Corsairs**: Recognizing that criminals, smugglers, and rogue corporate agents exploit the jurisdictional seam between planetary skies and open orbit, the Flotilla authorized independent, multi-party sponsored privateer cells granted cross-jurisdictional mandates: the **Corsairs**.

### Era IV: The L5 Boundary & The Ancestral Encounter (340 AA – 450 AA)

#### 350 AA — Turn to the L5 Trojans

With Fume's primary and leading worlds stabilized, colonization fleets looked to the trailing Lagrangian pair: the dense fungal world of **Karst** and the glacial ice-world of **Rime**.

#### 365 AA — Discovery of the Lithari

Deep subterranean survey teams on Karst discovered vast bioluminescent cavern biomes inhabited by a primitive, tool-using sentient species: the tribal hunters known as the **Lithari**. 

#### 368 AA — The Non-Interference Protocol

Recognizing the moral weight of their own origin, the Council and the Flotilla ratified the **Non-Interference Protocol**. Karst was placed under permanent naval quarantine, enforced by a heavily armed Flotilla picket station at the L1 point to protect the Lithari until they achieve independent spaceflight. All colonial expansion was redirected to **Rime**, where subterranean cities were carved around its geothermally heated circular ocean.

#### 410 AA — The Inter-System Probes

Long-range autonomous survey drones burning at 1g crossed the 90-million-kilometer void to the neighboring gas giant system, **X’ara**. They returned startling telemetry: a second, engineered six-world binary track mirroring Fume's architecture, inhabited by a hyper-advanced, uniform civilization. Soon after, the worlds of Fume received an enigmatic, formal transmission inviting them to open diplomatic talks.

#### 425 AA — First Contact with the Ancestrals

A high delegation of Newcomer diplomats aboard the Flotilla cruiser *Endeavour* arrived at X'ara, meeting the **Ancestrals**. The Ancestrals presented themselves as the rightful heirs to the Sphere's architectural legacy, living under a strict theological dogma: the **Vigil of Reverent Stillness**. Governed by their venerated spiritual ruler, the **Archon**, Ancestral doctrine taught that expansion and reckless exploration were sins of hubris. The sudden arrival of noisy, multi-species, fusion-powered Newcomers shocked Ancestral society and ignited deep philosophical unease.

#### 432 AA — The Ancestral Treaty

To prevent conflict and establish a diplomatic buffer between the civilizations, the Archon and Flotilla delegates negotiated the **Ancestral Treaty**:
* **The Avon Partition**: The L4 Trojan pair of X'ara was divided. **Avon 1** remained under Ancestral sovereignty, while **Avon 2** was opened to Newcomer settlement, quickly evolving into a vibrant hub of art, trade, and cultural festivals.
* **The Avon 1 Schism**: Looking across the narrow 61,000-kilometer gap at the bustling, colorful night-skies of Avon 2, Ancestral society on Avon 1 fractured into the **Gilded Movement** (restless youth captivated by Newcomer vitality) and the **Pure Blade** (zealous military traditionalists who despised Newcomer influence).
* **The Oran Quarantine**: The sacred L5 worlds of **Oran 1 & 2** (revered by Ancestrals for their monolithic singing stones and colossal wildlife preserves) were permanently closed to industrial colonization.
* **The Flotilla Consulate**: A single, fortified consulate on the Ancestral capital world of Irul 1 was established as the sole legal port of entry into core Ancestral space.

### Era V: The Frontier War, The Evergaol, & The Present Day (450 AA – 518 AA)

#### 455 AA — Expansion into Wanabo

Pushing outward along the Middle Ring of the Sphere, Newcomer prospectors and corporate pioneers reached the third gas giant: **Wanabo**. The moon of **Kazahn** became an unregulated frontier of subterranean boomtowns, black markets, and outlaw claims, while neighboring **Karak** remained a perilous wilderness.

#### 465 AA — First Contact with the Orkish Empire

In the void beyond Wanabo, Newcomer explorers encountered the armored battle-cruisers of the **Orkish Empire**. The Orks emerged as a fiercely militaristic, conquest-driven warrior civilization hailing from the outer void. Governed by a brutal martial hierarchy of Supreme Warlords, their doctrine held that strength of arms was the only legitimate arbiter of galactic sovereignty.

#### 485–505 AA — The Great Frontier War

Tensions over Wanabo’s rich helium-3 fields boiled over into a grinding, 20-year interstellar war. The Flotilla's disciplined kinetic doctrine clashed with the massive, armored ram-prows and overwhelming assault doctrine of the Orkish Warlords. Decisive battles across the trenches of Kazahn and the orbital corridors of Wanabo ground both militaries down to near-exhaustion.

#### 508 AA — The Evergaol Incursion & The Great Last Stand

As the battered fleets of the Flotilla and the Orkish Empire clashed in the outer reaches of the Fume system, an ancient cataclysm of unknown origin awakened: the ***Evergaol***, a colossal automated super-dreadnought of ancient design, warped into the battlespace. Unaligned with either civilization and operating on lethal automated combat algorithms, the dreadnought began systematically obliterating warships from both sides.

Facing total annihilation, Flotilla battlecruisers and Orkish dreadnoughts forged an unthinkable, desperate battlefield alliance. In an earth-shattering clash across the upper atmosphere of Landfall, Flotilla railguns, Ork boarding torpedoes, **Goldheart**, and **Stormdrinker** united. The two dragons grappled directly with the dreadnought’s drive arrays, tearing open its heat manifolds and allowing combined kinetic salvos to break its orbital anchors. 

The burning *Evergaol* crashed violently into the surface of Landfall, gouging a continent-scale scar known as the **Evergaol Crash Site**. Boarding teams led by veteran Flotilla commanders sacrificed their lives inside the burning wreckage to disable the primary automated command matrix.

#### 509 AA — The Great Truce

Horrified by the dreadnought's destructive power and hollowed out by wartime casualties, the Flotilla Admiralty and the Orkish Empire signed **the Great Truce**. The treaty established an uneasy, heavily militarized border across Wanabo, with both sides retreating to rebuild.

#### 518 AA — The Present Day: The New Wave of Corsairs

Exactly ten years have passed since the Fall of the Evergaol:
* **The Flotilla in Crisis**: The navy is stretched to the breaking point. Its capital fleet is a fraction of its pre-war strength, struggling to decommission scarred warships and maintain patrols across Fume, X'ara, and Wanabo.
* **The Evergaol Exclusion Zone**: The crash site on Landfall remains a smoldering, highly restricted wasteland of lethal automated defenses and strange radiation, targeted by illegal Scavs, corporate black-ops units, and daring relic hunters.
* **The Smoldering Frontier**: The Orkish Empire watches the border with growing impatience, while Ancestral hawks on Avon 1 push for a preemptive strike against the weakened Newcomers.
* **The New Wave**: Unable to police the void alone, the Flotilla and colonial sponsors have thrown open the doors to a **New Wave of Corsairs**. Fresh crews—flying refitted gunboats, surplus corvettes, and salvaged hulls—are stepping forward to take on the most dangerous contracts in the Sphere, standing as the thin line between civilization and total collapse.
