# Landfall

Landfall was the very first moon in the Sphere that the Newcomers landed on circa 500 years ago. The original four races
were the Humans, the Naga, the Craggen and the Sylvan, all waking up from cryo sleep onboard their crash-landed ships.
The monuments of those early days are still very much present, chiefly amongst them is Crossroads, the very first
settlement built by the Newcomers, now the most populated city maybe in the entire Sphere.

Landfall is the most Earth-like of the colonized moons in terms of climate and biome, although with a little more
landmass that's more clustered together than the continents of old Earth. Crossroads sits at the merging point of two
rivers on the near-side of the moon, facing towards its sister, Nimbus, and very close to the landing site of the Exodus
ships the Newcomers landed on. The surrounding area has a continental climate with fields and forests, but travel far
enough beyond the edge of the continent and a short hop over the Sun Canal, a narrow sea separating the north-eastern
and south-eastern continents; you will find savannas turning into lush rainforests the further you go. Follow the
rivers of Crossroads to the north instead, and you will find mountain ranges that span almost to the North Pole.

The western continent sits on the far side of the moon and is about the same size as its eastern counterpart, only it
is offset to the south by a huge margin. The largest city there is Settler's Bay, established 60 years after Crossroads.
Even to this day, a large segment of both continents is designated as protected wildlife reserves, although the
expansion of civilization already left a noticeable footprint on the land.

The flora and fauna has its unique quirks, such as a large majority of plants evolving to have purple or red-colored
leaves and stems when, and green seems to be a rarity, except amongst flower colors. The fauna is also rather
interesting where in the sense that even its larger predators are completely neutral if not friendly towards the
Newcomers, unless they are provoked.

Crossroads itself is mostly flat, bar a couple of truly monumental buildings such as the Crossroads Council Hall, the
Sphere Reseach Center, and the Spaceport. Most of it's architecture is rather rural and a mix of high-tech small prefabs
and diverse cultural decorative pieces. You can find paved roads, flowerbeds, trees and decorative flags strewn above
the narrow streets of the city but with the actual buildings having insulation made of modern composite materials,
although frequently covered with wood, plants, or simply painted over. Transportation is mostly underground, with trams
lines spanning the entirety of the settlement's area.

500 years spent together made Crossroads one of the most culturally diverse and tolerant places in the Sphere, also
helped by Unitarianism, a semi-religious movement that believes that original four must have been created, or at least
influenced by some greater being early on in their history, hence the ability to procreate with each other and the
ability to find a common tongue so easily. Crossroads has the highest number of cultural holidays and festivities in the
Sphere too, rivaled only by Irul'loa, managing to find the time and place for all the races to maintain their
traditions.

Most megacorps also maintain facilities here, if for no other reason that they started out from here. Vandal's Workshop
and Redfeather Aerospace probably being the two most notable. Megacorps on Landfall are directed to operate in dedicated
Industrial Zones some distance away from cities. Newer corps are inclined to build arcologies and circumvent having
their workers commute to and from the settlements, but the Crossroads Council is attempting to shut down this practice
to curb isolationist tendencies.

The Landing Site in the current day is treated more like a historic site than anything of industrial significance.
Almost every child on Landfall has at one point taken a school trip to the site and wandered the massive halls of the
Exodus Ships and browsed the archives of each to learn about their history. While much of it is preserved, the reason
why they had to leave their own planet behind still remains a mystery.

The most dangerous place on the moon is the Evergaol Exclusion Zone, the crash site of the massive mothership
that invaded the planet around 164 AL. It was a wholly alien spacecraft that unleashed thousands of machines that
abducted the Newcomers and brought them aboard the Evergaol. The spacecraft was nigh impervious to any conventional
weapon, the only way it could be brought down using a similar ancient abandoned spacecraft discovered around the same
time, the Orion. The Orion was commandeered by the Flotilla and smashed into the Evergaol from above, forcing them both
to Landfall's surface. A strike force was then dispatched to rescue the abducted Newcomers and take out whatever was in
charge. The rescue was a success, and the Evergaol was permanently disabled, but the area surrounding the two twisted
ships was locked down permanently. Attempts have been made to study the wrecks, but too they end too often with a
gruesome accident. Most of Landfall's criminal element is now concentrated in the Exclusion Zone for better or worse.
Some are just daring opportunists trying to score something valuable from the wreckage while others are, like the
Jailers, made the site their home.

Compared to the other moons, Landfall had a scarce few Precursor sites, and even those were picked clean by now.
Corsairs coming here either have something to do with the crash site, or they are visiting in an unofficial capacity.
Since Landfall still has the largest population of Newcomers in the Sphere, a large number of visitors are simply here
to meet friends and family, Corsairs being no exceptions.
