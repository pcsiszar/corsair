# The Hunter's Guild

Born from the crucible of the Proving, the Hunter's Guild traces its lineage directly back to the legendary "Worm
Hunters" of Genesis Prime. These were the elite teams who first dared to venture into the wilderness, facing down the
colossal, robotic Wardens to breach the ancient Vaults. Their legacy is one of courage, ingenuity, and a deep
understanding of what it takes to survive in the most hostile environments in the galaxy.

The Hunter's Guild is not a traditional Starguild focused on interstellar commerce, but a rugged fraternity of
explorers, monster hunters, and survivalists. They prize practical experience and time-tested designs over flashy
aesthetics. Their equipment is renowned for its durability, modularity, and effectiveness against large, dangerous
targets. The Guild operates on the fringes of settled space, often taking on contracts that other organizations deem too
perilous.

### Prominent Manufacturers of the Hunter's Guild

The manufacturers that operate under the Hunter's Guild's banner are helmed by families whose names have been synonymous
with the hunt for generations. Their products are a direct reflection of the Guild's ethos: tough, reliable, and deadly
effective.

*   **Grabowski's Garage:**
    *   **Specialization:** All-Terrain Vehicles, Heavy-Duty Industrial Equipment, and Vehicle Customization.
    *   **Description:** "If it can't crawl over a mountain, it ain't a Grabowski." Originally a small workshop that
      retrofitted ground-crawlers for the first Worm Hunts, Grabowski's Garage has become the premier name in rugged,
      all-terrain vehicles. Their designs emphasize powerful engines, reinforced chassis, and modular hardpoints,
      allowing for extensive field customization to tackle any environment.

*   **Panthera Arms:**
    *   **Specialization:** High-Caliber Firearms, Anti-Materiel Rifles, and Large-Game Melee Weapons.
    *   **Description:** Named after the apex predators of Old Earth, Panthera Arms creates weapons designed to take down
      threats far larger and tougher than a humanoid. They are masters of kinetic energy transfer, producing firearms
      with exceptional stopping power and melee weapons perfectly weighted and balanced for severing thick hides and
      armored plates.

*   **Hawken's Shipyard:**
    *   **Specialization:** Long-Range Exploration Vessels, Patrol Craft, and Starship Combat Systems.
    *   **Description:** While other guilds were building trade fleets, the Hawken family was designing ships that could
      operate for months in deep space, hunt pirates, and survive encounters with cosmic horrors. Hawken ships are known
      for their powerful sensor suites, redundant systems, and weapon hardpoints designed for spinal-mount cannons and
      heavy torpedoes, making them formidable combat and exploration platforms.

# The Brightstar Conglomerate

While other guilds trade in raw materials or weapons of war, the Brightstar Conglomerate trades in a far more potent commodity: information. As the galaxy's largest media and communications empire, Brightstar controls everything from the interstellar news networks to the most popular virtual entertainment. They believe that perception is reality, and they have mastered the art of shaping it. Their influence is wielded not with armies, but with carefully crafted stories, galaxy-wide broadcasts, and the allure of celebrity.

Their products are synonymous with luxury, cutting-edge technology, and popular culture. To own a Brightstar device is to be connected to the pulse of the galaxy, and to be seen with their luxury goods is a symbol of status and modern sophistication.

### Prominent Manufacturers of the Brightstar Conglomerate

The manufacturers under the Brightstar umbrella are trendsetters and technological innovators, creating the products that define the modern, connected lifestyle.

*   **Aurelian Designs:**
    *   **Specialization:** Luxury Personal Apparel, High-Fashion Smart-Devices, and Signature Star-Yachts.
    *   **Description:** Aurelian Designs sets the trends that the rest of the galaxy follows. Their clothing lines are worn by celebrities and holo-stars, their personal comms are sleek works of art, and their limited-run star-yachts are the ultimate symbol of having 'arrived'. Their products are often seen in major media productions, further cementing their desirability.

*   **Lucent Technologies:**
    *   **Specialization:** Holographic Communication Systems, Consumer-Grade Cybernetics, and Entertainment Technology.
    *   **Description:** Lucent brings the future to the masses. They are the creators of the ubiquitous 'Holo-Net' that connects the galaxy and pioneers of the immersive virtual realities offered by Prism Entertainment. Their line of 'Lumen' cybernetics focuses on aesthetic and social enhancements, popular among public figures and media personalities.

*   **Prism Entertainment:**
    *   **Specialization:** Interactive Media, Virtual Reality Experiences, and Galaxy-Wide News Broadcasting.
    *   **Description:** Prism Entertainment is the heart of the Conglomerate's media empire. They produce the most-watched news network, 'The Spectrum', as well as the most popular sports leagues, dramas, and immersive VR games in the known universe. They don't just report on the culture; they create it.

# The Foundry

If the Hunter's Guild is the sharp edge of the spear, The Foundry is the unyielding haft. Their origins are as old as Cradle itself, rooted in the collective of architects, engineers, and laborers who raised the first city from the dust of Genesis Prime. The Foundry's philosophy is simple: build to last. They are the master crafters of the galaxy, responsible for the vast cityscapes, colossal terraforming projects, and the industrial backbone that supports interstellar civilization.

Where other guilds specialize in precision or luxury, The Foundry specializes in scale. They are the mass producers for the masses, creating the reliable, workhorse equipment that builds and sustains colonies across the galaxy. Their designs are utilitarian, robust, and easily repaired, embodying the spirit of communal effort and relentless progress.

### Prominent Manufacturers of The Foundry

The manufacturing divisions of The Foundry are industrial titans, capable of undertaking projects of staggering scope and complexity. Their brand is a guarantee of industrial-grade quality and endurance.

*   **Keystone Prefabs:**
    *   **Specialization:** Modular Colony Habitats, Atmospheric Processors, and Mass-Produced Survival Gear.
    *   **Description:** Keystone is the reason new colonies survive their critical first years. They produce entire settlements in flat-pack, ready-to-assemble formats, from living quarters and hydroponic farms to water purifiers and power generators. A Keystone habitat is the first building raised on a hundred new worlds.

*   **Argo Cargo Solutions:**
    *   **Specialization:** Heavy Cargo Freighters, Interplanetary Haulers, and Orbital Construction Platforms.
    *   **Description:** Argo builds the behemoths that are the lifeblood of interstellar trade. Their ships are designed for one purpose: to move massive amounts of material as efficiently and reliably as possible. While slow and unwieldy, an Argo freighter is a symbol of civilization's reach, capable of carrying the resources needed to build or sustain a world.

*   **TerraForm Industries:**
    *   **Specialization:** Planetary-Scale Engineering, Terraforming Equipment, and Heavy Construction Vehicles.
    *   **Description:** When a planet needs to be reshaped, TerraForm Industries gets the call. They design and build the colossal atmospheric regulators, orbital mirrors, and seismic dampeners that can turn a barren rock into a habitable world over decades. Their massive construction vehicles are a common sight at any large-scale planetary engineering project.
