## The Corsairs of Fume

In the engineered vastness of the **Fume** system, where four habitable moons drift in a delicate mechanical dance
around their gas giant, the line between planetary law and the void of space is a constant source of friction. To
prevent total bureaucratic paralysis or open conflict between the **Flotilla** and the **Sovereign Moons**, the *
*Corsair** system was established.

Corsairs are the elite "problem solvers" of the system—individuals granted a rare, cross-jurisdictional mandate to
operate where others cannot.

## The Corsair Cell: The Fundamental Unit

The Corsair system does not operate as a massive, bureaucratic army. Instead, its power is decentralized into the
*Corsair Cell*—the fundamental building block of the entire organization. These are small, highly autonomous groups of
**3 to 5 specialists** living and working aboard a single, high-performance vessel.

The small scale is a deliberate strategic choice: a single ship is small enough to land in a crowded **Raindrop**
spaceport or dock at a **Skyreach** refinery without being perceived as a military invasion, yet versatile enough to
handle high-stakes investigations that would normally be bogged down by interplanetary red tape.

### The Collaborative Cell Structure

To ensure that no single power can turn a Cell into a private "hit squad," every team is a mandatory collaboration of
different **Sponsors**. This diversified makeup ensures a "self-policing" environment; because the members report back
to different interests, any attempt to use the Cell for an unfair advantage is immediately flagged by the others.

### The Vessel and the Mandate

The ship itself serves as more than just transport; it is a mobile embassy. While in the void, it follows the Flotilla’s
safety protocols, but the moment the ramp drops on a planetary surface, the Cell transitions seamlessly into the role of
local law enforcement.

This structure makes the Corsair Cell the only entity in the system capable of speaking for the Flotilla and the Moons
simultaneously. They are the ultimate scalpels in a universe of sledgehammers, solving the problems that "bleed" across
the line between the ground and the stars.
