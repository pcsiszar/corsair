# The Corsairs

In the engineered vastness of **the Sphere**, where two hundred habitable worlds drift in a delicate mechanical dance,
the line between planetary law and the void of space is a constant source of friction. To prevent total bureaucratic
paralysis or open conflict between the **Flotilla** and the **Sovereign Planets**, the **Corsair** system was
established.

Corsairs are the elite "problem solvers" of the system—individuals granted a rare, cross-jurisdictional mandate to
operate where others cannot.

---

## The Mandate of the Corsair

A Corsair is a legal anomaly. While a Flotilla officer must halt their pursuit at a planet’s clouds and a planetary
sheriff cannot legally board a starship in high orbit, a Corsair belongs to both worlds—and neither.

* **The Atmospheric Pass:** Corsairs hold the unique legal right to transition from the vacuum of space to the soil of a
  planet without a change in authority.
* **The Shared Trust:** They are the only agents in the Sphere permitted to carry out investigations, enforcement, and
  diplomatic mediation that traverse the "Cloud Line."
* **Neutral Intermediaries:** Because they are not strictly part of the Flotilla’s military hierarchy or any single
  planet’s government, they are often used as neutral third parties to resolve disputes that would otherwise escalate
  into interplanetary incidents.

---

## The Sponsorship System

A Corsair’s authority is not granted by birthright or simple enlistment; it is **sponsored**. Every Corsair cell is a
collaborative creation, funded and recognized by a consortium of powerful entities within the Sphere.

* **Selection by Merit:** Candidates are drawn from a pool of the Sphere’s most capable individuals. These are experts
  in their fields—veteran explorers, master tacticians, brilliant scientists, or legendary pilots—who have demonstrated
  a level of skill that transcends local interests.
* **The Collaborative Cell:** To ensure balance, a Corsair cell is composed of individuals suggested by different
  sponsors. This prevents any single planet or corporation from turning a cell into their private "hit squad."
* **The Investment:** Sponsors provide the essential resources: the high-performance vessels capable of atmospheric
  entry, specialized gear, and the diplomatic credentials required to bypass local blockades.

---

## The Burden of Representation

While a Corsair enjoys immense freedom, their position is a "High-Stakes Privilege." They are held to a standard of
conduct far above that of a standard soldier or citizen.

* **The Shadow of Debt:** Under normal operations, a Corsair is a respected representative. However, the sponsorship is
  a conditional agreement. If a Corsair "steps out of line"—violating the neutrality of the Sphere, causing unnecessary
  diplomatic damage, or failing their mandate—sponsors may revoke their support.
* **The Revocation:** A Corsair without a sponsor is a person without a home. Revocation converts the cost of their
  ship, gear, and past operations into a crushing personal debt. More importantly, it strips them of their
  jurisdictional immunity, leaving them vulnerable to the very laws they used to bypass.
* **The Cycle of Service:** For most, being a Corsair is the pinnacle of a career—a chance to operate with total
  autonomy in the service of the Sphere’s stability. For the sponsors, it is a way to ensure that even the most
  difficult problems have a solution that doesn't involve a war.

---

## Types of Sponsors

Sponsors do not nominate candidates out of charity; they do so to gain a scalpel in a universe of sledgehammers. Because
a Corsair cell can legally cross the atmosphere line, they are the only entities capable of solving problems that "
bleed" from one jurisdiction into another.

Below are the primary sponsors of the Sphere and the specific nature of their involvement with the Corsair system.

---

### 1. The Flotilla

The Flotilla is the largest single sponsor, providing the backbone of the Corsair fleet’s orbital infrastructure and
long-range communications.

* **The Motivation:** The Flotilla’s mandate is the protection of humanity from outside threats and the maintenance of
  space-traffic safety. However, they are legally barred from setting foot on a planet. They sponsor Corsairs to act as
  their "Surface Investigators."
* **The Jobs:**
    * **Tracking Orbital Contraband:** When illegal tech is smuggled from a station down to a planet’s
      surface, the Flotilla cannot follow. They task a Corsair cell to finish the bust.
    * **Threat Assessment:** If a planetary colony reports a potential "outside threat" (such as anomalous Architect
      tech activation), the Flotilla sends Corsairs to investigate the ground-site.

### 2. The Governments

Individual planets or planetary blocs sponsor Corsairs to extend their reach beyond their own gravity wells without
violating the Flotilla's non-interference treaties.

* **The Motivation:** A planet’s laws are useless once a criminal reaches orbit. They sponsor Corsairs to ensure that "
  Planetary Justice" has a long arm that can reach into the stars and into the territory of rival neighboring planets.
* **The Jobs:**
    * **Extradition & Fugitive Recovery:** Bringing high-profile criminals back to the planet for trial
      after they’ve fled to deep space.
    * **Sovereignty Disputes:** Mediating between two neighboring planets over resource rights in the "overlap" zone
      between their atmospheres.

### 3. The Corporations

The massive entities that manage the terraforming arrays, mining belts, and transport hubs. They sponsor Corsairs to
protect the logistical health of the Sphere.

* **The Motivation:** Corporations hate delays and "legal dead-zones." They sponsor Corsairs to bypass the red tape of
  local planetary bureaucracies when those bureaucracies threaten the flow of trade.
* **The Jobs:**
    * **Asset Recovery:** Retrieving stolen corporate data or specialized hardware.
    * **Anti-Piracy Operations:** Clearing shipping lanes of raiders that hide in the nebulous clouds of uninhabited
      planets where the Flotilla lacks the scouts to look.

### 4. The Fringe

Groups representing the collective rights of colonists, environmentalists, or planetary separatists. They sponsor
Corsairs to ensure a "moral check" on the larger powers.

* **The Motivation:** To ensure that the Flotilla and the Corporations don't trample over the rights of the smaller,
  newly settled worlds and other lesser represented groups. They want "watchdogs" with a badge.
* **The Jobs:**
    * **Colony Security:** Providing some semblance of protection to newly founded colonies outside of Flotilla
      patrolled space.
    * **Impartial Observers:** Making sure that any dealings going on between two parties of vastly unequal size and
      power proceed fairly, without the little guy being screwed over.

### 5. The Academy

The institutions dedicated to uncovering the secrets of the Sphere.

* **The Motivation:** Most Architect tech is considered a "security risk" by the Flotilla or "salvage" by the planets.
  The Consortia sponsor Corsairs to preserve these sites for study before they are destroyed or weaponized.
* **The Jobs:**
    * **Anomaly Containment:** Responding to "wake-up" signals from ancient engineered structures on
      uninhabited worlds.
    * **Tech Recovery:** Infiltrating "Dead Zones"—planets where the biosphere is too dangerous or the automated
      defenses are active—to retrieve data cores.

### 6. The Unions

The unions representing the pilots, technicians, miners and workers from all over that seek to collectively represent
themselves and put up a fight against any would be oppressor.

* **The Motivation:** To protect the workforce from being caught in the crossfire of planetary wars or corporate "
  cost-cutting" that leads to unsafe space conditions.
* **The Jobs:**
    * **Industrial Mediation:** Investigating "accidents" on orbital refineries to determine if they were
      actually sabotage or negligence.
    * **Search and Rescue:** Finding missing crews in deep space or on hostile planetary surfaces where official
      Flotilla rescue missions are deemed "too expensive" or "too risky."
