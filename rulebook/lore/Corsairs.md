## The Corsairs of the Sphere

The Corsair system was established to manage jurisdictional friction between the **Flotilla** and
the **Sovereign Moons**. It provides a mechanism for cross-jurisdictional operations in cases where local laws and
orbital regulations overlap.

Corsairs are specialists granted a cross-jurisdictional mandate to operate in both planetary and orbital environments.

## The Corsair Cell: The Fundamental Unit

The Corsair system is decentralized and operates through the **Corsair Cell**. Each cell is a small, autonomous group of
**3 to 5 specialists** based on a single, high-performance vessel.

This small-scale structure is a strategic choice: a single vessel can operate in crowded spaceports like **Raindrop**,
dock at **Skyreach** refineries, or land at fringe outposts on **Kazahn** without the political implications of a
military deployment. This versatility allows cells to handle investigations and tactical operations that would otherwise
be complicated by interplanetary bureaucracy.

### The Collaborative Cell Structure

To ensure neutrality, every cell is a mandatory collaboration between different **Sponsors**. This structure provides a
self-policing environment; because members represent different interests, the cell is discouraged from serving any
single power's exclusive agenda.

### The Vessel and the Mandate

The Corsair vessel serves as a mobile operations center and diplomatic platform. While in the void, it adheres to
Flotilla safety and transit protocols. Upon landing on a planetary surface, the cell transitions to a local enforcement
role under the host moon's jurisdiction.

This dual-authority structure makes the Corsair Cell the only entity capable of operating seamlessly between Flotilla
space and planetary gravity wells. They solve issues that transition between ground and space jurisdictions.
