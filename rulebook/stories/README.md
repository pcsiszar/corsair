# Stories of the Sphere

A collection of standalone short stories capturing the lived experience, cosmic absurdities, and mundane realities of life across the Sphere—from planetary municipal council chambers to deep-void pirate hulls.

---

### Anthology

1. **[A Point of Order on the Twenty-Seventh Hour](A_Point_of_Order_on_the_Twenty-Seventh_Hour.md)**
   * *Location:* Crossroads Council Hall, Landfall (The Fume Cluster)
   * *Featuring:* Speaker Jesse Haddon, Councilwoman Vaelith, Brom of the Stone-Carvers, Magistrate Ssisera, Goldheart the void dragon, and an apologetic Corsair cell.
   * *Synopsis:* On an ordinary afternoon in Crossroads, the municipal council debates a laundry-sooting shuttle tax while a celestial eclipse looms—until a runaway scrap-runner crashes through the stained-glass ceiling clutching stolen alien tech, with two breathless Corsairs dropping in hot on his heels.

2. **[The Mathematics of the Great Tithe](The_Mathematics_of_the_Great_Tithe.md)**
   * *Location:* Cargo hold of the *Rust-Weevil*, burning towards The Hoard across the Wanabo DMZ
   * *Featuring:* Skrix, Krikk, and a boarding Corsair cell.
   * *Synopsis:* Two four-armed Scav grunts sorting a raided Precursor phase-emitter, scrap copper, and canned peaches debate the holy pyramid scheme of the Great Tithe, the greed of The Collector, and the terrifying entities of the Sphere—only to learn why veteran raiders dread tiny, silent Corsairs far more than massive Ork gunships, capital battleships, or wild void dragons.
