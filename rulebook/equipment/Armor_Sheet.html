Armor & Protection Rules

Protection (Flat Damage Reduction): Subtract your total Protection value directly from incoming physical and kinetic damage before applying damage to health.

Minimum Strength (Min STR): Managing heavy ballistic plating or powered servos requires physical power. If Strength < Min STR, the armor cannot be worn or donned.

Defense Stacking: Armor Protection stacks with bonuses from Shields (+1 to +3) and Cover (+1 to +3). You can only benefit from one worn suit of armor at a time.

Damage Formula: Net Damage = Attack Damage - (Armor + Shield + Cover)

Armor Upgrade Kits

Mastercrafted armorer customization. An armor suit can receive a maximum of 3 Upgrade Kits:

Tier Market Cost Selectable Upgrade Option
1st Kit 25,000 Cr -1 Load (min 1) OR +1 Protection
2nd Kit 60,000 Cr -1 Load (min 1) OR +1 Protection
3rd Kit 120,000 Cr -1 Load (min 1) OR +1 Protection
Load reduction cannot lower armor below 1 Load. Protection increases stack.
