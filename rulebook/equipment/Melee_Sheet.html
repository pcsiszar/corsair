Dual Wielding & Modifications

Dual Wielding: Wielding two 1H weapons adds the flat Damage Bonus of both weapons to attacks. Choose leading weapon for damage type.

Upgrade Kits (Max 3): Tiered costs (10k / 25k / 50k CR). Each grants: -1 Load (min 1/2) OR +1 Flat Dmg Bonus.

Shield Rules: Requires 1H, stacks with armor Protection, cannot be dual wielded.
