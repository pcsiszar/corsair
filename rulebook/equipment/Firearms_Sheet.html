Firearms & Support Weapons

Tactical Ballistics, Accuracy Arrays, Firepower Profiles & Upgrade Systems
SYSTEM REF // A4-SEC-06

Accuracy & Range Bands

Accuracy + Finesse: Attacks with firearms are always resolved by pairing the weapon's Accuracy at the target's range band with Finesse.

Range Bands:

• Short (S): 0–5 spaces (~0–20m)

• Medium (M): 6–12 spaces (~20–40m)

• Long (L): 13–20 spaces (~40–80m)

• Extreme (E): 20+ spaces (~80m+)

Rate of Fire & Handling

Rate of Fire (RoF): Max total Crit and Chain effects spendable per attack roll.

• Heavy (RoF 0): High Dmg, no RoF Crits.

• Precision (RoF 1): Up to 1 Crit or Chain.

• Burst / Auto (RoF 2–4): Up to 2–4 Crits/Chains.

Min STR: 1H needs 2H if STR < Min STR; 2H cannot be wielded if STR < Min STR.

Firearm Upgrade Kits

Upgrade Kits (Max 3): Tiered costs (10k / 25k / 50k CR). Choice per kit applied:

• -1 Load (min 1/2 Load)

• +1 Flat Damage Bonus

• +1 Rate of Fire (RoF)

• +1 Accuracy at 1 designated range band

Dual Wielding Sidearms

1H Weapons Only: Dual wield two 1H firearms if meeting both Min STR scores.

Combined Firepower: Adds flat Damage Bonus AND Rate of Fire (RoF) of both weapons together.

Worse Accuracy: Attack test must use the worse (lower) Accuracy at target's range band.

Ranged Attack Sequence

1. Range Band: Check distance to target (S / M / L / E) for weapon's Accuracy attribute.

2. Action Roll: Roll higher attribute in pool (8+ = Hit). Spend extra Hits on Action Effects.

3. Success Roll: Roll lower attribute. Highest die = Success Points. Spend Hits on Crits (up to RoF).

4. Total Damage: Success Points + Crits (+2 each) + Weapon Flat Dmg Bonus.
