# Gear

*You can download and print the [Gear Printable Reference Sheet](Gear_Sheet.html) for play.*

Gear encompasses the broad spectrum of mission-critical tactical equipment, specialized toolkits, refillable field rigs, and survival hardware utilized by operators in the Corsair universe. While weapons and armor govern basic combat survival, gear provides the technical leverage, mobility, intelligence, and utility needed to complete dangerous boarding operations and wilderness expeditions.

Every item in this catalog is defined by its **Properties**, **Load**, **Cost**, and dedicated **Item Attributes**.

---

## Gear Rules & Resolution

1. **Dedicated Attributes & Test Pairs:** Most gear categories provide dedicated item attributes (such as *Mobility*, *Medicine*, *Sensors*, *Tinkering*, *Locksmith*, *Disguise*, *Forgery*, *Interfacing*, *Hacking*, or *Ordnance*). When activated, the item's attribute is paired with a character's core attribute to form the dice pool for the test.
2. **Action Point Costs:** Deploying, activating, or utilizing any piece of gear requires **1 Action Point (AP)**, unless explicitly noted otherwise. Passive items that require no roll (such as deploying *Cover*) still require 1 AP to deploy.
3. **Capacity & Automatic Refill:** Items with expendable charges (such as medical kits, grenade rigs, and stimulant injectors) have an innate **Capacity (Cap X)** representing their reservoir of uses. Activating the item expends 1 charge. All expended charges **automatically refill to maximum** whenever the character visits an eligible supply hub (their starship, a station clinic, armory, colony merchant, or supply depot) during **place-to-place** or **day-to-day pacing**, without spending Credits or tracking individual restock quantities.
4. **Stand-Alone Explosive Rolls:** Explosives do not pair with a character attribute. Instead, grenades and charges resolve as Area Attack **Success Tests** rolling pure **Ordnance** dice pools against spaces within range.
5. **Upgrade Compatibility:** Like weapons and armor, eligible mechanical gear and electronic devices can be enhanced with up to 3 standard Upgrade Kits to reduce Load by 1 or increase their primary attribute by +1.

### Mobility Special Properties

* **Jetpack (Xm):** Activating a jetpack costs **1 AP** and requires a **Success Test** using **Mobility + Agility**. Your flight movement is equal to the jetpack's base speed (**X meters**) plus **1 meter per Success Point**. You can move freely in three dimensions, hover, and ignore difficult terrain. You must end your round on solid ground directly beneath you unless in zero-g. You ignore all falling damage while wearing an active jetpack.
  * **Vector Boost (Crit Effect):** When resolving a jetpack's Success Roll, spending a Hit modifies the **Crit Effect** to grant an additional **+4 meters of movement** per Hit spent instead of the standard +2 Success Points.
  * **Charging:** You can spend **1 AP** to fly up to the jetpack's base movement speed in a straight line and immediately execute a **Melee Attack** at the end of the movement.
* **Surface Adhesion:** Scaling sheer, vertical, or inverted surfaces costs **1 AP** and requires a **Success Test** using **Mobility + Strength**. While scaling, you adhere securely to surfaces; base climbing movement is 8 meters plus 1 meter per Success Point.
* **Zipline (40m):** Launching the motorized cable to a solid anchor point up to 40 meters away costs **1 AP**. Once secured, any creature can traverse the cable as part of their standard Movement Action to either fixed end. Reeling in and recovering the launcher costs 1 AP from either terminus.
* **Grapple (Xm) & Target Displacement:** Firing a grapple launcher costs **1 AP** and expends **1 Charge**. It can target solid anchor points or creatures within its maximum range (**8 meters** for Standard, **16 meters** for Tactical):
  * **Inanimate Targets & Terrain:** Automatic success (no roll required). The motorized winch immediately pulls the user in a straight line directly to the anchor point.
  * **Hostile Creatures:** Resolved as a **Complex Test** using **Mobility + Strength**:
    * **Tether Pull (Push Effect):** Each Hit spent on the roll can trigger a **Push Effect** that pulls the target creature 1 space (2 meters) directly towards the user.
    * **Advancing:** Following the pull, the user can freely decide to move any number of spaces along the tether line towards the target (up to entering the target's space).
    * **Tearing Damage (Optional):** If the user desires, the grapple claw tears into the target upon impact, dealing damage equal to the **Success Points** generated on the Success Roll.
* **Utility Cordage:** Simple braided line with no intrinsic roll required. Provides physical support for climbing, hoisting, or securing objects at the GM's discretion.

### Fortification Special Properties

* **Deployable Cover:** Deploying or packing up a barricade costs **1 AP**. Once deployed, the barricade occupies one space and provides its listed **Cover Protection Bonus** (+1 to +3) to any creature taking shelter behind it. The barricade possesses its own **Cover HP** and **Innate Protection** rating. It can be picked up and recovered for **1 AP**.
* **Intercept (X) & Area-Defense Reaction:** Once deployed (1 AP), an Active Protection System beacon automatically monitors its space and all adjacent spaces. It gains **1 Free Reaction AP per round** to contest incoming Area Attacks and Ordnance explosions. It resolves an **Intercept Contest** rolling pure **Intercept (X)**. If no dice showing 8+ remain after the contest, the incoming explosive munition is destroyed mid-flight and its effect is completely negated. It can be picked up and recovered for **1 AP**.
* **Automount (X):** A motorized tripod and optical sensor package. Deploying the pod (including mounting any ranged weapon) costs **1 AP**; dismounting and packing up also costs **1 AP**. An operator or linked ally can spend **1 AP** to fire the mounted weapon remotely, rolling **Automount (X) + Finesse** (or pure **Automount (X)** if automated) for the attack. It can be picked up and recovered for **1 AP**.

## 3. Explosives & Launchers

Explosives deliver high-yield area destruction, obscuration, and tactical disruption. Rather than tracking individual loose throwables, combatants carry refillable **Grenade Rigs** that allow on-the-fly munition selection at the point of use.

| **Heavy Demolitions Rig** | Capacity 2, Refillable, Point-of-Use Choice | 3 | 8,000 | Reinforced breaching rig holding 2 heavy demolition or high-explosive charges. |

| **Missile Guidance System** | Delivery (Extreme Range) | 6 | 60,000 | Heavy laser-guided missile tube firing Ordnance out to Extreme Range. |

### Available Munition Profiles

When deploying a charge from a **Grenade Rig** or **Demolitions Rig**, the user chooses one of the following profiles at the point of use:

* **Frag Munition:** **Ordnance 4, Blast 3, 8m Radius.** Anti-personnel fragmentation bomb with serrated wire casing.
* **Flashbang Munition:** **Ordnance 5, Area Stun, 4m Radius.** Pyrotechnic stun canister blinding and deafening hostiles.
* **Smoke Canister:** **Area Obscurance, 8m Radius.** Dense aerosol canister deploying an opaque thermal smoke screen without rolling.
* **HE Munition:** **Ordnance 5, Blast 5, 4m Radius.** High-explosive concussion charge optimized for enclosed bulkheads.
* **Remote Demolition Charge:** **Ordnance 7, Blast 5, 12m Radius, Remote.** Heavy industrial explosive block triggered via encrypted radio. Available exclusively via the Heavy Demolitions Rig.

### Explosives Special Properties

* **Point-of-Use Munition Choice:** When expending a charge from a Grenade Rig or Demolitions Rig (1 AP), the operative chooses which munition to draw and prime at the exact moment of deployment. No pre-assignment during refilling is necessary.
* **Ordnance (X) Area Attack:** Throwing or launching an explosive costs **1 AP** and expends **1 Charge**. Hand-thrown grenades target any space within **Short Range** (0–5 spaces). Dedicated launcher rigs extend delivery to Medium, Long, or Extreme Range. Explosives resolve as an **Area Attack Success Test** rolling a pure **Ordnance (X)** dice pool.
* **Blast (X):** High-yield destructive shockwave. Blast modifies the bonus damage granted by the **Crit Effect**: spending a Hit on the explosive's Success Roll adds **+X damage** per Hit spent instead of the default +2.
* **Area Stun:** Non-lethal disorienting concussion. For each die showing 8+ on the Ordnance test, every creature caught within the blast radius discards its highest die on its next roll until the start of the next Player Turn.
* **Area Obscurance:** Igniting a smoke canister costs **1 AP** and automatically fills an **8m Radius** sphere with dense aerosol smoke without rolling. The cloud blocks line of sight and grants full concealment for the duration of the combat scene or until vented.
* **Remote:** Deploying a demolition charge costs **1 AP**. Once placed, the operator can detonate the charge at any time as a **Triggered Effect**.
* **Delivery (Range Band):** Dedicated launcher platforms extend the delivery range of loaded Ordnance to the specified range band (**Medium**, **Long**, or **Extreme Range**).

### Medical & Consumable Properties

* **Medicine (X) Triage:** Applying a medical kit to yourself or an adjacent ally costs **1 AP**, expends **1 Charge**, and requires a **Success Test** using **Medicine + Knowledge**.
* **Lesser Healing (1/X, Track):** Restores **1 segment** on the designated track (Physical or Mental) per **X Success Points** generated on the test. Lesser healing cannot repair locked (Aggravated) damage segments.
* **Greater Healing (1/X, Track):** Restores damage on the designated track at the standard rate (1 segment per X SPs). Additionally, Greater Healing can clear **Aggravated damage** segments at double the SP cost (2X SPs per locked segment cleared).
* **Point-of-Use Stim Choice:** When activating a Stim Injector or Chem-Rig (1 AP), the operative expends 1 Charge and chooses which single chemical formula to administer (each boosting one corresponding Core Attribute):
  * **Roid (Strength):** **+1 Strength.** High-grade anabolic surge providing raw muscular leverage and kinetic force (Volatile 2, Fleeting).
  * **Nitro (Agility):** **+1 Agility.** Adrenaline booster accelerating muscle twitch response, sprint speed, and evasive bounds (Volatile 2, Fleeting).
  * **Edgedance (Finesse):** **+1 Finesse.** Fine-motor neural enhancer for micrometric hand-eye precision and weapon alignment (Volatile 2, Fleeting).
  * **SharpX (Instinct):** **+1 Instinct.** Sensory cortex stimulant accelerating peripheral awareness, reaction speed, and battlefield read (Volatile 2, Fleeting).
  * **Cognita (Knowledge):** **+1 Knowledge.** Nootropic neuro-accelerator boosting tactical calculation, memory access, and technical recall (Volatile 2, Fleeting).
  * **Rizz (Presence):** **+1 Presence.** Vocal and pheromonal enhancement cocktail projecting commanding authority and poise under duress (Volatile 2, Fleeting).
* **Volatile (X):** Chemical toxicity and physical strain. When administered, the user immediately suffers **X unmitigated damage**. Consuming a stimulant while already under the effects of a Volatile substance causes additional damage equal to the total number of active Volatile substances in your system.
* **Fleeting:** The stimulant's surge lasts until the end of the current combat round during place-to-place encounters, or until the end of the Player Turn in day-to-day scenes.
