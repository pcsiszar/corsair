# Gear

Gear encompasses the broad spectrum of mission-critical tactical equipment, specialized toolkits, field consumables, and survival hardware utilized by operators in the Corsair universe. While weapons and armor govern basic combat survival, gear provides the technical leverage, mobility, intelligence, and utility needed to complete dangerous boarding operations and wilderness expeditions.

Every item in this catalog is defined by its **Properties**, **Load**, **Cost**, and dedicated **Item Attributes**.

---

## Gear Rules & Resolution

1. **Dedicated Attributes & Test Pairs:** Most gear categories provide dedicated item attributes (such as *Thrust*, *Medicine*, *Sensors*, or *Hacking*). When activated, the item's attribute is paired with a character's core attribute to form the dice pool for the test.
2. **Action Point Costs:** Deploying, activating, or utilizing any piece of gear requires **1 Action Point (AP)**, unless explicitly noted otherwise. Passive items that require no roll (such as *Smoke Grenades* or deploying *Cover*) still require 1 AP to deploy or ignite.
3. **Stand-Alone Explosive Rolls:** Explosives do not pair with a character attribute. Instead, grenades and charges resolve as Area Attack **Success Tests** rolling pure **Ordnance** dice pools against spaces within range.
4. **Upgrade Compatibility:** Like weapons and armor, eligible mechanical gear and electronic devices can be enhanced with up to 3 standard Upgrade Kits to reduce Load by 1 or increase their primary attribute by +1.

### Mobility Special Properties

* **Jetpack (Xm):** Activating a jetpack costs **1 AP** and requires a **Success Test** using **Thrust + Agility**. Your flight movement is equal to the jetpack's base speed (**X meters**) plus **1 meter per Success Point**. You can move freely in three dimensions, hover, and ignore difficult terrain. You must end your round on solid ground directly beneath you unless in zero-g. You ignore all falling damage while wearing an active jetpack.
  * **Vector Boost (Crit Effect):** When resolving a jetpack's Success Roll, spending a Hit modifies the **Crit Effect** to grant an additional **+4 meters of movement** per Hit spent instead of the standard +2 Success Points.
  * **Charging:** You can spend **1 AP** to fly up to the jetpack's base movement speed in a straight line and immediately execute a **Melee Attack** at the end of the movement.
* **Grip (X):** Scaling sheer, vertical, or inverted surfaces costs **1 AP** and requires a **Success Test** using **Grip + Strength**. While scaling, you adhere securely to surfaces; base climbing movement is 8 meters plus 1 meter per Success Point.
* **Zipline (Xm):** Deploying a zipline between two solid anchor points within **X meters** costs **1 AP**. Once secured, any creature can traverse the motorized cable as part of their standard Movement Action to either fixed end. Reeling in and recovering the launcher costs 1 AP from either terminus.
* **Utility Cordage:** Simple braided line with no intrinsic roll required. Provides physical support for climbing, hoisting, or securing objects at the GM's discretion.

### Fortification Special Properties

* **Deployable Cover:** Deploying or packing up a barricade costs **1 AP**. Once deployed, the barricade occupies one space and provides its listed **Cover Protection Bonus** (+1 to +3) to any creature taking shelter behind it. The barricade possesses its own **Cover HP** and **Innate Protection** rating. It can be picked up (and thus redeployed later) for **1 AP**.
* **Intercept (X) & Area-Defense Reaction:** Once deployed (1 AP), an Active Protection System beacon automatically monitors its space and all adjacent spaces. It gains **1 Free Reaction AP per round** to contest incoming Area Attacks and Ordnance explosions. It resolves an **Intercept Contest** rolling **Intercept (X)**. If no dice showing 8+ remain after the contest, the incoming explosive munition is destroyed mid-flight and its effect is completely negated. It can be picked up (and thus redeployed later) for **1 AP**.
* **Automount (X):** A motorized tripod and optical sensor package. Deploying the pod (including mounting any ranged weapon) costs **1 AP**; dismounting and packing up also costs **1 AP**. An operator or linked ally can spend **1 AP** to fire the mounted weapon remotely, rolling **Automount (X) + Finesse** (or pure **Automount (X)** if automated) for the attack. When damaged or commanded, the pod locks into the deck plate, preventing unauthorized dismounting. It can be picked up (and thus redeployed later) for **1 AP**.

### Explosives Special Properties

* **Ordnance (X) Area Attack:** Throwing or placing an explosive costs **1 AP**. Hand-thrown grenades target any space within **Short Range** (0–5 spaces). Launchers extend this delivery distance to Medium, Long, or Extreme Range. Explosives resolve as an **Area Attack Success Test** rolling pure **Ordnance (X)** dice pool.
* **Blast (X):** High-yield destructive shockwave. Blast replaces the bonus damage granted by the **Crit Effect** by the specified amount: spending a Hit on the explosive's Success Roll adds **+X damage** per Hit spent instead of the default +2.
* **Area Stun:** Non-lethal disorienting concussion. For each die showing 8+ on the Ordnance test, every creature caught within the blast radius discards its highest die on its next roll until the start of the next Player Turn.
* **Area Obscurance:** Igniting a smoke grenade costs **1 AP** and automatically fills an **8m Radius** sphere with dense aerosol smoke without rolling. The cloud blocks line of sight and grants full concealment for the duration of the combat scene or until vented.
* **Remote:** Deploying a demolition charge costs **1 AP**. Once placed, the operator can detonate the charge at any time as a **Triggered Effect** (or Persistent Trigger Effect).
* **Delivery (Range Band):** Dedicated launcher platforms extend the operational delivery range of loaded Ordnance to the specified range band (**Medium**, **Long**, or **Extreme Range**).

### Medical & Consumable Properties

* **Lesser Healing (1/X, Track):** Restores **1 segment** on the designated track (Physical or Mental) per **X Success Points** generated on the test. Lesser healing cannot repair locked (Aggravated) damage segments.
* **Greater Healing (1/X, Track):** Restores damage on the designated track at the standard rate (1 segment per X SPs). Additionally, Greater Healing can clear **Aggravated damage** segments at double the SP cost (2X SPs per locked segment cleared).
* **Stimulant (Stim):** Administering a stimulant costs **1 AP** and immediately increases the listed character Attributes for the duration. Taking a stimulant that boosts an already-enhanced attribute overrides the prior dose.
* **Volatile (X):** Chemical toxicity and physical strain. When administered, the user immediately suffers **X unmitigated damage**. If you consume a stimulant while already under the effects of a Volatile substance, you suffer additional damage equal to the total number of active Volatile substances in your system.
* **Fleeting:** The stimulant's chemical surge lasts until the end of the current combat round during place-to-place encounters, or until the end of the Player Turn in day-to-day scenes.
