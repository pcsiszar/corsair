# Armor

In the perilous expanses of the settled systems, a dependable suit of armor is often the only barrier between a Corsair and lethal trauma. Whether weathering high-velocity kinetic slug rounds in a depressurized corridor, deflecting shrapnel from an explosive hull breach, or absorbing punishing melee impacts during a boarding skirmish, protective gear preserves a combatant's operational capability in the deadliest environments.

---

## Armor Statistics Explained

Every suit of armor is defined by mechanical attributes that govern its defensive resilience, physical requirements, and logistical burden:

* **Protection:** The flat integer value of physical and kinetic damage reduced from each incoming attack before it affects your health. When you are struck by an attack, subtract your armor's Protection directly from the incoming damage.
  * **Stacking Rules:** Armor Protection stacks directly with defensive bonuses provided by **Shields** (e.g., a Riot Shield providing +2 Protection) and **Cover** (e.g., Medium Cover providing +2 Protection). However, a character can only wear **one suit of armor** at a time; separate flat armor values do not stack.

* **Minimum Strength (Min STR):** Moving swiftly while clad in dense ballistic weaves, ceramic strike plates, or motorized hydraulic servos requires physical power and conditioning. Armor enforces a strict zero-division requirement:
  * **Worn Requirement:** If your Strength is lower than the armor's Min STR, the suit is too heavy and cumbersome for your musculature to articulate. You **cannot wear or don** the armor under any circumstances.

* **Load:** The mass and physical bulk of the armor as governed by the inventory Load system. Armor counts against your character's maximum Load limit (`12 + (Strength × 2)`).

* **Cost:** The standard galactic market price of the armor in Credits.

## Armor Upgrade Kits

Standard armor suits can be customized into mastercrafted protection systems through specialized armorer machining, lightweight composite substitution, energy-absorbing liquid gels, or high-density ceramic strike faces. Because each subsequent modification requires increasingly intricate fitting and structural balancing, upgrade kits escalate in cost with every installation.

* **Upgrade Limit:** A single suit of armor can benefit from a maximum of **3 Upgrade Kits** in total.
* **Tiered Cost Structure:**
  * **1st Upgrade:** **25,000 Credits**
  * **2nd Upgrade:** **60,000 Credits**
  * **3rd Upgrade:** **120,000 Credits**
* **Upgrade Choices:** Each time an Upgrade Kit is applied, choose one of the following permanent modifications:
  * **Lightweight Composites:** Precision skeletonization and advanced aerogel composites reduce the armor's **Load by 1** (to a minimum of 1 Load).
  * **Hardened Plating:** High-density ceramic-carbide strike faces or micro-layered titanium inserts increase the armor's **Protection by +1**.

## Example: Armor and Protection in Action

> ### Tactical Example: Weathering the Boarding Barrage
> During a violent breach of a station cargo bay, Jax is caught in the open when a pirate gunner opens fire with a **Battle Rifle**.
> 
> Jax is equipped with **Tactical Battleplate** (**Protection 4**, Min STR 4, Load 4) and is carrying a **Riot Shield** (**+2 Protection Bonus**, Min STR 4, 1H, Load 2). Jax's character sheet shows **Strength 5**, comfortably satisfying the Min STR requirements for both pieces of defensive gear.
> 
> 1. **Incoming Attack:** The pirate gunner rolls an attack and generates a devastating total of **12 points of physical damage**.
> 
> 2. **Calculating Jax's Total Protection:**
>    * Tactical Battleplate Base Protection: **4**
>    * Riot Shield Protection Bonus: **+2**
>    * Total Combined Protection: **6** (4 + 2)
> 
> 3. **Applying Damage Reduction:**
>    * Incoming Damage: **12**
>    * Subtracted Protection: **-6**
>    * **Net Damage Received:** 12 - 6 = **6 points of damage**.
> 
> Thanks to the combined ballistic mitigation of the battleplate and riot shield, Jax absorbs half the incoming kinetic shock, avoiding critical trauma and remaining in the fight.
