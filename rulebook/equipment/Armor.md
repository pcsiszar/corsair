# Armor

*You can download and print the [Armor Printable Reference Sheet](Armor_Sheet.html) for play.*

In the perilous expanses of the settled systems, a dependable suit of armor is often the only barrier between a Corsair and lethal trauma. Whether weathering rifle fire in a depressurized corridor, deflecting shrapnel from an explosive hull breach, or absorbing punishing melee impacts during a boarding skirmish, protective gear preserves a combatant's operational capability in the deadliest environments.

---

## Armor Statistics Explained

Every suit of armor is defined by mechanical attributes that govern its defensive resilience, tactical properties, and logistical burden:

* **Protection:** The flat integer value of physical and kinetic damage reduced from each incoming attack before it affects your health. When you are struck by an attack, subtract your armor's Protection directly from the incoming damage.
  * **Stacking Rules:** Armor Protection stacks directly with defensive bonuses provided by **Shields** (e.g., a Riot Shield providing +2 Protection) and **Cover** (e.g., Medium Cover providing +2 Protection). However, a character can only wear **one suit of armor** at a time; separate armor Protection values do not stack.

* **Load:** The mass and physical bulk of the armor as governed by the inventory Load system. Armor counts against your character's maximum Load limit (`12 + (Strength × 2)`).

* **No Minimum Strength Requirement:** Unlike melee weapons and shields, armor does not enforce a binary Minimum Strength requirement to don or wear. Any operative can wear any suit of armor, provided they have sufficient Load capacity to carry its weight. However, heavier suits feature the **Cumbersome (X)** property, imposing significant physical penalties on characters who lack the conditioning or agility to handle dense ballistic plating.

* **Armor Properties:** Specialized engineering features and structural treatments that provide unique tactical benefits or impose physical trade-offs:
  * **Cumbersome (X):** The mass and rigid plates of this armor restrict sudden articulation. While wearing this armor, you suffer **X Downgrades** on all tests involving **Agility** (including movement Success Tests, acrobatics, athletics, stealth, and agility-based attack rolls or contest rolls).
  * **Hardened:** Layered boron-carbide strike faces and shock-absorbing ceramic weaves diffuse concentrated kinetic penetrators. When an attacker scores a **Crit Effect** against you, the bonus Success Points gained per Hit spent is reduced by 1 (granting **+1 Success Point per Hit spent**, instead of +2).
  * **Blast-Dampened:** Heavy aramid spall-liners and concussive baffles dissipate explosive shockwaves. You gain **+2 additional Protection against Area Attacks and Explosives**.
  * **Tactical Harness (X):** The armor features reinforced load-bearing webbing, quick-draw holsters, and modular equipment pouches. It provides **X dedicated Load slots for any equipment** (weapons, ammunition, medkits, sensors, or tools). Items stowed in the harness do not count against your character's standard Load capacity, up to a total of X Load.
  * **Muffled:** Acoustic-dampening micro-weaves and friction-reducing joint gaskets suppress gear rattle. You gain **+1 Upgrade on Stealth tests**.

* **Cost:** The standard galactic market price of the armor in Credits.

## Prominent Manufacturers

While the armor catalog lists universal functional archetypes, protective gear across the cosmos is manufactured by major aerospace and defense conglomerates:

* **Chidori-Qwei Combatwear:** Sleek, flexible polymer garments, smart-fiber flightsuits, and stylish armored jackets tailored for urban operations and covert security.
* **Panthera Arms:** Precision lightweight composite armor and modular plate carriers favored by corporate security strike teams.
* **Vandal's Workshop:** Stamped-sheet alloy armor, rugged industrial hazard suits, and salvage-reinforced plating designed to endure brutal frontline abuse.
* **Caldera Foundry:** Heavy cast ceramic-titanium battleplate and fortified carapace suits engineered for zero-compromise kinetic resistance.
* **Ker'Satz Korp:** Reinforced ballistic assault chassis and industrial siege rigs built to withstand catastrophic structural punishment.

## Armor Upgrade Kits

Standard armor suits can be customized into mastercrafted protection systems through specialized armorer machining, lightweight composite substitution, liquid-gel trauma dampeners, or high-density ceramic strike faces. Because personal armor passively mitigates every incoming attack, custom mil-spec armorer tuning commands a significant market premium.

* **Upgrade Limit:** A single suit of armor can benefit from a maximum of **3 Upgrade Kits** in total.
* **Tiered Cost Structure:**
  * **1st Upgrade:** **50,000 Credits**
  * **2nd Upgrade:** **100,000 Credits**
  * **3rd Upgrade:** **180,000 Credits**
* **Upgrade Choices:** Each time an Upgrade Kit is applied, choose one of the following permanent modifications:
  * **Reinforced Plating:** High-density ceramic-carbide strike faces or micro-layered titanium inserts increase the armor's **Protection by +1** (maximum +3 Protection total across all kits).
  * **Lightweight Composites:** Precision skeletonization and advanced aerogel composites reduce the armor's **Load by 1** (to a minimum of 1 Load).
  * **Specialized Modification:** Installs one specialized armor property onto the suit: **Hardened**, **Blast-Dampened**, **Tactical Harness (1)**, or **Muffled**. *(An armor cannot possess the same property more than once; adding Tactical Harness to a suit that already features it increases its rating by +1, up to a maximum of Tactical Harness (2)).*

## Example: Armor and Protection in Action

> ### Tactical Example: Weathering the Boarding Barrage
> During a violent breach of a station cargo bay, Jax is caught in the open when a pirate gunner opens fire with a **Battle Rifle**.
> 
> Jax is equipped with **Tactical Battleplate** (**Protection 5**, Load 4) and carries a **Riot Shield** (**+2 Protection Bonus**, Min STR 4, 1H, Load 2). Because Jax's armor is Tactical Battleplate, it carries no **Cumbersome** penalty, leaving his **Agility** rolls completely unhindered.
> 
> 1. **Incoming Attack:** The pirate gunner rolls an attack test and scores a Hit, spending 1 Hit on a **Crit Effect** (+2 damage bonus) alongside the Battle Rifle's flat +4 Damage Bonus, generating a devastating total of **14 points of physical damage**.
> 
> 2. **Calculating Jax's Total Protection:**
>    * Tactical Battleplate Base Protection: **5**
>    * Riot Shield Protection Bonus: **+2**
>    * Total Combined Protection: **7** (5 + 2)
> 
> 3. **Applying Damage Reduction:**
>    * Incoming Damage: **14**
>    * Subtracted Protection: **-7**
>    * **Net Damage Received:** 14 - 7 = **7 points of damage**.
> 
> Thanks to the combined protection of his battleplate and riot shield, Jax absorbs half the incoming impact, containing the damage cleanly within Track I of his Physical Condition and remaining ready to return fire.
