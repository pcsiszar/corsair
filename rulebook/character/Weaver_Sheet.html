Nanite Reservoir

Starting Reserve: 3 Reserve stowed at creation. All nanite actions and weave skills cost 1 Reserve by default.

End of Round Recharge: Spent Reserve recharges at the end of the round.

Nanite Actions

Reserve Costs
1
Handheld tools, rope, grapple hook (Load 1–2)
2
Ladder, chair, clothing, blanket (Load 3–4)
3
Bench, thick 1m x 1m wall, space suit (Load 5–6)
Restrictions

Weapons, armor, cover, and healing items are off-limits unless unlocked via Weave skills. Creations/modifications have 0 Load. Action range is your current space.

Disassembly

Command creations to disassemble at any time for free to instantly regain spent Reserve.

Class Modifications
