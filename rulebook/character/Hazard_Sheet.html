Core Mechanics

Pressure: Skills adjust Pressure up/down. Pressure cannot exceed bounds (0-3). Higher pressure scales Volatile Die.

Volatile Die: Add to any Hazard Attribute test. If Volatile Die rolls a Hit, take 2 damage and reroll the Volatile Die to reset Pressure:

  • 1-3: Pressure 3 | 4-6: Pressure 2
  • 7-9: Pressure 1 | 10-12: Pressure 0

Baseline Actions (No Skill required):

  • Venom (Apply Downgrade): Melee attack. Hits create Downgrades (permanent until cleared). Max active = Venom Attribute.
  • Hazard (Melee Attack): Melee attack in own space. Immediate damage force.
  • Serum (Apply Upgrade): Success Test. SP establishes permanent Upgrade. Max active = Serum Attribute.
