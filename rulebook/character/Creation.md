# Character Creation

Before building individual characters, the group and the GM decide on the campaign's **Mode of Play**. Your chosen mode determines your crew's legal authority, access to starships, contract types, and relationship with sponsors across the Sphere.

---

## Modes of Play

Corsair supports three distinct modes of play:

### 1. Freelancers (Aspirants)
* **Status & Resources:** Independent contractors with **no Sponsor** and **no ship**.
* **Campaign Premise:** The crew operates at the ground level of the Sphere. Taking dirt-side security jobs, orbital habitat contracts, planetary bounties, or salvage gigs, operatives rely on commercial passenger liners, cargo transports, and local shuttles to travel between locations.
* **Crew Objective:** Building a reputation, amassing sufficient credits to purchase their own hull, or catching the attention of a major power to secure an official Corsair sponsorship and charter.
* **Character Creation:** Skip the **Sponsor** step.

### 2. Corsairs (Chartered Cell)
* **Status & Resources:** Commissioned agents holding an official cross-jurisdictional legal mandate, **individual Sponsors**, and a shared **Corsair Starship** (such as an RF "Kestrel").
* **Campaign Premise:** The standard Corsair experience. Autonomous cells of 3 to 5 specialists dispatched across planetary surfaces and orbital stations to investigate high-profile incidents, enforce inter-jurisdictional law, conduct tactical strikes, and resolve geopolitical crises.
* **Sponsor Selection:** Each player chooses an active **Sponsor**. To reflect the institutional requirement that Corsair cells remain impartial and self-policing, it is strongly recommended that **each player selects a different sponsor**.
* **Crew Objective:** Fulfilling sponsor petitions, balancing conflicting faction loyalties, answering to the Oversight Committee, and keeping the peace across the Sphere.

### 3. Rogue Corsairs (Outlaws)
* **Status & Resources:** Outlaws operating on the fringes of the law with **ex-sponsors** and an unregistered, jury-rigged, or re-flagged **ship** (such as a GVI "Rusty Hook").
* **Campaign Premise:** Former Corsairs who were stripped of their mandates by the Oversight Committee, broke regulations, went rogue, or walked away from corrupt superiors. The crew takes on **mostly legally questionable jobs at best**—black-market salvage, high-stakes smuggling, shadow ops, clandestine extraction, and privateering.
* **Ex-Sponsors & Backchannels:** Rather than active sponsors, each player chooses an **Ex-Sponsor**. This represents the faction the operative formerly served and might still maintain contact with—leveraging backchannel favors, old comrades, mutual blackmail, or grey-market contracts while dodging official authorities.
* **Crew Objective:** Evading Flotilla patrol cutters and bounty hunters, paying for illicit ship repairs and black-market ammunition, and surviving in the shadows of the Sphere.

## Sponsor (or Ex-Sponsor)

Depending on your chosen **Mode of Play**, select a **Sponsor** (for *Corsairs*) or an **Ex-Sponsor** (for *Rogue Corsairs*). If playing as *Freelancers*, skip this step.

For Corsairs, it is recommended that each player selects a different sponsor:

- **Flotilla:** The independent interstellar defense force of Newcomer civilization. Focuses on fleet defense, orbital integrity, anti-piracy operations, and peacekeeping.
- **Colonial:** The planetary governing bodies and administrations across the Sovereign Moons. Focuses on territorial defense, local law enforcement, and resource stability.
- **Corporate:** Multi-system megacorporations with vast industrial assets across the moons of the Sphere. Focuses on corporate intelligence, trade protection, asset recovery, and proprietary security.
- **Civilian:** Labor unions, scientific consortiums, religious sects, political leagues, or humanitarian NGOs. Focuses on worker rights, civil liberties, research ethics, and refugee relief.
- **Private:** An extraordinarily wealthy oligarch, dynastic family, or private syndicate. Focuses on private collections, high-stakes personal vendettas, discreet body-guarding, and shadow influence.

## Race

Choose a race for your character. Your race represents your character's biological lineage, granting innate attribute adjustments or additional Experience Points (XP) at character creation:

- **Human:** *Adaptable, ambitious, and widespread humanoids found across the Sphere.*
    - Gain **+4 XP** (for a total of 10 starting XP).
- **Craggen:** *Stout, small-sized humanoids with mineral-dense skin and crystalline gemstone eyes.*
    - Gain **+2 points** distributed between **Finesse** and **Knowledge** (+2/+0, +1/+1, or +0/+2).
- **Naga:** *Cold-blooded, bipedal reptilians adorned with vibrant, decorative cranial crests.*
    - Gain **+2 points** distributed between **Agility** and **Instinct** (+2/+0, +1/+1, or +0/+2).
- **Sylvan:** *Imposing, powerful humanoids marked by majestic manes, tusks, and chlorophyllic skin.*
    - Gain **+2 points** distributed between **Strength** and **Presence** (+2/+0, +1/+1, or +0/+2).
- **Drakeling:** *Nimble, scaly humanoids bearing swept horns and expressive, serpentine tails.*
    - Gain **+2 XP** (for a total of 8 starting XP), plus **+1 Agility** or **+1 Instinct**.
- **Elf:** *Graceful, slender humanoids with pointed ears and mild chlorophyllic pigmentation.*
    - Gain **+2 XP** (for a total of 8 starting XP), plus **+1 Strength** or **+1 Presence**.
- **Gnome:** *Inquisitive, slender humanoids with standard skin tones and iridescent eye, hair, and nail pigmentation.*
    - Gain **+2 XP** (for a total of 8 starting XP), plus **+1 Finesse** or **+1 Knowledge**.
- **Dwarf:** *Dense, subterranean humanoids combining crystalline skin deposits with muscular, reinforced frames.*
    - Gain **+1 Strength** or **+1 Presence**, plus **+1 Finesse** or **+1 Knowledge**.
- **Kobold:** *Cunning, small-stature reptilians crowned with natural, gemstone-patterned facial bone masks.*
    - Gain **+1 Agility** or **+1 Instinct**, plus **+1 Finesse** or **+1 Knowledge**.
- **Troll:** *Towering, horned humanoids with thick hides, prominent tusks, and striking skin pigmentation.*
    - Gain **+1 Strength** or **+1 Presence**, plus **+1 Agility** or **+1 Instinct**.

## Class (MANTLE Rig)

Choose a class for your character. In *Corsair*, your class is not an abstract profession or biological caste, but an advanced proprietary **MANTLE** (*Modular Adaptive Neural Transmission Linked Equipment*)—a specialized, aerospace-grade cybernetic or technological chassis that interfaces directly with your central nervous system via a permanent **Neural Control Chip (NCC)** and spinal shunt.

Every starting operative begins play equipped with their first MANTLE. Commissioned **Corsairs** receive an officially registered frame legally underwritten by their sponsor, while uncommissioned **Freelancers** acquire theirs through competitive Sponsor Candidate training pipelines, veteran hand-me-downs from the **Freelancer Network**, or refurbished post-war salvage.

Because an operative's synaptic bandwidth can only sustain one active telemetry mapping at a time without risking catastrophic **Synaptic Burnout**, you can only have **one active MANTLE chassis equipped at a time**. Operatives may purchase and calibrate additional bespoke chassis over their career for **100,000 Credits** each during campaign downtime. For complete origins, technical specs, and neuro-biology, see [Classes & The MANTLE System](../classes/Classes.md).

### Class Options

- **[Conduit](../classes/Conduit.md):** *A living battery and shock trooper channeling volatile elemental energy through implanted capacitor plates.*
    - **Class Attributes:** **Flame**, **Frost**, **Lightning**
    - **Starting Package:** 1 Amp & Burst Ability Pair of your choice.
- **[Hazard](../classes/Hazard.md):** *A rogue combat chemist armed with a pressurized Catalyst Chamber and corrosive, restorative, or explosive reagents.*
    - **Class Attributes:** **Venom**, **Hazard**, **Serum**
    - **Starting Package:** 1 Chem Kit of your choice.
- **[Pilot](../classes/Pilot.md):** *A versatile operator neurally integrated with an Exodrone that can augment personal physiology or act as an autonomous avatar.*
    - **Class Attributes:** **Power**, **Speed**, **Precision**
    - **Starting Package:** The Basic Module, plus 1 Exo-Module of your choice.
- **[Vanguard](../classes/Vanguard.md):** *A high-mobility frontline combatant commanding a morphing kinetic board that shifts between defense, flight, and heavy firepower.*
    - **Class Attributes:** **Shield**, **Glider**, **Cannon**
    - **Starting Package:** 1 Form Ability Package of your choice.
- **[Weaver](../classes/Weaver.md):** *A nanite artisan and saboteur manipulating a living fluid swarm to forge equipment, augment allies, or infiltrate technology on the fly.*
    - **Class Attributes:** **Create**, **Control**, **Modify**
    - **Starting Package:** 4 Reserve, plus 1 Nanite Weave of your choice.

### Class Attributes

Every class is defined by a signature set of three Class Attributes. During tests, Class Attributes pair with your Core Attributes (such as *Flame + Strength* or *Create + Finesse*) or with other Class Attributes (such as *Hazard + Hazard*).

* **Starting Values:** All three of your Class Attributes start at a baseline rating of **1** (`1 / 1 / 1`).
* **Point Budget:** Spend **7 Class Creation Points** to increase your three Class Attributes.
* **Point Costs:**
    * Increasing an Attribute from **1 to 4** costs **1 point** per increase.
    * Increasing an Attribute from **4 to 5** costs **2 points**.
* **Starting Cap:** The maximum rating for any Class Attribute at character creation is **5**. You may leave an Attribute at its starting value of 1.

### Starting Ability Package

Every class includes unique baseline chassis mechanics (such as minor touch effects, volatile dice, attached/detached drone modes, board stances, or raw nanite creation). In addition to these baseline mechanics, choose **one starting Class Ability Package** from your chosen class chapter. This package immediately unlocks its associated abilities on your character sheet.

## Core Attributes

Every character in Corsair is defined by six Core Attributes representing their fundamental physical and mental conditioning: **Strength**, **Finesse**, **Agility**, **Presence**, **Knowledge**, and **Instinct**.

* **Starting Values:** All six of your Core Attributes start at a baseline rating of **1** (`1 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 1`).
* **Point Budget:** Spend **10 Creation Points** to increase your six Core Attributes.
* **Point Costs:**
    * Increasing an Attribute from **1 to 4** costs **1 point** per increase.
    * Increasing an Attribute from **4 to 5** costs **2 points**.
* **Starting Cap:** The maximum rating for any Core Attribute at character creation is **5**.
* **Racial Bonuses:** Racial attribute increases are applied prior to Creation Point distribution, meaning if you want to increase an Attribute to 5, you cannot use a racial bonus to avoid paying 2 Creation Points for going from 4 to 5.

## Starter Kit

Every operative is equipped with baseline survival gear for life in the fringe, consisting of standard personal communications, armor, a melee weapon, a firearm, and one specialized utility kit. Select items from each category below that fit your **Strength** and chosen combat attributes:

### Maximum Load Capacity

All weapons, protective armor, and gear are governed by the **Load** system, representing the physical mass, weight, and bulk of equipment carried on your person. An operative's carrying limit is determined directly by their physical power:

$$\text{Maximum Load Capacity} = 12 + (\text{Strength} \times 2)$$

This carrying capacity is an absolute operational limit; an operative cannot carry items with a total Load exceeding this value under any circumstances. When outfitting your operative with weapons, armor, and gear below, sum their Load values against your maximum capacity, keeping in mind that leaving spare Load capacity is vital for carrying mission salvage, heavy ammo supplies, or recovered assets in the field.

### 1. Universal Gear
All characters begin with:
* 1 **[Commlink](../equipment/Gear.md)** (Load 0 | Long-Range Communication)

### 2. Armor (Choose 1)
Every operative receives their choice of baseline personal protective gear:
* **[Padded Flightsuit](../equipment/Armor.md)** (Protection 2 | Load 1) — *Lightweight, pressurized flight jumpsuit with minimal bulk.*
* **[Armored Jacket](../equipment/Armor.md)** (Protection 3 | Load 2) — *Durable utility coat lined with flexible ballistic inserts for heavier protection.*

### 3. Melee Weapon (Choose 1)
Select one melee weapon tier your **Strength** satisfies (1H weapons require two hands if below Min STR). Within your chosen tier, pick the weapon that matches your favored attribute pair:
* **Piercing (Agility + Finesse):** Adds the lower of Agility or Finesse to damage.
* **Slashing (Agility + Strength):** Adds the lower of Agility or Strength to damage.
* **Striking (Strength + Finesse):** Adds the lower of Strength or Finesse to damage.

Choose one of the following:
* **Light Melee (Min STR 1 | Load 1/2 | Dmg +0):**

## Credits

You start with 5,000 Credits, which you can use to purchase any desired gear or keep for later.

## Skills & Traits

While Attributes represent your character's raw physical and mental talent, Skills represent specialized training and field expertise linked to specific Attribute Pairs (granting automatic **Upgrades** on relevant tests, as detailed in [Skills](Player_Characters.md#skills)). In addition, operatives can acquire unique [Traits](Traits.md) that provide tactical exceptions, defensive reactions, and specialized combat capabilities.

Now that your operative's chassis, core attributes, combat equipment, and loadout are established, you finalize your character by selecting their specialized training and tactical habits. Instead of tracking an isolated pool of Skill Points, characters receive flat **Experience Points (XP)** at character creation. Operatives can spend this starting XP freely to purchase Skills, acquire Traits, or combine both:

* **Starting XP Budget:** Characters begin with a baseline of **6 XP** (with **Humans** receiving **+4 XP** for 10 XP total, and **Human-mixed races**—Drakelings, Elves, and Gnomes—receiving **+2 XP** for 8 XP total).
* **Purchasing Skills:**
    * Learning a new **Level 1** Skill costs **2 XP** (granting +1 Upgrade).
    * Increasing a Skill from **Level 1 to Level 2** costs **4 XP** (cumulative cost of 6 XP; granting +2 Upgrades).
    * Increasing a Skill from **Level 2 to Level 3** costs **6 XP** (cumulative cost of 12 XP; granting +3 Upgrades).
* **Purchasing Traits:**
    * Operatives can spend their starting XP directly on any [Traits](Traits.md) of their choice.
    * Each Trait costs between **2 XP and 8 XP** as listed in the Traits catalog. There are no prerequisite unlock challenges or gatekeeping requirements.
* **Flexible Creation Options:** With a baseline of 6 XP, operatives have full freedom to shape their operational focus:
    * **Three Level 1 Skills:** Purchase three separate Level 1 Skills (3 × 2 XP = 6 XP) for well-rounded field proficiency.
    * **One Level 2 Skill:** Invest all 6 XP into a single signature specialization (2 XP for Level 1 + 4 XP for Level 2 = 6 XP) to start as a dedicated specialist.
    * **Traits Focus:** Spend the entire 6 XP on Traits (such as *Tough as Nails* for 6 XP, or *Brawler* [2 XP] + *Counter Striker* [4 XP] = 6 XP).
    * **Mixed Skills & Traits:** Combine one Level 1 Skill (2 XP) and one 4 XP Trait (such as *Marksman*, *Iron Will*, or *Healer*) for 6 XP total.
    * **Human & Hybrid Customization:** Races with bonus XP enjoy even broader combinations. A Human with 10 XP can acquire a Level 2 Skill (6 XP) plus two Level 1 Skills (4 XP), or choose a high-end Trait like *Controlled Blast* (8 XP) alongside a Level 1 Skill (2 XP).
* **Starting Skill Cap:** The maximum rating for any Skill at character creation is **Level 2** (6 XP total), preserving Level 3 as an elite distinction achieved through campaign advancement.
