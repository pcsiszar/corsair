# Character Advancement

There are various ways characters can improve as they gain experience and Credits to purchase gear. While buying superior weapons and armor with earned Credits is straightforward, players cannot simply spend money to increase a character's physical or mental Attributes.

## Experience Points

You earn XP (Experience Points) for every 10,000 Credits your team earns. This is done primarily through contracts you receive from sponsors, but it can be achieved through other means as well. Selling gear or assets previously owned or purchased does not count toward earning XP.

The GM may also reward the team with XP for notable feats and accomplishments. For example, a team that chooses to perform a heroic act, sacrificing a large payday, may be rewarded with double the XP they would have otherwise gained. Such rewards are subject to the GM's discretion.

## Improving Attributes

To improve an Attribute, you must pay an amount of XP equal to three times the new level of the Attribute. You can only advance one level at a time. For example, increasing your **Strength** from 4 to 5 costs 15 XP.

## Learning Skills

To learn or improve a Skill, you must pay an amount of XP equal to two times the new level of the Skill. You can only advance one level at a time. You can learn a new Skill at level 1 for 2 XP. For example, learning Marksmanship 1 (**Accuracy + Finesse**) costs 2 XP, and then improving it to level 2 costs 4 XP.

## Gaining Traits

After unlocking a Trait, you must master it. Every Trait has a Mastery cost in XP that you must pay in order to use it.
