# Character Advancement

There are various ways your character can improve as they gain more and more experience and credits to buy gear with.
While buying better weapons and armor with the Credits you earned is straightforward, you can't just throw money at your
muscles or brain to make you stronger or smarter.

## Experience Points

You earn XP or Experience Points for every 10,000 Credits your team earns. This is done primarily through contracts you
receive from your sponsors, but can be achieved through other means as well, though selling gear and things you owned or
bought previously does not count.

The GM may also reward the team for feats and accomplishments with XP if they wish. For example, a team that choose to
do a heroic thing but sacrificed a huge payday, may be rewarded with double the XP they would have gained from the
money. Or not. It's up to them.

## Improving Attributes

You have to pay an amount of XP equal to 3 times new level you want to advance to, and you can only advance one level at
a time. For example, increasing your Strength from 4 to 5 costs 15 XP.

## Learning Skills

You have to pay an amount of XP equal to 2 times the level of the skill you want to advance to, and you can only advance
on level. You can learn a new skill for 2 XP. For example, learning Marksmanship 1 (Accuracy + Finesse) costs 2 XP, then
improving it to level 2 costs 4 XP.

## Gaining Traits

After unlocking a Trait, you still have to master it. Every Trait will have a Mastery cost in XP that you have to pay in
order to use it.
