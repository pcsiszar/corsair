# Character Advancement

There are various ways characters can improve as they gain experience and Credits to purchase gear. While buying
superior weapons and armor with earned Credits is straightforward, players cannot simply spend money to increase a
character's physical or mental Attributes.

## Experience Points

To increase your attributes, advance your skills, and master traits, you need Experience Points (XP). XP is earned in
two ways, training, and missions. Training is the process of learning new skills and improving existing. To train, you
need to spend 1 AP during day-to-day pacing, which can be performed anywhere, even onboard your ship, or out in the
wilds.

The other way to earn XP is by spending Gambit on tests. Any time you spend Gambit on a test you would have otherwise
failed. It doesn't matter if you fail or succeed after adding Gambit, and it doesn't matter how many Gambits you spend
on the test; the maximum amount of XP you can earn is 1 XP per test.

### Improving Attributes

To improve an Attribute, you must pay an amount of XP equal to three times the new level of the Attribute. You can only
advance one level at a time. For example, increasing your **Strength** from 4 to 5 costs 15 XP.

| Current Level | Target Level | XP (AP/Days) Required | Narrative Scale                                                 |
|---------------|--------------|-----------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1             | **2**        | 6 XP                  | Basic operational competency.                                   |
| 2             | **3**        | 9 XP                  | Standard field agent proficiency.                               |
| 3             | **4**        | 12 XP                 | Highly trained veteran specialist.                              |
| 4             | **5**        | 15 XP                 | Elite sector-level capability.                                  |
| 5             | **6**        | 18 XP                 | Master-class performance / High-end conditioning.               |

| 7             | **8**        | 24 XP                 | The absolute limit of human and standard cybernetic capability. |

### Learning Skills

To learn or improve a Skill, you must pay an amount of XP equal to two times the new level of the Skill. You can only
advance one level at a time. You can learn a new Skill at level 1 for 2 XP. For example, learning Marksmanship 1 (*
*Accuracy + Finesse**) costs 2 XP, and then improving it to level 2 costs 4 XP.

| Current Level | Target Level | XP (AP/Days) Required | Narrative Scale                                                   |
|---------------|--------------|-----------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 0             | **1**        | 2 XP                  | Basic operational familiarity and protocol knowledge.             |
| 1             | **2**        | 4 XP                  | Advanced field expertise; the standard for dedicated specialists. |
| 2             | **3**        | 6 XP                  | Elite master-class execution; recognized sector-level authority.  |

### Traits

Traits are an additional layer of power that can be unlocked by completing specific challenges related to the trait
itself. For example, unlocking the **Draw Two** Trait requires you to Bank at least two  **Gambit** dice on a single
roll, while **Rapid Fire** requires you to perform two successful ranged attacks during a single turn.

After unlocking a Trait, you must master it. Every Trait has a Mastery cost in XP that you must pay before you can gain
its benefits.

# Cell Advancement

While characters can advance their Attributes and Skills, the Corsair Cell also has its own advancement system. The Cell
is both an asset and an investment in the eyes of its Sponsors, and not every Cell is granted the same level of
leeway and trust.

Corsair Cells have a letter grade rank going from D to S, with D being the lowest and S being the highest. Cells are
promoted based on their performance in missions, which is tracked by their **Reputation** score. Once a Cell reaches
high enough standing, it will trigger a Promotion Evaluation. This Evaluation requires each Sponsor to approve the
promotion, which can be automatic in some cases, or requires additional tasks from the Cell. In some events, the Cell is
required to complete an additional mission to qualify for promotion, and their performance in that mission is also
considered. Other times, the Cell may be interviewed about their performance and choices in their recent missions by the
sponsors.

Once a Cell receives a Rank Promotion, it is granted two bonuses. The first is a Discretionary Bonus, which is
distributed amongst the Cell Members to be spent at their discretion, usually in the range of 20,000 to 100,000 Credits
based on the achieved Rank. The second is a Ship Bonus, which is a large sum of credits that's in the range of millions
of Credits. It is strictly restricted to be spent on the Cell's spaceship, but it includes selling their current
ship for full price and buying a new one.

## The Reputation Ladder (Grades D to S)

Reputation is a cumulative pool representing your Cell's standing across the Sphere. Crossing a milestone immediately
triggers a **Rank Evaluation** with your individual Sponsors.

```
                           [ THE REPUTATION LADDER ]
 
 [ 0 - 14 Rep ]   👉 GRADE D: Greenhorns
 [ 15 Rep ]       👉 Evaluation 1 (Unlocks Grade C Clearance)
 [ 16 - 39 Rep ]  👉 GRADE C:  Proven Cells
 [ 40 Rep ]       👉 Evaluation 2 (Unlocks Grade B Clearance)
 [ 41 - 74 Rep ]  👉 GRADE B:  Elite Specialists
 [ 75 Rep ]       👉 Evaluation 3 (Unlocks Grade A Clearance)
 [ 76 - 119 Rep ] 👉 GRADE A: Strategic Icons
 [ 120 Rep ]      👉 The Sovereign Tribunal (Unlocks Grade S Clearance)
 [ 121+ Rep ]     👉 GRADE S: Living Legends

```

## Post-Grade S Prestige

Once a Cell achieves **Grade S**, they have reached the peak of the Sphere's operational hierarchy.

To reward a Grade S crew that continues to take legendary risks, the Sponsors implement a recurring retention loop:

* **The Legend Cycle:** Every time a Grade S Cell accumulates an additional **$+30 \text{ Reputation}$** beyond the 120
  baseline (e.g., hitting 150, 180, 210 Rep), they trigger an automatic **Prestige Evaluation**.
* **The Recurring Payday:** Upon clearing this eval, each character receives a fresh **$\$100,000 \text{ Credits}$
  Discretionary Bonus**, and the ship pool receives an additional **$\$20,000,000 \text{ Credits}$** to fund elite
  maintenance, custom retrofits, or fleet support elements.
* **High Expectations:** Reputation losses are doubled on an S Rank Corsair Cell.

## Mission Economy & Reputation Flux

Reputation is volatile. Successfully completing base goals increases your standing, while political blunders or
operational stagnation strip it away.

### Mission Rewards

* **Tier 1 (Low-Risk / Local Interdiction):** $+1 \text{ Rep}$ Base | $+1 \text{ Rep}$ Sponsor Bonus
* **Tier 2 (Mid-Risk / Cross-Jurisdictional):** $+3 \text{ Rep}$ Base | up to $+2 \text{ Rep}$ Sponsor Consensus
* **Tier 3 (High-Risk / Systemic Crisis):** $+7 \text{ Rep}$ Base | up to $+3 \text{ Rep}$ Asset Preservation

### Operational Failures

* **Objective Failure:** $-2 \text{ to } -5 \text{ Reputation}$ *(Target escapes, asset is destroyed, or crisis
  escalates).*
* **Illegal Activity:** $-3 \text{ to } -8 \text{ Reputation}$ *(Caught operating outside the law).*
* **Sponsor Slight:** $-1 \text{ to } -3 \text{ Reputation}$ *(Doing something that your Sponsors don't like).*

### Idle Penalties

If a full **5-Day Upkeep Cycle** passes and the Cell fails to complete a contract, or increase their Reputation by other
means, their Reputation takes a hit.

### Rank Demotions

When a Cell's Reputation falls below the baseline for their current Rank, they are given a grace Period of one Upkeep
Cycle to recover, starting from the end of the current one. If their Reputations are still below the required level,
they are stripped of their Rank. A cell falling below Grade D is immediately recalled and expelled from the Corsair
Program.

When a Cell regains their Rank, they receive no Ship Funds or Bonuses.
