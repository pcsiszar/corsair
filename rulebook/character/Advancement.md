# Character Advancement

There are various ways characters can improve as they gain experience and Credits to purchase gear. While buying
superior weapons and armor with earned Credits is straightforward, players cannot simply spend money to increase a
character's physical or mental Attributes.

## Experience Points

To increase your attributes, advance your skills, and acquire traits, you need Experience Points (XP). XP is earned in two primary ways: **completing operational missions** and **downtime training**.

### Earning XP from Missions
Active field operations are the primary engine of operative growth. When a Corsair cell successfully completes an operational contract, each participating operative receives a flat award of Experience Points based on the contract's threat tier:

| Contract Tier | Threat Level & Operational Scope | Experience Award (Per Operative) | Reputation Gain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | Local interdictions, precinct security, minor asset recovery | **+4 XP** | $+1 \text{ to } +2 \text{ Rep}$ |
| **Tier 2** | Cross-jurisdictional strikes, corporate black-ops, hostile boardings | **+8 XP** | $+3 \text{ to } +5 \text{ Rep}$ |
| **Tier 3** | High-threat systemic crises, Precursor vault incursions, fleet conflicts | **+12 XP** | $+7 \text{ to } +10 \text{ Rep}$ |

### Earning XP from Training (Downtime)
Between operational missions, operatives can spend time studying tactical theory, practicing firing drills, or conditioning their bodies. Spending **1 Day-to-Day Action Point (AP)** during downtime awards **1 XP**.

* **Strict Daily Limit:** To reflect the biological and cognitive limits of daily conditioning, an operative can earn a **maximum of 1 XP from Training per 24-hour round** (spending at most 1 Day-to-Day AP on training per day). The remaining 2 AP must be allocated to other activities (such as medical rest, starship repairs, freelance odd jobs, shopping, or personal projects).
* **Closing the Gap:** Downtime training serves primarily to close the small experience gap (typically 1 to 2 XP) remaining after an operational mission, allowing an operative to top off the final points needed for a coveted new Trait, Skill rank, or Attribute increase before their next deployment.

### Improving Attributes

To improve an Attribute, you must pay an amount of XP equal to three times the new level of the Attribute. You can only
advance one level at a time. For example, increasing your **Strength** from 4 to 5 costs 15 XP.

| Current Level | Target Level | XP Required | Narrative Scale                                                 |
| ---------------| --------------| -------------| -----------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1             | **2**        | 6 XP        | Basic operational competency.                                   |
| 2             | **3**        | 9 XP        | Standard field agent proficiency.                               |
| 3             | **4**        | 12 XP       | Highly trained veteran specialist.                              |
| 4             | **5**        | 15 XP       | Elite sector-level capability.                                  |
| 5             | **6**        | 18 XP       | Master-class performance / High-end conditioning.               |
| 6             | **7**        | 21 XP       | Peak performance / Heavy specialization.                        |
| 7             | **8**        | 24 XP       | The absolute limit of human and standard cybernetic capability. |

### Learning Skills

To learn or improve a Skill, you must pay an amount of XP equal to two times the new level of the Skill. You can only
advance one level at a time. You can learn a new Skill at level 1 for 2 XP. For example, learning Marksmanship 1 (*
*Accuracy + Finesse**) costs 2 XP, and then improving it to level 2 costs 4 XP.

| Current Level | Target Level | XP Required | Narrative Scale                                                   |
| ---------------| --------------| -------------| -------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 0             | **1**        | 2 XP        | Basic operational familiarity and protocol knowledge.             |
| 1             | **2**        | 4 XP        | Advanced field expertise; the standard for dedicated specialists. |
| 2             | **3**        | 6 XP        | Elite master-class execution; recognized sector-level authority.  |

### Traits

Traits are specialized qualities, tactical reflexes, and operational doctrines that operatives can acquire by spending Experience Points (XP). Unlike Attributes and Skills—which advance along tiered numerical ratings—each Trait is a distinct standalone capability with a fixed XP cost (ranging from 2 XP to 8 XP, as detailed in [Traits](Traits.md)).

Operatives can acquire new Traits during day-to-day downtime by spending earned XP through dedicated training. Once an operative spends the required XP for a Trait, they permanently gain its mechanical benefits. There are no unlock challenges, prerequisite trees, or artificial gatekeeping requirements—operatives have direct, flexible access to any Trait that fits their operational archetype.

## Class Chassis Advancement

In *Corsair*, your **Class** is not an abstract career or permanent biological destiny. Instead, it represents an advanced, proprietary **cybernetic or technological chassis**—such as subcutaneous elemental capacitor plates ([Conduit](../classes/Conduit.md)), a pressurized chemical delivery harness ([Hazard](../classes/Hazard.md)), a neurally tethered exodrone ([Pilot](../classes/Pilot.md)), a morphing kinetic board ([Vanguard](../classes/Vanguard.md)), or an integrated nanite reservoir ([Weaver](../classes/Weaver.md)).

Because a chassis is manufactured hardware interfacing with your neural port, it does not advance through biological experience (XP). Instead, operatives upgrade their chassis by financing precision biomedical retrofits, micro-machined parts, and firmware overclocks with **Credits** during day-to-day downtime.

### Standardized Chassis Upgrade Table
All class chassis follow a standardized upgrade path from Level 1 to Level 5:

| Chassis Level | Benefits Unlocked | Credit Cost | Narrative & Tactical Milestone |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Base Chassis, 7 Attribute Points (base 1/1/1), Starting Package, Class Engine | **Free\*** | Initial commissioning as a licensed Corsair operative. |
| **Level 2** | +1 Class Attribute Point, +1 Ability Package (3 Abilities) | **20,000 Credits** | Field-tested optimization; expands operational toolkit. |
| **Level 3** | +1 Class Attribute Point, +1 Class Mod Slot (Slot 1), +1 Ability Package | **40,000 Credits** | Proven specialist; unlocks first signature hardware overclock. |
| **Level 4** | +1 Class Attribute Point, +1 Ability Package (All Packages Unlocked) | **75,000 Credits** | Elite master-craftsman; complete omnirole tactical flexibility. |
| **Level 5** | +1 Class Attribute Point, +1 Class Mod Slot (Slot 2) | **150,000 Credits** | Peak chassis capability; dual-overclock state of the art. |

*\*The Level 1 chassis selected during character creation is free. Purchasing an additional alternative chassis during downtime costs **100,000 Credits**.*

### Class Mods (Hardware Overclocks)
Class Mods represent high-end firmware alterations and mechanical customizations that bend the chassis's core rules:
* **Fixed Cost:** Each Class Mod costs **25,000 Credits**.
* **Sockets:** Operatives unlock **Mod Slot 1 at Level 3** and **Mod Slot 2 at Level 5**, allowing up to two active mods at a time per chassis.
* **Swapping Mods:** Operatives can freely swap between owned mods during day-to-day downtime at no cost.

### Chassis Ownership & Swapping
Due to metabolic strain and neural bandwidth limits, an operative can have **only one active chassis equipped at a time**. During downtime, an operative can purchase additional class chassis for **100,000 Credits** and swap their active suit. Upgraded levels, attribute ratings, and purchased mods are tied to each physical chassis and are permanently preserved whenever an operative swaps suits.

# Cell Advancement

While characters can advance their Attributes and Skills, the Corsair Cell also has its own advancement system. The Cell
is both an asset and an investment in the eyes of its Sponsors, and not every Cell is granted the same level of
leeway and trust.

Corsair Cells have a letter grade rank going from D to S, with D being the lowest and S being the highest. Cells are
promoted based on their performance in missions, which is tracked by their **Reputation** score. Once a Cell reaches
high enough standing, it will trigger a Promotion Evaluation. This Evaluation requires each Sponsor to approve the
promotion, which can be automatic in some cases, or requires additional tasks from the Cell. In some events, the Cell is
required to complete an additional mission to qualify for promotion, and their performance in that mission is also
considered. Other times, the Cell may be interviewed about their performance and choices in their recent missions by the
sponsors.

Once a Cell receives a Rank Promotion, it is granted two bonuses. The first is a Discretionary Bonus, which is
distributed amongst the Cell Members to be spent at their discretion, usually in the range of 20,000 to 100,000 Credits
based on the achieved Rank. The second is a Ship Bonus, which is a large sum of credits that's in the range of millions
of Credits. It is strictly restricted to be spent on the Cell's spaceship, but it includes selling their current
ship for full price and buying a new one.

## The Reputation Ladder (Grades D to S)

Reputation is a cumulative pool representing your Cell's standing across the Sphere. Crossing a milestone immediately
triggers a **Rank Evaluation** with your individual Sponsors.

```
                           [ THE REPUTATION LADDER ]
 
 [ 0 - 14 Rep ]   👉 GRADE D: Greenhorns
 [ 15 Rep ]       👉 Evaluation 1 (Unlocks Grade C Clearance)
 [ 16 - 39 Rep ]  👉 GRADE C:  Proven Cells
 [ 40 Rep ]       👉 Evaluation 2 (Unlocks Grade B Clearance)
 [ 41 - 74 Rep ]  👉 GRADE B:  Elite Specialists
 [ 75 Rep ]       👉 Evaluation 3 (Unlocks Grade A Clearance)
 [ 76 - 119 Rep ] 👉 GRADE A: Strategic Icons
 [ 120 Rep ]      👉 The Sovereign Tribunal (Unlocks Grade S Clearance)
 [ 121+ Rep ]     👉 GRADE S: Living Legends

```

|--------------|--------------|---------------------------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Grade C**  | **15 Rep**   | $\$20,000 \text{ Credits}$            | $\$5,000,000 \text{ Credits}$    | Regional trade networks, commercial hull modifications, standard heavy ordnance.         |
| **Grade B**  | **40 Rep**   | $\$45,000 \text{ Credits}$            | $\$10,000,000 \text{ Credits}$   | System defense hardware, restricted electronic warfare packages, medium frigate hulls.   |

## Post-Grade S Prestige

Once a Cell achieves **Grade S**, they have reached the peak of the Sphere's operational hierarchy.

To reward a Grade S crew that continues to take legendary risks, the Sponsors implement a recurring retention loop:

* **The Legend Cycle:** Every time a Grade S Cell accumulates an additional **$+30 \text{ Reputation}$** beyond the 120
  baseline (e.g., hitting 150, 180, 210 Rep), they trigger an automatic **Prestige Evaluation**.
* **The Recurring Payday:** Upon clearing this eval, each character receives a fresh **$\$100,000 \text{ Credits}$
  Discretionary Bonus**, and the ship pool receives an additional **$\$20,000,000 \text{ Credits}$** to fund elite
  maintenance, custom retrofits, or fleet support elements.
* **High Expectations:** Reputation losses are doubled on an S Rank Corsair Cell.

## Mission Economy & Reputation Flux

Reputation is volatile. Successfully completing base goals increases your standing, while political blunders or
operational stagnation strip it away.

### Mission Rewards
* **Tier 1 (Low-Risk / Local Interdiction):** $+4 \text{ XP}$ per operative | $+1 \text{ Rep}$ Base (up to $+1 \text{ Rep}$ Sponsor Bonus)
* **Tier 2 (Mid-Risk / Cross-Jurisdictional):** $+8 \text{ XP}$ per operative | $+3 \text{ Rep}$ Base (up to $+2 \text{ Rep}$ Sponsor Consensus)
* **Tier 3 (High-Risk / Systemic Crisis):** $+12 \text{ XP}$ per operative | $+7 \text{ Rep}$ Base (up to $+3 \text{ Rep}$ Asset Preservation)

### Operational Failures

* **Objective Failure:** $-2 \text{ to } -5 \text{ Reputation}$ *(Target escapes, asset is destroyed, or crisis
  escalates).*
* **Illegal Activity:** $-3 \text{ to } -8 \text{ Reputation}$ *(Caught operating outside the law).*
* **Sponsor Slight:** $-1 \text{ to } -3 \text{ Reputation}$ *(Doing something that your Sponsors don't like).*

### Idle Penalties

If a full **5-Day Upkeep Cycle** passes and the Cell fails to complete a contract, or increase their Reputation by other
means, their Reputation takes a hit.

### Rank Demotions

When a Cell's Reputation falls below the baseline for their current Rank, they are given a grace Period of one Upkeep
Cycle to recover, starting from the end of the current one. If their Reputations are still below the required level,
they are stripped of their Rank. A cell falling below Grade D is immediately recalled and expelled from the Corsair
Program.

When a Cell regains their Rank, they receive no Ship Funds or Bonuses.
