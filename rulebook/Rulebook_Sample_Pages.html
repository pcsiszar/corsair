Core Mechanics & The Dice Engine

The Conversational Cycle, Attribute Pairs & The Triad of Tests
"The void does not care how righteous your grievance is, how many credits you are owed, or whether you remembered to double-torque the decompression seal on airlock four. Out past the orbital rings, the only god that answers your prayers is the cold arithmetic of thrust, reaction mass, and kinetic velocity."
— Provost Marshal Corin Vane, Flotilla Maritime Review, 514 AA

03.1 The Conversational Core

In Corsair, the game unfolds through an ongoing, tactile conversation between the players and the Game Master (GM). The players narrate what their characters attempt, how they leverage their equipment, and what words they speak. The Game Master, in turn, portrays the living world—the roar of attitude thrusters, the hum of plasma relays, and the reactions of corporate mercenaries and frontier scavengers.

Most of the time, this dialogue flows naturally without dice. If an operative walks across the pressurized cargo deck and cycles the manual release valve, and there is no ticking alarm or opposing force barring their way, the valve turns. You do not roll dice to breathe, walk, or open unlocked doors.

Dice enter the conversation only when the outcome is genuinely uncertain and failure carries immediate, interesting consequences. When an operative leaps across a vented elevator shaft under heavy PDC fire, attempts to slice an encrypted Flotilla naval transponder before sector security arrives, or lines up a high-velocity slugthrower shot through smoke-choked corridors—we look to the dice.

03.2 Attributes & The Spectrum

Every physical and mental capability in Corsair is represented by a concrete numerical rating known as an Attribute. Ratings range from 1 (baseline) to 5 (peak operative).

◈ System Matrix // The Triads SYS // MATRIX

PHYSICAL TRIAD: Strength (kinetic force, mass, brute endurance), Agility (speed, reflex, acrobatics), Finesse (precision, sleight, dexterity).

MENTAL TRIAD: Knowledge (technical acumen, slicing), Presence (command, resolve), Instinct (intuition, threat awareness).

03.3 The Attribute Pair

When the GM calls for a test, you never roll an isolated stat. Instead, the Game Master designates an Attribute Pair bridging intent with method:

  • Agility + Finesse: Slipping a ceramic blade between the unarmored neck seal of an environmental rig.
  • Strength + Strength: Tearing open a buckled blast door with hydraulic pry bars.
  • Knowledge + Instinct: Spotting an erratic voltage spike in an automated sentry turret's targeting loop.

03.4 The Triad of Tests

Corsair utilizes standard ten-sided dice (d10s). A die result of 8, 9, or 10 constitutes a Hit. Every challenge resolves via one of three testing paradigms:

1. Simple Tests (Binary)

Used when the outcome is strictly pass or fail. Roll a number of d10s equal to the higher Attribute in the pair. If at least one die rolls 8+, the action succeeds. Otherwise, you fail.

2. Success Tests (Degree)

Used when success is assured, but speed, yield, or quality matters. Roll a number of d10s equal to the higher Attribute. The single highest die face rolled becomes your total Success Points.

◈ GM RULING // THE RECOIL PRINCIPLE PROTO // 518

Friction Invariant: Never roll without immediate consequences. In microgravity or vacuum, an unbraced shot or failed leap burns 1 Reaction AP to stabilize.

3. Complex Tests (Binary + Degree)

Standard in combat and high-stakes operations where you must answer both "Did it connect?" and "How hard?":

  1. Step 1 (The Hit): Roll d10s equal to the higher Attribute. You must score at least one Hit (8+) to succeed.
  2. Step 2 (The Quality): Retain a number of dice equal to the lower Attribute. The highest die among those retained becomes your Success Points.

03.5 Upgrades & Downgrades

Situational edges and hazards do not add fiddly flat arithmetic like "+1" or "-2". Tactical conditions alter the physical die type you roll. This mechanic is known as Stepping.

The standard die for all tests is the d10.

  • Upgrades (d10 ➔ d12): Flanking in CQC, using optic stabilizers, or squad teamwork steps dice up to d12s. Because Hits occur on an 8+, d12s offer a massive 41.7% hit probability per die.
  • Downgrades (d10 ➔ d8): Blinding silica smoke, rapid decompression, or heavy suppression steps dice down to d8s. On a d8, only a maximum roll of 8 scores a Hit (12.5% chance).

Upgrades and Downgrades cancel each other out 1-to-1 before you roll. If you have 2 Upgrades and 1 Downgrade, you roll with 1 net Upgrade.

03.6 Gambits: Pushing Luck

When survival hangs on a razor's edge, an operative can commit to a Gambit before rolling. You declare you are pushing your rig, over-charging capacitors, or leaning past ballistic cover.

A Gambit grants an immediate Upgrade. However, if your roll contains any dice that show a 1, you suffer an immediate Complication or Light Stress.

◈ Armor Directive // Cover Facing DEF // PROT

Cover in Corsair is directional and possesses physical facing:

  • Light Cover: +1 Protection (barrel, thin pipe).
  • Medium Cover: +2 Protection (engine block, titanium bulkhead).
  • Heavy Cover: +3 Protection (fortified blast bunker).

Attacks crossing adjacent sides reduce cover by 1 on hexes and 2 on squares. Flanked attacks receive zero benefit.

Section IV · Operational Pacing

Scenes, Pacing & The Action Economy

The 3x3 Token Engine, Zooming Transitions & The End-of-Round Heartbeat

04.1 The Camera Lens: Pacing Tiers

Time in Corsair is never tracked in mindless bookkeeping. Instead, the Game Master acts as a cinematic cinematographer, operating a fluid camera lens that zooms seamlessly between three distinct Pacing Tiers:

1. Moment to Moment (Seconds)

Activated when steel meets steel and kinetic rounds pierce bulkheads. Rounds last roughly 3 to 5 seconds. Positioning on tactical 4x4 meter spaces matters immensely. Every operative receives 3 Action Points (AP) per round to move, aim, fire, or brace.

2. Place to Place (Minutes to Hours)

Used for infiltration sweeps, social interrogations, high-speed chases, and derelict exploration. Rounds represent 5 to 15 minutes. Operatives spend AP to search rooms, slice Shrouds, or tail targets.

3. Day to Day (Shifts & Weeks)

The macro-pacing of interplanetary transit, drydock ship overhauls, medical rehabilitation, and long-term research. Governed by the 24-hour shift cycle in Chapter 9: Downtime.

04.2 The 3x3 Chip Model

Regardless of the zoom level, Corsair uses an identical, tactile token economy: each round, players receive 3 physical Action Point chips.

04.3 The Universal Heartbeat

Every round across all pacing tiers concludes with the End-of-Round Heartbeat. At this exact moment:

  • Countdown Tracks: All active countdown tracks advance by 1 segment.
  • Environmental Ticks: Reactor heat spikes, decompression hypoxia, and toxic contamination apply.
  • AP Reset: Unspent AP clears, and 3 fresh action chips are distributed to each operative.
