Nanite Reservoir

Starting Reserve: 4 Reserve stowed at creation. All nanite actions and weave skills cost 1 Reserve by default.

End of Round Recharge: Spent Reserve recharges at the end of the round.

Nanite Actions

Reserve Costs

Restrictions

Weapons, armor, cover, and healing items are off-limits unless unlocked via Weave skills. Creations/modifications have 0 Load. Action range is your current space.

Disassembly [Free]

Command creations to disassemble at any time for free (0 AP) to instantly regain spent Reserve.
