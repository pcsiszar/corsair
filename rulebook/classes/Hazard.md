# Class: The Hazard

*You can download and print the [Hazard Printable Sheet](Hazard_Sheet.html) for play.*

## Description

The Hazard is the cell’s combat alchemist and chemical warfare specialist, defined by their neural synchronization with a pressurized ChemPack and an intricate network of reinforced subcutaneous fluid manifolds. Mounted between the operative’s shoulder blades, the Catalyst Chamber houses high-pressure reaction vessels, micro-solenoid valves, and high-frequency acoustic mixers that synthesize complex biochemical compounds in fractions of a second. Translucent fluoropolymer conduits trace along the operative’s arms and torso, pulsing rhythmically with luminous reagents as transdermal dermojets and wrist-mounted atomizers prepare volatile mixtures for instant deployment. Often seen wearing a low-profile negative-pressure respirator to withstand their own toxic microclimates, the Hazard operates in an unmistakable aura of ozone, sharp ether, and metallic vapor.

In active combat, the ChemPack functions under principles of rapid binary synthesis and extreme hydraulic pressure. Rather than hauling bulk quantities of unstable compounds, the Hazard stores stabilized base reagents in three hermetic reservoirs, combining them within the central Catalyst Chamber only at the moment of discharge. This internal pressure dictates the potency of every brew: as the operative redlines the chamber, internal atomizers produce hyper-reactive mists, searing incendiaries, and accelerated stimulant cocktails. Yet this power demands disciplined regulation. Pushing the Catalyst Chamber beyond its mechanical tolerance risks catastrophic blowout, violently rupturing safety gaskets and spewing caustic backwash across the operative’s own chassis.

The modular versatility of the ChemPack makes the Hazard an indispensable tactical pivot across all combat scenarios. In heavy firefights, the operative can lay down blinding aerosol screens and adhesive polymer barriers to disrupt enemy advances, dissolve armored bulkheads with hyper-reactive acids, or detonate thermal blasts that scour entrenched firing nests. When cell members fall under heavy fire, the Hazard shifts immediately from biohazard offensive to frontline triage, injecting neuro-metabolic stimulants to restore spent action potential, dosing wounded allies with coagulant biogels, or shocking flatlined comrades back to consciousness. Whatever the operational theater demands, the Hazard controls the biochemical battlefield.

## Attributes

A Hazard's capabilities are governed by three specialized Class Attributes:

* **Venom**: The synthesis and delivery of debilitating neurotoxins, systemic bio-agents, and corrosive acids that degrade enemy resilience from within.
* **Hazard**: The compounding and detonation of volatile, hypergolic, and thermal reagents engineered to deliver catastrophic concussive force on impact.
* **Serum**: The precision compounding and rapid administration of restorative bio-gels, clotting coagulants, and cellular metabolic stimulants.

At character creation, distribute **7 Class Creation Points** across your Venom, Hazard, and Serum Attributes (baseline `1 / 1 / 1`). Raising an Attribute from 1 to 4 costs 1 point per increase; raising an Attribute from 4 to 5 costs 2 points. The maximum starting rating for any Class Attribute is **5**.

## Basic Mechanics

### The Catalyst Chamber & Pressure Engine

The Hazard's abilities are powered by the internal pressure of their ChemPack's Catalyst Chamber. Internal pressure dictates chemical atomization velocity, mixture density, and overall potency:

* **Pressure Range:** Chamber Pressure is measured on a scale from **0 to 3**. Operatives begin every combat encounter at **Pressure 0**.
* **The Volatile Die:** As chamber pressure climbs, internal agitation supercharges your tests with an additional **Volatile Die**. The size of the Volatile Die corresponds directly to your current Pressure:
  * **Pressure 0:** No Volatile Die.
  * **Pressure 1:** **d8** Volatile Die.
  * **Pressure 2:** **d10** Volatile Die.
  * **Pressure 3:** **d12** Volatile Die.
* **Inherent Volatility:** Whenever Pressure is **1 or higher**, rolling your active Volatile Die is **mandatory** on all tests using Hazard Attributes. The Volatile Die cannot be stepped up, stepped down, or modified by standard Upgrades or Downgrades.
* **Test Pool Integration:** In Simple and Success Tests, roll the Volatile Die alongside your standard test pool. In Complex Tests, the Volatile Die is added to your Action Roll pool; if selected as one of your kept dice during the Success Roll, its face value determines your Success Points as normal.

### Rupture Resolution (Chamber Blowout)

Pushing the Catalyst Chamber to high pressure offers immense tactical reward, but risks catastrophic mechanical failure:

* **Triggering a Rupture:** Whenever the Volatile Die rolls a **Hit (8 or higher)** on any test, a **Rupture** occurs immediately.
* **The Hit Counts:** The Hit rolled on the Volatile Die is valid and contributes to the success of your action, Success Points, or Hit-spending effects as normal.
* **Blowout Consequence:** The chamber's emergency pressure seals blow out under the stress. You immediately suffer **2 Light Wounds** (bypassing Physical Protection) and your Chamber Pressure **instantly vents to 0**.

### Regulate Chamber `[Action]`

To ensure you never become tactically deadlocked when your active abilities require specific pressure conditions, you can manually modulate the Catalyst Chamber's valves:

* **Action Cost:** 1 AP
* **Effect:** You manually cycle the ChemPack's pneumatic purge valves or stroke the primer pump. Choose one of the following:
  * **Pump:** Increase Chamber Pressure by **+1** (to a maximum of Pressure 3).
  * **Vent:** Decrease Chamber Pressure by **-1** (to a minimum of Pressure 0).

### Baseline Attribute Actions

Every Hazard Attribute provides a baseline combat application that can be executed without unlocking specific ability kits. Each baseline action costs **1 AP** and standardizes around spending rolled **Hits**:

* **Venom — Debilitating Dose `[Action]`:** Administer a debilitating dose against a creature in your space or an adjacent space (which can be delivered as a melee strike or applied **covertly**, such as a subtle touch or concealed contact), rolling a **Venom Success Test**. For each **Hit** you sacrifice from the roll, apply **1 Downgrade** to that creature. This Downgrade is permanent until treated or removed. A creature can be affected by only one of your Venom Downgrades at a time, and you can maintain a maximum number of active Venom Downgrades equal to your **Venom** Attribute rating.
* **Hazard — Explosive Strike `[Action]`:** Make a melee attack using your **Hazard** Attribute to detonate a pressurized binary charge against a target in your space or an adjacent space. This attack deals your standard melee striking damage plus **bonus damage equal to your Hazard Attribute rating** (and you can spend Hits on effects as normal).
* **Serum — Performance Dose `[Action]`:** Administer a fast-acting metabolic cocktail to yourself or a willing creature in your space or an adjacent space, rolling a **Serum Success Test**. When administered, bind the dose to a specific **Core Attribute** (Strength, Agility, Finesse, Knowledge, Presence, or Instinct). For each **Hit** you sacrifice from the roll, apply **1 Upgrade** that triggers specifically on tests using that chosen Attribute. This Upgrade is permanent until removed or expended. A creature can benefit from only one of your Serum Upgrades at a time, and you can maintain a maximum number of active Serum Upgrades equal to your **Serum** Attribute rating.

## Class Abilities

Hazard abilities are grouped into specialized **Kits**, each containing three synergized chemical techniques. Operatives select **1 starting Kit** at character creation, and unlock **1 additional Kit** at Levels 2, 3, and 4.

Every ability explicitly specifies its **Pressure Cost/Change** and **Action Economy Tag**:
* If an ability's Pressure change would cause your Pressure to exceed **3** or drop below **0**, that ability **cannot be used** (unless modified by hardware such as the *Redline Engine*).

### Medic Kit

The Medic Kit packages rapid-clotting bio-foams, transdermal tissue reconstructors, and defibrillating micro-currents for frontline surgical stabilization.

**First Aid `[Action]`**
* **Attribute:** Serum
* **Pressure Change:** `-1 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 1 AP
* **Effect:** *Pressurized bio-gel seals lacerations and stabilizes hemorrhaging flesh.* Target yourself or a creature in your space or an adjacent space and make a **Serum Success Test**. For every 2 Success Points, heal **1 Light Wound** on the target. In addition, heal a flat number of extra Light Wounds equal to your current **Pressure** level.

**Regenerator `[Action]`**
* **Attribute:** Serum
* **Pressure Change:** `-1 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 1 AP
* **Effect:** *Neuro-calming biogels soothe frayed synapses and alleviate cognitive overload.* Target yourself or a creature in your space or an adjacent space and make a **Serum Success Test**. For every 2 Success Points, heal **1 Light Stress** (Mental Wound) on the target. In addition, heal a flat number of extra Light Stress equal to your current **Pressure** level.

**Defibrillator `[Action]`**
* **Attribute:** Serum / Hazard
* **Pressure Change:** `+2 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 1 AP
* **Effect:** *A high-voltage chemical electrode discharges either restorative stimulus or lethal concussive shock.* Target a creature in your space or an adjacent space:
  * **Unconscious Target (Serum):** Make a **Serum Success Test**. For every 2 Success Points, heal **1 Light Wound**. In addition, if your Pressure is at least 1, heal **1 Aggravated Wound**.
  * **Conscious Enemy (Hazard):** Make a melee attack using **Hazard**. The attack deals standard melee damage plus bonus damage equal to your current **Pressure** level.

### Booster Kit

The Booster Kit delivers aggressive neuro-stimulants, respiratory dilators, and muscular overclocks to shatter biological limits under fire.

**Micro-Booster `[Rider]`**
* **Pressure Change:** `+1 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 0 AP
* **Trigger:** When you or an ally in your space or an adjacent space declare an action.
* **Effect:** *A pressurized transdermal jet releases a micro-dose of hyper-stimulant into the bloodstream.* Add your active **Volatile Die** to all rolls involved in the declared action's test. If the Volatile Die rolls a Hit (8+), it triggers a Rupture on your ChemPack as normal.

**Maximum Effort `[Action]`**
* **Attribute:** Serum
* **Pressure Change:** `-2 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 1 AP
* **Effect:** *Adrenal superchargers flood motor neurons, amplifying raw physical output.* Roll a **Serum Simple Test**. For each **Hit** you sacrifice, grant **+1 Upgrade** to all physical tests made by a creature in your space or an adjacent space until the end of the round.

**Adrenaline Rush `[Action]`**
* **Attribute:** Serum
* **Pressure Change:** `-3 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 1 AP
* **Effect:** *An emergency surge of neuro-stimulants accelerates synaptic transmission to hyper-reflexive speeds.* Roll a **Serum Simple Test**. For each **Hit** you sacrifice, grant **1 temporary Action Point (1 AP)** to an ally in your space or an adjacent space (up to the character maximum of 3 AP). This bonus AP must be spent before the end of the target's current Player Turn or it is lost.

**Envenom `[Rider]`**
* **Attribute:** Venom
* **Pressure Change:** `+1 Pressure`

Additionally, you may sacrifice **Hits** rolled on the attack to apply **1 Venom Downgrade per Hit** to the target (subject to your active Venom limit).

**Decay `[Action]`**

* **Pressure Change:** `-3 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 1 AP
* **Effect:** *A resonant acoustic pulse or catalytic compound triggers violent cellular breakdown in previously dosed targets.* Make a **Venom Success Test**. **Cloudkill `[Action]`**
* **Attribute:** Venom

* **Cost:** 1 AP

For each **Hit** you sacrifice, apply **1 level of a Venom Downgrade** to every creature occupying the target space. The cloud heavily obscures vision into, out of, and through that space until the end of the round.

### Caustic Kit

The Caustic Kit utilizes hyper-concentrated fluorosulfuric acids and molecular solvents to dissolve inorganic armor plating, vehicle chassis, and structural cover.

**Acid Strike `[Action]`**
* **Attribute:** Venom
* **Pressure Change:** `+2 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 1 AP
* **Effect:** *A pressurized burst of fuming acid coats the target, eating away structural seals and composite armor.* Make a melee attack against a creature or object in your space or an adjacent space using **Venom**. For each **Hit** you sacrifice from your roll, you set aside that Hit as an armor-melting effect on the target. You or an ally can expend a set-aside Hit when dealing damage to the target to completely ignore the target's Physical Protection for that instance of damage.

**Caustic Bullets `[Rider]`**
* **Pressure Change:** `+1 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 0 AP
* **Trigger:** When you declare a ranged attack with a Firearm.
* **Effect:** *Chambered ammunition is coated in high-potency molecular acid jackets prior to firing.* The attack **ignores the target's Physical Protection entirely**. However, the weapon loses any standard flat Damage Bonus it would normally provide for this shot.

**Metal Transfer `[Reaction]`**
* **Attribute:** Venom
* **Pressure Change:** `-2 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 0 AP

* **Effect:** *Catalytic bio-conduits siphon dissolved metallic residue from active corrosive agents, instantly flashing a protective metallic film across allied hulls and armor.* Every friendly creature in **Short range (0–5 spaces)** gains **+1 Physical Protection** against the next instance of damage equal to the number of **Hits** you currently have spent and active across your ongoing Venom abilities and Downgrades.

### Ooze Kit

The Ooze Kit synthesizes rapid-curing visco-elastic polymers and non-Newtonian fluids to tether adversaries, restrict limbs, and reshape tactical terrain.

**Rubberband `[Action]`**
* **Attribute:** Serum
* **Pressure Change:** `+1 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 1 AP
* **Effect:** *A high-tensile polymer filament launches from your wrist harness at an anchor point or creature within Short range (0–5 spaces / 16 meters):*
  * **Inanimate Anchor & Terrain:** You are immediately winched in a straight line directly to the anchor point.
  * **Hostile Creature:** Roll a **Serum Simple Test**. If you score at least one Hit, the tether latches on; you can spend any **Hits** from the roll to activate **Push Effects** that pull the target creature 1 space (4 meters) directly towards you per Hit spent (spending a single Hit on this Push Effect still counts the action as a success). Following the pull, you may advance along the tether line any number of spaces towards the target (up to entering its space). If you and the target make contact (ending in the same space), you deal collision damage to the target equal to the highest die rolled.

**Slimeball `[Action]`**
* **Attribute:** Venom
* **Pressure Change:** `-2 Pressure`
* **Cost:** 1 AP
* **Effect:** *A pressurized globule of heavy, quick-curing resin engulfs the target’s extremities.* Make a ranged attack against a target within **Short range (0–5 spaces)** using **Venom**. Instead of dealing damage, sacrifice **Hits** from the roll to establish a **Blocker Effect**:
  * **Locomotion Blocker (Legs/Thrusters):** Negates the target's highest die on movement tests.
  * **Manipulation Blocker (Hands/Weapons):** Negates the target's highest die on weapon attack or manipulation tests.

**Slip and Slide `[Action]`**

### Smoke Kit

The Smoke Kit deploys dense chemical aerosols, thermal-scattering smokescreens, and dual-agent particulate clouds to control visual sightlines and tactical positioning.

**Smoke Screen `[Action]`**

Creatures inside the smoke cannot see out, and creatures outside cannot see in or through. Furthermore, any creature **other than you** within the cloud receives **2 Downgrades** on any test requiring vision. The smoke persists until the end of the round.

During this movement, you ignore Difficult Terrain and can fly. Furthermore, any **Crit Effects** you activate on this test add **+4 Success Points** instead of the standard +2 per Hit spent. You ignore all falling damage until the end of the round.

## Class Mods

Class Mods represent precision hardware overhauls, custom pressure valves, and firmware overrides installed directly onto the ChemPack chassis.

* **Mod Pool & Acquisition:** The Hazard has access to **5 distinct Class Mods**. Each Mod costs **25,000 Credits**.
* **Mod Slots:** Operatives unlock **Mod Slot 1 at Level 3** and **Mod Slot 2 at Level 5**. An operative can install a maximum of **2 Class Mods** simultaneously.
* **Downtime Reconfiguration:** Installed mods can be swapped during downtime pacing.

---

### 1. Dart Launcher
*Wrist-mounted pneumatic dart projector fed directly by subcutaneous reagent manifolds.*
* **Rule Override:** You can execute your baseline Attribute actions (**Venom — Debilitating Dose**, **Hazard — Explosive Strike**, and **Serum — Performance Dose**) against targets within **Short range (0–5 spaces)** instead of requiring melee reach.

### 2. Pressure Controls
*Precision bypass valves and digital solenoid micro-actuators installed along the primary manifold line.*
* **Rule Override:** Whenever you activate a Hazard ability that alters your chamber pressure, you can alter that ability's Pressure cost/change by **`+1`** or **`-1`** (for example, reducing a `+2 Pressure` cost to `+1`, or shifting a `-2 Pressure` cost to `-3` or `-1`). You cannot alter an ability's cost such that it shifts from positive to negative or vice versa.

### 3. Critical Rupture
*Reinforced secondary combustion chambers that siphon kinetic backblast into rolling catalytic detonations.*
* **Rule Override:** Whenever your Volatile Die rolls a **Hit (8 or higher)**, you score the Hit as normal and may immediately choose to **roll the Volatile Die again**. You can continue scoring bonus Hits and rerolling as long as each subsequent roll is a Hit (8+). However, your chamber suffers a Rupture for **each Hit scored this way**—you suffer **2 Light Wounds** and your chamber Pressure vents to **0** for each Hit generated in the chain.

### 4. Catalyst Controls
*Automated multi-chamber pre-mixers capable of pre-pressurizing or purging the ChemPack between engagements.*
* **Rule Override:** At the start of each round during combat (before any actions are taken), you can freely set your Catalyst Chamber Pressure to **any value of your choice** (0, 1, 2, or 3).

### 5. Redline Engine
*Removal of physical mechanical limiters, permitting the Catalyst Chamber to run beyond rated safety tolerances.*
* **Rule Override:** You can activate Hazard abilities even if their positive Pressure cost would push your Chamber Pressure **beyond the maximum rating of 3**. The ability resolves at maximum Pressure (treating your Pressure as 3 for all scaling effects). However, as soon as the action resolves, your ChemPack automatically suffers a **Rupture**: you take **2 Light Wounds** and your Pressure immediately vents to **0**.

## Progression

The ChemPack chassis can be upgraded at specialized cybernetic fabricators and armories by spending Credits. Each upgrade level expands attribute capacity, unlocks advanced Kits, and opens hardware Mod slots.

At character creation, distribute your **7 Class Creation Points** among Venom, Hazard, and Serum (base `1 / 1 / 1`, starting cap 5). You begin with **1 Kit** of your choice.

| Level | Benefits Unlocked | Credit Cost |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| **Level 1** | Base Chassis, 7 Attribute Points (base 1/1/1), Starting Kit, Pressure Engine (Max 3) | **100,000 Credits\*** |
| **Level 2** | +1 Attribute Point, +1 Kit | **20,000 Credits** |
| **Level 3** | +1 Attribute Point, +1 Class Mod Slot (Slot 1), +1 Kit | **40,000 Credits** |
| **Level 4** | +1 Attribute Point, +1 Kit | **75,000 Credits** |
| **Level 5** | +1 Attribute Point, +1 Class Mod Slot (Slot 2) | **150,000 Credits** |

*\*The 100,000 Credit cost for Level 1 applies only when purchasing an additional class chassis after character creation; an operative's starting class is free.*
