Core Mechanics

Pressure Engine: Starts at 0 each encounter (range 0–3). Abilities alter Pressure up/down; cannot exceed bounds. Rolling Volatile Die is mandatory on Hazard tests when Pressure ≥ 1.

Rupture (Blowout): When the Volatile Die rolls a Hit (8+), the Hit counts, but the chamber ruptures: suffer 2 Light Wounds and Pressure instantly vents to 0.

Regulate Chamber [Action] (1 AP): Manually stroke pump (+1 Pressure) or purge valve (−1 Pressure) without rolling.

Baseline Actions [Action] (1 AP, space/adjacent):

  • Venom (Debilitating Dose): Success Test (can be covert). Sacrifice Hits to apply 1 Downgrade per Hit (permanent until treated, max active = Venom).
  • Hazard (Explosive Strike): Melee strike. Deals base striking damage + bonus damage equal to Hazard Attribute.
  • Serum (Performance Dose): Success Test. Bind to Core Attribute; sacrifice Hits for 1 Upgrade per Hit to tests using that Attribute (max active = Serum).

Class Modifications (25,000 Cr · Max 2)

Act Mod Effect
Dart Launcher You can execute your baseline Attribute actions (Venom, Hazard, and Serum) against targets within Short range instead of melee reach.
Pressure Controls You can alter the Pressure cost/change of any Hazard ability by +1 or −1 (cannot invert positive/negative).
Critical Rupture On a Volatile Die Hit (8+), score the Hit and reroll. Keep scoring Hits and rerolling while 8+. Suffer 2 LW and vent to 0 for each Hit scored.
Catalyst Controls At the start of each combat round, freely set your Catalyst Chamber Pressure to any value of your choice (0, 1, 2, or 3).
Redline Engine You can activate abilities exceeding max Pressure (3). Resolves at Pressure 3, then triggers an immediate Rupture (2 LW, vents to 0).
