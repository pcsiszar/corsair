Core Mechanics

Pressure Engine: Starts at 0 each encounter (range 0–3). Abilities alter Pressure up/down; cannot exceed bounds. Rolling Volatile Die is mandatory on Hazard tests when Pressure ≥ 1.

Rupture (Blowout): When the Volatile Die rolls a (8+), the Hit counts, but the chamber ruptures: suffer 2 and instantly vents to 0.

Regulate Chamber [Action] (1 ): Manually stroke pump (+1 ) or purge valve (−1 ) without rolling.

Baseline Actions [Action] (1 , space/adjacent):

  • Venom (Debilitating Dose): Success Test (can be covert). Sacrifice to apply 1 /Hit (permanent until treated, max active = Venom).
  • Hazard (Explosive Strike): Melee strike. Deals standard striking damage + bonus damage equal to Hazard Attribute.
  • Serum (Performance Dose): Success Test. Bind to Core Attribute; sacrifice for 1 /Hit to tests using that Attribute (max active = Serum).
