Kinetic Engine & Form Rules

Auto-Shift: Rolling a Vanguard Attribute or activating an ability auto-transforms your Board into that Form immediately prior to resolving, counting as a Form Move.

Gain Boost: Spend 1 to swap forms OR bank (8+). Max 1 banked (d10).

Spend Boost: Add banked d10 to any Vanguard test roll, or power combo abilities.

Golden Rule: You may either spend OR gain a during a test/action, but never both!
