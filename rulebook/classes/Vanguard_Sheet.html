Board Forms

Shield: Phys Protection +SHD/2 (rounded down). Melee weapon (STR + SHD, +lower attr Dmg). Use SHD to contest.

Glider: Use GLD in movement. Ignore difficult terrain & fall damage. Free hands.

Cannon: Ranged weapon attacks. Acc = CNN at short, decreases by 1 per range. 2 hands occupied.

Form Rules: Abilities shift into their Form unless requiring a stance (e.g. Repulse: Shield) or setup (e.g. Turbo Slam: Glider Move; Meteor Crash: 4m+ height).

Gain Boost: Spend 1 AP to swap forms, OR bank a Hit (8+) from a Vanguard test roll (max 1 die banked).

Spend Boost: Add d10 to any Vanguard test, or power abilities. Cannot gain and spend Boost on same test.

Class Modifications

Act Mod Effect
Twin Turbo Can hold up to two Boost dice at the same time.

Hesitation Moves Qualify for Form Move requirements if performed Form Move this Round (not Turn).
Boost Buildup At end of Player Turn, gain Boost if you haven't swapped Forms this turn.
