Board Forms

Shield: Phys Protection +SHD/2 (rounded down). Shield is melee weapon (STR + SHD). Use SHD to contest.

Glider: Use GLD in movement. Ignore difficult terrain & fall damage. Free hands.

Cannon: Ranged weapon attacks. Acc = CNN at short, decreases by 1 per range. 2 hands occupied.

Switching: Swap form instantly when taking test with corresponding Vanguard Attribute.

Gaining Boost: Roll Vanguard test without Boost. Set aside one die showing 8+ to bank as Boost die. Max 1.

Revving Up: Success test to swap forms and bank *any* die as Boost.
