# Class: The Conduit

*You can download and print the [Conduit Printable Sheet](Conduit_Sheet.html) for play.*

## Description

The Conduit is the cell’s living capacitor and high-intensity shock trooper, defined by total neuro-synaptic integration with subcutaneous capacitor plates and a dense network of subdermal energy conduits. Mounted along the operative’s spine and major limb axes, these layered graphene-dielectric plates interface directly with motor nerve clusters and cardiovascular lines. Translucent fluoropolymer conduits trace along the operative’s limbs and torso, glowing with sharp chromatic pulses—radiant amber, sub-zero cerulean, or electric violet—that correspond to their current electromagnetic and thermal saturation. Thermoelectric Peltier arrays and micro-vent heat sinks embedded across the shoulders continuously dissipate ambient thermal bleed, surrounding the Conduit in a shimmering haze of superheated air, drifting ice vapor, or crackling static discharge.

In active combat, the Conduit operates under hard-science principles of rapid energy accumulation, capacitive storage, and directed discharge. Rather than relying on external power cells, the operative’s internal plates draw kinetic recoil, thermal friction, and metabolic potential, converting them into a high-density internal charge. As these capacitor banks saturate through tactical Amp maneuvers, the Conduit channels this power into devastating physical and technical feats—accelerating muscle contraction for near-instant bounds, flash-freezing deckplates, or discharging high-voltage arcs across enemy formations. Yet this stored power creates immense systemic tension: holding peak voltage strains internal insulation and elevates core temperatures, demanding that the operative periodically vent their fully saturated plates in catastrophic, encounter-shifting Burst releases.

The modular versatility of the Conduit chassis makes them an invaluable asset across every operational theater. An operative can tune their capacitor discharge to deliver high-yield explosive breaching charges and searing thermal barrages to crack armored strongpoints, flash-freeze corridors into impenetrable crystalline barricades to secure a defensive retreat, or defibrillate flatlined cell members with calibrated neuro-electric pulses during frantic firefights. When technical systems fail, the Conduit can overcharge friendly electronics, fabricate temporary tactical gear from polarized plate residue, or surge bodily across the battlefield to dismantle enemy squads. Whatever the mission requires, the Conduit controls the flow of battlefield energy.

## Attributes

A Conduit's capabilities are governed by three specialized Class Attributes:

* **Flame**: The generation and vectoring of high-thermal incendiary blasts, concussive pressure waves, and kinetic combustion.
* **Frost**: The rapid endothermic extraction of heat, cryogenic crystallization, and molecular structural stabilization.
* **Lightning**: The accumulation and discharge of high-voltage galvanic arcs, electromagnetic pulses, and synaptic acceleration.

At character creation, distribute **7 Class Creation Points** across your Flame, Frost, and Lightning Attributes (baseline `1 / 1 / 1`). Raising an Attribute from 1 to 4 costs 1 point per increase; raising an Attribute from 4 to 5 costs 2 points. The maximum starting rating for any Class Attribute is **5**.

## Basic Mechanics

A Conduit's internal energy saturation constantly interfaces with the physical environment, enabling subtle narrative manipulations, devastating close-quarters combat, and dynamic tactical synergies.

### Minor Touch Effects

Even without a test, you can channel low-voltage currents, focused heat, or endothermic siphons through your bare hands to produce minor narrative effects:

* **Flame**: Warm an object, ignite a pilot light or fuse, dry wet fabric, thaw thin frost, or cauterize a minor surface abrasion.
* **Frost**: Chill a beverage, flash-freeze standing water, solidify a viscous chemical puddle, or soothe a localized burn.
* **Lightning**: Deliver a mild static shock, illuminate a low-voltage LED indicator, ground static interference on a console, or clear visual distortion from a monitor.

### Melee Combat

You can channel raw elemental energy through your fists, limbs, and close-quarters weapons, making melee attacks using your Conduit Attributes paired with their corresponding physical Core Attributes:

* **Flame (Thermal / Incendiary):** Favored pair **Flame + Strength**. Unleash concussive kinetic blasts and searing thermal strikes, directing explosive force with muscular might.
* **Frost (Cryogenic Piercing):** Favored pair **Frost + Finesse**. Wield and drive crystalline ice daggers into structural seams and joint seals with pinpoint precision.
* **Lightning (Galvanic Arc):** Favored pair **Lightning + Agility**. Blitz opponents with high-voltage arcing strikes and rapid-fire blows, using electrical potential to augment raw speed.
* **Favored Pair Damage Bonus:** When you make a melee attack using the weapon's favored Attribute Pair, you gain a damage bonus equal to the **lower attribute** in that pair.

### Effect Synergies

Channeling specific elemental frequencies through your Conduit Attributes enhances the potency of standard tactical Effects:

* **Flame**: When taking a test that uses your Flame attribute, your **Crit Effects** grant **3 Success Points** instead of the standard 2 per Hit sacrificed.
* **Frost**: When taking a test that uses your Frost attribute, any **Blocker Effects** or **Downgrade Effects** you establish become **Sustained Effects** until the end of the round, resetting after each affected attempt until overcome or cleared.
* **Lightning**: When taking a test that uses your Lightning attribute, your **Chain Effects** also increase your total **Success Points by +1**, and you can spend any dice that rolled **7 or higher** to activate them instead of the normal 8 or higher.

### The Charge Engine

Conduit abilities revolve around the dynamic accumulation and venting of **Charges** held within your subcutaneous capacitor plates:

* **Capacity & Starting State:** Your capacitor plates can hold a maximum of **3 Charges**. You begin every combat encounter at **0 Charges**.
* **Amp Abilities (Charge Generators):**
  * Amp abilities channel raw energy into your plates, building up elemental Charge while delivering immediate tactical advantages.
  * Activating an Amp ability with 0 Charges grants you **1 Charge** of that ability's element type.
  * Activating an Amp ability of the *same* element as your current Charges increases your stored **Charges by 1** (up to the maximum of 3 Charges).
  * Activating an Amp ability of a *different* element than your current Charges immediately resets your stored Charges to 0 before granting 1 Charge of the new element (unless modified by Class Mods).
  * You cannot activate an Amp ability if you already hold the maximum of 3 Charges for that element (unless modified by *Infinity*).
  * You can only activate a single Amp ability per action.
* **Burst Abilities (Charge Venters):**
  * Burst abilities vent your fully saturated capacitor plates in devastating, encounter-defining releases.
  * Burst abilities can only be activated when you hold **exactly 3 Charges** (unless modified by *Finality*).
  * You can only activate the Burst variant corresponding to your currently stored elemental Charge.
  * Activating a Burst ability consumes and vents **all 3 Charges**, resetting your stored Charges to 0.

## Class Abilities

Conduit abilities are organized into **7 thematic packages**, each featuring a paired **Amp Ability** and **Burst Ability** with distinct Flame, Frost, and Lightning branches. Operatives select **1 starting package** at character creation, and unlock **1 additional package** at Levels 2, 3, and 4.

Every ability explicitly declares its **Action Economy Tag**:
* **`[Rider]`**: Costs **0 additional AP**; enhances or triggers during an existing action (such as a firearm attack, melee strike, or movement action).
* **`[Reaction]`**: Costs **0 AP**; triggered in response to an external event (such as being targeted by an incoming attack). Limited to once per turn unless noted.
* **`[Action]`**: Costs **1 AP**; requires rolling a test or deploying an immediate tactical effect.

### 1. Ranged Package

The Ranged Package integrates subcutaneous capacitor lines directly with chambered ammunition and ballistic rails, infusing firearm projectiles with elemental payload before venting residual energy in devastating area bombardments.

**Amped Shot `[Rider]`**
* **Cost:** 0 AP
* **Trigger:** When declaring a ranged attack with a Firearm.
* **Effect:** *Capacitor shunts bleed raw energy directly into the chambered round as the firing pin strikes.* You gain **+1 Charge** of the chosen element and apply the matching effect to the attack:
  * **Flame:** The weapon gains a flat **+1 Damage Bonus per active Charge** (including the Charge just gained) as superheated thermal jackets detonate on impact.
  * **Frost:** You may reroll a number of dice across all rolls in the test equal to your **active Charges** (including the Charge just gained), freezing your targeting reticle onto structural weak points.
  * **Lightning:** When activating your first **Chain Effect** during the attack test, you can target a number of additional creatures equal to your **active Charges** (including the Charge just gained) without expending additional ammunition.

**Blast Burst `[Action]`**
* **Resource Cost:** Requires and consumes 3 Charges
* **Cost:** 1 AP
* **Effect:** *Capacitor plates vent their entire reserve through external projector nozzles in a catastrophic ranged detonation.* Target a space within **Short range (0–5 spaces)** and make a **Conduit Success Test** using the attribute matching your consumed Charges:
  * **Flame:** Searing incendiary blast waves scour the impact zone. You inflict thermal damage equal to your **Success Points + your Flame Attribute** to every creature occupying an **8-meter radius** centered on the target space.
  * **Frost:** A pressurized cryogenic shockwave flash-freezes the atmosphere. You inflict cryogenic damage equal to your **Success Points + your Frost Attribute** to every creature caught in a **90-degree cone, 12 meters long**, originating from your space. In addition, every space caught within the cone becomes **Difficult Terrain** until the end of the round.
  * **Lightning:** A piercing bolt of stepped-leader plasma lances forward. You inflict galvanic damage equal to your **Success Points + your Lightning Attribute** to up to **three chosen creatures** in your space, the target space, or any space intersecting the straight line of fire between them. You can activate **Chain Effects** on this roll to increase the number of targets.

* **Resource Cost:** Requires and consumes 3 Charges
* **Cost:** 1 AP

You instantly arc-discharge through the target, reappearing in any unoccupied space up to **8 meters away**; all hostile creatures caught in the line between your starting space and destination take damage equal to your **Lightning Attribute**.

### 3. Mobility Package

The Mobility Package siphons stored energy into myofibril micro-servos and personal propulsion vents, converting raw electrical and thermal voltage into blinding battlefield traversal.

**Amped Sprint `[Rider]`**
* **Cost:** 0 AP

* **Resource Cost:** Requires and consumes 3 Charges
* **Cost:** 1 AP

You may use any remaining movement after the attack to disengage freely. ## Class Mods

Class Mods represent advanced dielectric overclocks, auxiliary capacitor banks, and firmware polarity overrides installed directly into the Conduit’s chassis.

* **Mod Pool & Acquisition:** The Conduit has access to **5 distinct Class Mods**. Each Mod costs **25,000 Credits**.
* **Mod Slots:** Operatives unlock **Mod Slot 1 at Level 3** and **Mod Slot 2 at Level 5**. An operative can install a maximum of **2 Class Mods** simultaneously.
* **Downtime Reconfiguration:** Installed mods can be swapped among those owned during downtime pacing.

---

### 1. Duality
*Secondary dielectric capacitor splitter installed along the spinal manifold, isolating twin storage channels.*
* **Rule Override:** You can store Charges of **two different elements simultaneously**. Your maximum capacitor capacity remains **3 Charges** (shared between both elements). When you activate an Amp ability of an element you already hold, but your capacitor is at maximum capacity (3 Charges), you override one charge of the other element. When you activate an Amp ability of an element you do not hold while already holding two different elements, you choose which existing element's charges are lost. When you activate a Burst ability, you consume **all stored charges**, venting the Burst variant corresponding to whichever element holds the majority of your charges (your choice in case of a split).

### 2. Infinity
*Dynamic load-dump coils and thermo-resistive bleed shunts that safely bleed excess voltage.*
* **Rule Override:** You can continue to activate Amp abilities even when you already hold the **maximum of 3 Charges** for an element. The Amp ability resolves its full active mechanical effect (such as bonus damage, rerolls, extra movement, or defense), but your stored Charges remain capped at 3 without resetting or overloading.

### 3. Intensity
*Kinetic-piezoelectric recovery filaments woven through myofibril sheaths to siphon combat momentum into storage.*
* **Rule Override:** Whenever you score a **Hit (8 or higher)** on any test using your Conduit Attributes, you may sacrifice that Hit to **generate 1 Charge** of the corresponding element type, up to your maximum capacity.

### 4. Finality
*Emergency capacitor bypass circuits permitting premature dielectric discharge under dire circumstances.*
* **Rule Override:** You can activate a Burst ability even when you hold **fewer than 3 Charges** (including at 0 Charges). However, triggering a premature discharge violently trips internal safety breakers: you consume all current charges, and you **cannot gain Charges of any element until the end of the round**.

### 5. Ingenuity
*Microsecond polarity inverter and thermal phase-shift controller installed at the primary manifold junction.*
* **Rule Override:** Whenever you activate an Amp ability of a different element than the one you currently hold, you do not lose your stored charges. Instead, you can choose to **convert all your existing Charges into the new element type**, retaining your current charge count and increasing it by +1 as normal.

## Progression

The Conduit chassis can be upgraded at specialized cybernetic armories and neuro-clinics by spending Credits during downtime. Each upgrade level expands attribute capacity, unlocks advanced ability packages, and opens hardware Mod slots.

At character creation, distribute your **7 Class Creation Points** among Flame, Frost, and Lightning (base `1 / 1 / 1`, starting cap 5). You begin with **1 Ability Package** of your choice.

| Level | Benefits Unlocked | Credit Cost |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| **Level 1** | Base Chassis, 7 Attribute Points (base 1/1/1), Starting Package (Amp & Burst), Charge Engine (Max 3) | **100,000 Credits\*** |
| **Level 2** | +1 Attribute Point, +1 Ability Package | **20,000 Credits** |
| **Level 3** | +1 Attribute Point, +1 Class Mod Slot (Slot 1), +1 Ability Package | **40,000 Credits** |
| **Level 4** | +1 Attribute Point, +1 Ability Package | **75,000 Credits** |
| **Level 5** | +1 Attribute Point, +1 Class Mod Slot (Slot 2) | **150,000 Credits** |

*\*The 100,000 Credit cost for Level 1 applies only when purchasing an additional class chassis after character creation; an operative's starting class is free.*
