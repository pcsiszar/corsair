### Class: The Monarch

This is a class that is all about mental abilities. The enabling piece of gear is a dermal implant that causes shards
to form around the body, most typically the head. Most users form these in the shape of a crown, hence the name.

Each shard of the user is tied to a specific attribute of the class, and their number is equal to the Attribute's value.

The attributes the class uses are the following:

- Awareness: Abilities to perceive information in another person's mind and senses.
- Guidance: Abilities that help an ally.
- Tyranny: Abilities that hinder an opponent.

The shards are detachable at will, and can exert their effects to a single target per shard in the 2-meter vicinity of
the shard. To select a target for the shard, and to use them, the Monarch has to be within 20 meters, when the shard is
within 2 meters of the intended target.

You can throw any amount of shards to a single designated space up to 20 meters away, for 1 AP.

A shard can be autimatically retrieved if the Monarch is within a 2 meter radius and it's path isn't completely blocked.

A shard can be regrown for 1 segment of physical and mental condition track. If you would go above the maximum number
of shards for a specific type, the first shard you used of the type that was regrown becomes permanently inactive.

Each attribute has linked special abilities that rely on the given attribute.
A shard linked to a given attribute can only use an ability of their own attribute.
The Monarch can only use one ability per shard in range of their target.
The Monarch can only target one person at a time with their abilities.
An ability with a persistent effect stays active until:

- The target leaves the 2-meter range of the shard.
- The monarch leaves the 20-meter range of the shard.
- The monarch uses an ability on a different target.

#### Awaraness Abilities

Unless specified otherwise, Awareness abilities either require a Simple Test with Awareness + Awareness if used by
themselves, or they can be used in tandem with a different attribute when the information obtained via the ability helps
to perform some action.

Awareness abilities do not alert their targets to the fact that they are under their effect.

- Sense Memory: Allows the Monarch to view a memory that their target is currently recalling.
- Sense Vision: Allows the Monarch to see the same thing their target sees.
- Sense Audio: Allows the Monarch to hear the same thing their target hears.
- Sense Emotions: Allows the Monarch to read their target's current mood. They receive detailed information on what they
  are feeling, but the Monarch doesn't receive information as to the target of those emotions.
- Sense Character: Allows the Monarch to get a brief glimpse into their overall personality. This will not be an
  objective description, rather how the target perceives themselves.
- Sense Danger: When the target would perform an intentional harmful action, if the Monarch contests the roll, they get
  Upgrades equal to their Awareness attribute.

#### Guidance Abilities

Grant abilities only work on willing targets and require no rolls. Unless specified otherwise, activating a Grant
ability is free. The Monarch and the target of the Grant ability can communicate telepathically and can share their
senses with each other, so both of them hear and see and feel the same thing. Unless stated otherwise, Grant abilities
can only be activated during the Player Turn

- Grant Awareness: Allows the target to share the benefit of an ongoing Awareness ability as if they were the Monarch,
  without ending the effect of the ability.
- Grant Charisma: Allows the target to use the Monarch's Charisma in place of their own.
- Grant Knowledge: Allows the target to use the Monarch's Knowledge in place of their own.
- Grant Instinct: Allows the target to use the Monarch's Instinct in place of their own.
- Grant Action: Allows the Monarch during the Player Turn to give a command to an ally and transfer 1 AP to them. The
  target must immediately follow that command and make an action, or lose the AP they were given.
- Grant Warning: When an ally would contest a test, the Monarch can pay the AP cost of contesting the test instead of
  the target.

#### Tyranny Abilities

Tyranny abilities, unless stated otherwise, require a Complex Test and generally result in an Upgrade/Downgrade, or a
Blocker effect.

- Suppress Memory: Blocks the target from remembering something he knows. If they are the target of Sense Memory, you
  are able to recollect the information, while the target isn't.
- Distort Confidence: The target either becomes overwhelmed by extreme confidence or lack thereof. They can only gain
  Upgrade or Downgrade effects from the result of your test. If they are overwhelmed by doubt, they gain Downgrades to
  feats of Charisma due to lack of inhibition, but the same Upgrades to feats of Instinct, due to being overly cautious.
- Morph Tactics: The target either is forced to either focus on attacking or defending when in combat. If forced into
  a defensive mindset, you can create a blocker or downgrade effect on offensive actions that target a hostile creature,
  but they receive an equivalent Upgrade effect to their defensive moves, including Contests. If forced into an
  aggressive mindset, the reverse applies.
