## Media & Literary Inspirations

* **The Expanse:** For grounded orbital physics, Brachistochrone trajectories, the physical reality of reaction mass and heat management, and the cold indifference of the hard vacuum.
* **Cowboy Bebop:** For the lived-in grit of worn starships, eccentric bounty hunters scraping together fuel credits, and the jazz-paced rhythm of freelance frontier life.
* **Mass Effect:** For a macro-scale engineered galaxy, ancient precursor ruins, rich lineage cultures, and the camaraderie of a specialist crew facing the unknown.
* **Guardians of the Galaxy:** For found-family crews surviving on sheer stubbornness, loyalty, and eccentric flair in an overwhelmingly vast cosmos.
* **Terry Pratchett:** For the grounded, wry, and deeply observant human perspective on politics, bureaucracies, and ordinary people dealing with extraordinary cosmic machinery.
