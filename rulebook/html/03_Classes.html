Classes & The MANTLE System

In Corsair, your Class is not an abstract RPG profession, cultural caste, or permanent biological destiny. Instead, it represents an advanced, proprietary MANTLE (Modular Adaptive Neural Transmission Linked Equipment)—a specialized, aerospace-grade cybernetic and technological chassis engineered for high-intensity tactical operations across planetary gravity wells and the void.

While civilian spacers and commercial security guards rely on standard vac-suits and off-the-shelf ballistic armor, Corsair operatives and fleet specialists wear MANTLEs. These fully integrated tactical rigs synchronize directly with the wearer's central nervous system, translating split-second subconscious impulses into overwhelming kinetic, thermal, chemical, or molecular force.

1. The Neural Control Chip (NCC)

At the heart of every MANTLE rig lies the Neural Control Chip (NCC). Permanently fused into the base of the operative's skull and cervical spine, the NCC interfaces directly with major motor nerve clusters and sensory pathways through an array of sub-dermal optical conduits and cranial shunt ports.

Operating a MANTLE is not like picking up a rifle or donning a set of body armor; it requires extensive neuro-physical conditioning, biometric calibration, and mental discipline. When an operative jacks into their chassis, the NCC establishes a closed-loop telemetry bridge:

  • Reflex Synchronization: Actuator servos, capacitor banks, and manifold solenoids respond to motor intentions at the speed of neuro-chemical impulse—bypassing the mechanical latency of physical triggers or levers.
  • Sensory Telemetry: The operative feels the condition of their chassis as a natural extension of their physical anatomy. A Conduit feels electrostatic charge prickling along their peripheral nerves; a Hazard perceives fluid pressure within their Catalyst Chamber as visceral chest tension; a Pilot senses external drone hull-stress as direct proprioceptive pressure.
  • Air-Gapped Isolation: Because early neural experiments in human history proved vulnerable to electronic intrusion, all NCC architectures utilize strict, air-gapped optical buses. A MANTLE never communicates with external wireless networks without hardware-isolated firewalls, protecting the operative's brain from cyber-warfare intrusion or rogue signal resonance.

Once surgically installed and bonded to the operative's spinal column, the Neural Control Chip remains a permanent part of the character's body, ready to interface with any authorized MANTLE chassis they acquire.

2. Origins & Discovery

The technology underpinning modern MANTLE rigs was born out of survival and desperation across two centuries of Newcomer history.

The Vault Discoveries & Early Covenant (14–60 AA)

Following the founding of Crossroads and the historic alliance with Stormdrinker on Nimbus, early Newcomer researchers venturing into dormant Precursor Vaults discovered fragments of ancient, air-gapped optical-neural bus architecture. While the cosmic purpose of these relics was kept contained under the Covenant's charter to prevent cosmic beacons from firing, cyberneticists studied their inert conductive pathways, laying the theoretical groundwork for bio-compatible neural interfaces.

The Scavenger Incursions & Abe-Redmane Skunkworks (315–320 AA)

In 315 AA, the peaceful expansion of the Fume cluster was shattered by the Scavenger Incursions—brutal raider armadas composed of biologically modified Scav clans. When Scav boarding parties sacked Cloudspire and ambushed civilian transports, Newcomer defense militias were outmatched in close-quarters void combat by the raiders' terrifying biological reaction speeds.

Facing fleet extinction, Commander Velen Redmane rallied an independent coalition of spacers and engineers at the Port Zenith Orbital Engineering Yards, suspended in the upper atmosphere of Landfall. Redmane commissioned Chief Fleet-Engineer Kira Abe to weaponize vault-derived neural bus conduits into an emergency combat harness.

Working around the clock in Port Zenith’s zero-gravity skunkworks, Abe’s team created the first generation of closed-loop MANTLE prototypes. By linking heavy tactical systems directly into the wearer’s motor reflexes, strike teams could match and exceed Scav agility in zero-g boarding actions. Equipped with these experimental rigs, Redmane’s coalition routed the raiders at the decisive Battle of Fume Rings (320 AA), leading directly to the birth of the Flotilla and the signing of the Skyline Accords (335 AA).

In the two centuries following the Battle of Fume Rings, MANTLE technology evolved through continuous refinement across dozens of production blocks. Early military engineers incorporated dragon biology research—studying the efficient bio-electric resonance of dragon scales and nerve tissue—alongside new discoveries extracted from deep Precursor vaults and hard-won industrial telemetry from the Drift Wars. By the time of the Frontier War (485–508 AA), the MANTLE had matured into the defining tactical platform of modern void combat.

3. The 518 AA Reality

In the present day of 518 AA—ten years after the Fall of the Evergaol and the signing of the Great Truce—the production, possession, and maintenance of MANTLE rigs are shaped by post-war realities.

MANTLE Acquisition Pipelines (518 AA)
  • 1. Sponsor Candidate Pipelines: Official patron underwrites NCC surgery and issues a certified fleet rig during elite training, pending Oversight Committee charter.
  • 2. The Freelancer Network: Grassroots fraternity of Corsair veterans and dockside armorers passing down repaired, surplused, or salvaged frames to promising aspirants.
  • 3. Post-War Salvage & Black Markets: Decommissioned war stock or battlefield salvage from the Frontier War repaired in orbital chop-shops and retrofitted with jailbroken firmware.

Flotilla Control & Legal Status

Under the Law of the Void, the Flotilla High Council classifies combat-grade MANTLEs as Tier-1 Restricted Military Hardware. Licensed production is tightly restricted to major orbital naval foundries (such as the Bastion Yards at Port Zenith), and unauthorized possession in orbital transit lanes carries severe criminal penalties. However, under the Skyline Accords (335 AA), planetary governments hold sovereign authority within their atmospheric gravity wells, allowing surface colonies, private corporations, and frontier syndicates to license and deploy their own proprietary variants.

For commissioned Corsairs, possessing a top-tier MANTLE is an official entitlement of their charter. When an operative joins a chartered cell, their individual Sponsor (Flotilla, Colonial administration, Megacorporation, Union, or Private patron) legally registers and underwrites their chassis, granting them cross-jurisdictional authorization to carry lethal tactical hardware across both orbital space and planetary soil.

Uncommissioned Freelancers (Aspirants) acquire starting frames through sponsor candidate pipelines, the informal Freelancer Network of veterans and dockside armorers, or restored Frontier War battlefield salvage calibrated in dockside cyber-clinics.

4. The Five Bespoke Chassis

While every MANTLE interfaces through the standardized Neural Control Chip, physical chassis are not interchangeable modular shells. Each class represents an entirely bespoke, dedicated full-body frame engineered from aerospace alloys, micro-servos, and specialized tactical hardware:

  • The Conduit: Engineered as a living capacitor suit, featuring spine-mounted graphene-dielectric plates, subcutaneous energy conduits, and shoulder Peltier thermoelectric heat sinks that vent excess thermal and cryogenic bleed during high-voltage Amp and Burst cycles.
  • The Hazard: An armored chemical warfare harness housing a pressurized thoracic Catalyst Chamber, micro-solenoid mixing valves, hermetically sealed reagent reservoirs, and chemical-resistant fluoropolymer conduits routed to transdermal dermojets.
  • The Pilot: A motorized bio-mechanical exoskeleton frame equipped with cranial high-frequency telemetry uplinks, heavy solid-state battery banks, reinforced skeletal shock-absorbers, and dorsal magnetic docking clamps that host an autonomous, articulated Exodrone.
  • The Vanguard: A high-mobility kinetic assault platform featuring articulated dorsal locking collars, calf-to-spine inertial balance nodes, and micro-servo shock mounts built to catch, wield, and redirect the tremendous torque of a morphing multi-segment kinetic Board.
  • The Weaver: An advanced molecular fabrication chassis centered around thoracic titanium-ceramic reservoirs housing millions of microscopic alloy nanites, governed by high-frequency magnetic confinement conduits and fluid-fabric kinetic shrouds.

5. Neuro-Physical Invariants

Single Active Chassis Limit (Synaptic Saturation)

Because the Neural Control Chip establishes a comprehensive bidirectional map between the operative's motor cortex, autonomic nervous system, and suit telemetry, the biological brain can only sustain one active chassis mapping at a time.

Attempting to cross-link or equip multiple MANTLE frames simultaneously causes immediate sensory confusion and neurological overload—a life-threatening condition known as Synaptic Burnout that results in violent motor seizures, cardiovascular shock, and permanent neural scarring. Consequently, an operative can have only one MANTLE chassis equipped and active at any time.

Class Attributes & Starting Creation

Every MANTLE chassis is governed by three specialized Class Attributes (e.g., Flame, Frost, Lightning for the Conduit; Venom, Hazard, Serum for the Hazard; Power, Speed, Precision for the Pilot).

  • Starting Values: All three Class Attributes begin at a baseline of 1 (1 / 1 / 1).
  • Creation Budget: At character creation, spend 7 Class Creation Points across your three Class Attributes.
  • Point Costs: Increasing an Attribute from 1 to 4 costs 1 point per step; raising an Attribute from 4 to 5 costs 2 points. Maximum starting rating is 5.

Downtime Acquisition & Swapping (100,000 Cr)

Operatives are not locked into a single chassis for their entire career. During campaign downtime, an operative can purchase an additional bespoke MANTLE chassis for 100,000 Credits.

This substantial investment reflects the true cost of acquiring an aerospace-grade military frame on the open or grey market, purchasing proprietary firmware licenses or black-market decryption keys, and spending an extended shift in an orbital cyber-clinic to undergo neuro-pod recalibration. During recalibration, the operative's NCC is flushed and flashed with the telemetry profiles of the new frame, preventing synaptic rejection.

Preserved Investment: Attribute ratings, unlocked ability packages, and purchased class mods belong permanently to each specific chassis. When an operative spends downtime swapping from their Conduit MANTLE to their Vanguard MANTLE in the ship's armory, their Vanguard attributes and unlocked combos remain exactly as they left them.

Class: The Conduit

The Conduit is the cell’s living capacitor and high-intensity shock trooper, defined by total neuro-synaptic integration with a specialized Conduit-pattern MANTLE (Modular Adaptive Neural Transmission Linked Equipment). Controlled directly through the operative's permanent Neural Control Chip (NCC) and cranial shunt, the rig synchronizes layered graphene-dielectric capacitor plates and a dense network of subdermal energy conduits mounted along the operative’s spine and major limb axes. Translucent fluoropolymer conduits trace along the limbs and torso, glowing with sharp chromatic pulses—radiant amber, sub-zero cerulean, or electric violet—that correspond to their current electromagnetic and thermal saturation. Thermoelectric Peltier arrays and micro-vent heat sinks embedded across the shoulders continuously dissipate ambient thermal bleed, surrounding the Conduit in a shimmering haze of superheated air, drifting ice vapor, or crackling static discharge.

In active combat, the Conduit operates under hard-science principles of rapid energy accumulation, capacitive storage, and directed discharge. Rather than relying on external power cells, the operative’s internal plates draw kinetic recoil, thermal friction, and metabolic potential, converting them into a high-density internal charge. As these capacitor banks saturate through tactical Amp maneuvers, the Conduit channels this power into devastating physical and technical feats—accelerating muscle contraction for near-instant bounds, flash-freezing deckplates, or discharging high-voltage arcs across enemy formations. Yet this stored power creates immense systemic tension: holding peak voltage strains internal insulation and elevates core temperatures, demanding that the operative periodically vent their fully saturated plates in catastrophic, encounter-shifting Burst releases.

The modular versatility of the Conduit MANTLE chassis makes them an invaluable asset across every operational theater. An operative can tune their capacitor discharge to deliver high-yield explosive breaching charges and searing thermal barrages to crack armored strongpoints, flash-freeze corridors into impenetrable crystalline barricades to secure a defensive retreat, or defibrillate flatlined cell members with calibrated neuro-electric pulses during frantic firefights. When technical systems fail, the Conduit can overcharge friendly electronics, fabricate temporary tactical gear from polarized plate residue, or surge bodily across the battlefield to dismantle enemy squads. Whatever the mission requires, the Conduit controls the flow of battlefield energy.

Attributes

A Conduit's capabilities are governed by three specialized Class Attributes:

  • Flame: The generation and vectoring of high-thermal incendiary blasts, concussive pressure waves, and kinetic combustion.
  • Frost: The rapid endothermic extraction of heat, cryogenic crystallization, and molecular structural stabilization.
  • Lightning: The accumulation and discharge of high-voltage galvanic arcs, electromagnetic pulses, and synaptic acceleration.

At character creation, distribute 7 Class Creation Points across your Flame, Frost, and Lightning Attributes (baseline 1 / 1 / 1). Raising an Attribute from 1 to 4 costs 1 point per increase; raising an Attribute from 4 to 5 costs 2 points. The maximum starting rating for any Class Attribute is 5.

Basic Mechanics

Minor Touch Effects

Even without a test, you can channel low-voltage currents, focused heat, or endothermic siphons through your bare hands to produce minor narrative effects:

  • Flame: Warm an object, ignite a pilot light or fuse, dry wet fabric, thaw thin frost, or cauterize a minor surface abrasion.
  • Frost: Chill a beverage, flash-freeze standing water, solidify a viscous chemical puddle, or soothe a localized burn.
  • Lightning: Deliver a mild static shock, illuminate a low-voltage LED indicator, ground static interference on a console, or clear visual distortion from a monitor.

Melee Combat

You can channel raw elemental energy through your fists, limbs, and close-quarters weapons, making melee attacks using your Conduit Attributes paired with their corresponding physical Core Attributes:

  • Flame (Thermal / Incendiary): Favored pair Flame + Strength. Unleash concussive kinetic blasts and searing thermal strikes, directing explosive force with muscular might.
  • Frost (Cryogenic Piercing): Favored pair Frost + Finesse. Wield and drive crystalline ice daggers into structural seams and joint seals with pinpoint precision.
  • Lightning (Galvanic Arc): Favored pair Lightning + Agility. Blitz opponents with high-voltage arcing strikes and rapid-fire blows, using electrical potential to augment raw speed.
  • Favored Pair Damage Bonus: When you make a melee attack using the weapon's favored Attribute Pair, you gain a damage bonus equal to the lower attribute in that pair.

Effect Synergies

Channeling specific elemental frequencies through your Conduit Attributes enhances the potency of standard tactical Effects:

  • Flame: When taking a test that uses your Flame attribute, your Crit Effects grant 3 Success Points instead of the standard 2 per Hit sacrificed.
  • Frost: When taking a test that uses your Frost attribute, any Blocker Effects or Downgrade Effects you establish become Sustained Effects until the end of the round, resetting after each affected attempt until overcome or cleared.
  • Lightning: When taking a test that uses your Lightning attribute, your Chain Effects also increase your total Success Points by +1, and you can spend any dice that rolled 7 or higher to activate them instead of the normal 8 or higher.

The Charge Engine

Conduit abilities revolve around the dynamic accumulation and venting of Charges held within your subcutaneous capacitor plates:

  • Capacity & Starting State: Your capacitor plates can hold a maximum of 3 Charges. You begin every combat encounter at 0 Charges.
  • Amp Abilities (Charge Generators):
    • Amp abilities channel raw energy into your plates, building up elemental Charge while delivering immediate tactical advantages.
    • Activating an Amp ability with 0 Charges grants you 1 Charge of that ability's element type.
    • Activating an Amp ability of the same element as your current Charges increases your stored Charges by 1 (up to the maximum of 3 Charges).
    • Activating an Amp ability of a different element than your current Charges immediately resets your stored Charges to 0 before granting 1 Charge of the new element (unless modified by Class Mods).
    • You cannot activate an Amp ability if you already hold the maximum of 3 Charges for that element (unless modified by Infinity).
    • You can only activate a single Amp ability per action.
  • Burst Abilities (Charge Venters):
    • Burst abilities vent your fully saturated capacitor plates in devastating, encounter-defining releases.
    • Burst abilities can only be activated when you hold exactly 3 Charges (unless modified by Finality).
    • You can only activate the Burst variant corresponding to your currently stored elemental Charge.
    • Activating a Burst ability consumes and vents all 3 Charges, resetting your stored Charges to 0.

Class Abilities

Conduit abilities are organized into 7 thematic packages, each featuring a paired Amp Ability and Burst Ability with distinct Flame, Frost, and Lightning branches. Operatives select 1 starting package at character creation, and unlock 1 additional package at Levels 2, 3, and 4.

Every ability explicitly declares its Action Economy Tag:

  • [Rider]: Costs 0 additional AP; enhances or triggers during an existing action (such as a firearm attack, melee strike, or movement action).
  • [Reaction]: Costs 0 AP; triggered in response to an external event (such as being targeted by an incoming attack). Limited to once per turn unless noted.
  • [Action]: Costs 1 AP; requires rolling a test or deploying an immediate tactical effect.

The Catalyst Chamber & Pressure Engine

The Hazard's abilities are powered by the internal pressure of their ChemPack's Catalyst Chamber. Internal pressure dictates chemical atomization velocity, mixture density, and overall potency:

Pushing the Catalyst Chamber to high pressure offers immense tactical reward, but risks catastrophic mechanical failure:

Class: The Vanguard

The Vanguard is the cell's frontline breaker and kinetic specialist, defined by their neural synchronization with a specialized Vanguard-pattern MANTLE (Modular Adaptive Neural Transmission Linked Equipment) and its adaptable multi-segment Board. Controlled directly through the operative's permanent Neural Control Chip (NCC) and cervical shunt, the rig interfaces with subdermal balance nodes embedded along the user's calves, spine, and forearms. Mounted along the operative's spine via a high-torque magnetic docking latch, this articulated piece of aerospace-grade hardware consists of interlocking ceramic-composite plates, micro-servos, and pneumatic locking pins that read minute shifts in center of mass, transmitting physical balance feedback at the speed of reflex. In its dormant state, the folded chassis rests as a compact, streamlined dorsal carapace across the operative's back, often scarred by shrapnel deflection or thermal wash from high-speed descents.

In active combat, the Board functions as an instrument of relentless momentum, transforming between three distinct operational geometries at a moment's notice. When facing suppressive fire, the chassis snaps forward into the Vanguard's off-hand, locking into a broad, reinforced Shield engineered to disperse heavy ballistic impacts and batter through enemy barricades in close-quarters breaches. When rapid repositioning or flanking becomes necessary, the mechanism deploys beneath the user's boots as a hoverboard Glider, engaging vectored thrusters to skim over hazardous terrain, traverse zero-gravity gaps, and launch the operative into aerial drop attacks. When distant threats demand decisive force, the Board hinges outward into a heavy shoulder-slung or hip-braced Cannon, cycling heavy penetrator rounds capable of punching clean through bulkhead plating and mechanized armor.

The true tactical depth of the Vanguard MANTLE chassis lies in the fluid, stance-dancing rhythm of its kinetic engine. Rather than committing to a single combat posture, an experienced Vanguard continuously reconfigures the Board mid-assault, converting the mechanical torque of each form swap into banked Boost dice that fuel explosive combo techniques. Whether shattering enemy defensive lines with a boosted shield charge, surfing over crossfire to extract a pinned ally, or laying down devastating artillery blasts from elevated vantage points, the Vanguard commands the pace of engagement. Their presence ensures the cell possesses an immovable defensive anchor, an agile vanguard scout, and a heavy fire-support platform unified in a single operative.

Switching & Swapping Forms

The Board is an adaptable kinetic weapon chassis capable of rapid mechanical reconfiguration. Its operational state is governed by two core concepts:

  • Current Form & Shifting Forms: Whenever you roll a test that uses one of your Vanguard Attributes (such as a melee attack with Shield, a Movement Action with Glider, or a ranged attack with Cannon) or activate a Vanguard ability, your Board automatically shifts into that Form immediately prior to resolving the action. You remain in that form until your Board transforms again. Taking a test with a Vanguard Attribute or executing an ability inherently counts as a Form Move for that Form during the current turn.
  • Specific Form & Setup Requirements: While most abilities automatically shift your Board into their associated Form upon activation, certain abilities have specific prerequisites:
    • Required Starting Form: Reactions and defensive stances (such as Repulse and Brick Wall) require you to already be in that specific Form when triggered.
    • Form Moves: Kinetic combo follow-ups (such as Turbo Slam) require a preceding Form Move to build the necessary momentum.
    • Situational Requirements: Aerial maneuvers (such as Meteor Crash) require specific environmental positioning (e.g. being at least 4 meters above your target space).

Boosts & Form Momentum

To channel kinetic momentum into explosive combos, the Vanguard banks kinetic energy as Boost dice. A Boost is a single banked d10 die. You can hold only one Boost die at a time in your reserve (unless increased by Class Mods like Twin Turbo). Banked Boost dice power signature Vanguard combo finishers and riders, or bolster regular actions.

Gaining a Boost: There are two ways to generate and bank a Boost die:

  1. Active Form Swap (1 AP): Spend 1 AP to swap into any of your three forms (Shield, Glider, or Cannon). Swapping immediately places you in that form, counts as a Form Move, and grants a banked d10 Boost die (replacing any previously held Boost).
  2. Banking a Hit: When rolling a test using a Vanguard Attribute while holding no Boost die, you may bank one Hit (8+) from the roll into your reserve as a d10 Boost die (that die does not contribute to successes or effects).

Spending a Boost: Spend your Boost die by rolling it and adding it to any Vanguard test pool (before or after the roll). Many abilities also consume your active Boost die to unleash devastating kinetic strikes, high-altitude maneuvers, or heavy artillery blasts.

The Golden Rule of Boosts: You can either spend or gain a Boost during a test or action, but never both. You cannot gain a Boost on an action where you spent a Boost, and a newly gained Boost is banked to fuel future actions.

The Vanguard reconfigures their Board into coordinated combat routines called Combos. Each Combo contains three synergized abilities: an opening setup, a devastating finisher, and a responsive tactical rider or passive.

Class: The Weaver

The Weaver is the cell’s master of molecular manipulation and on-the-fly fabrication, defined by total neural command over a specialized Weaver-pattern MANTLE (Modular Adaptive Neural Transmission Linked Equipment) housing millions of microscopic Nanites. Controlled directly through the operative's permanent Neural Control Chip (NCC) and cranial shunt, the rig centers around pressurized titanium-ceramic reservoirs anchored to the thoracic spine and routed across the body through subdermal micro-manifolds. Visually, a Weaver cuts an unmistakable, iridescent silhouette. Dormant nanite fluid constantly cycles across their person, pooling into shifting metallic scarves, flowing cloaks, or reinforced layers of kinetic weave that shimmer with subtle chromatic pulses. With a single neuro-synaptic impulse, these fluid fabrics can instantly solidify into razor-sharp monomolecular blades, lock into rigid ballistic plating, or atomize into an airborne cloud of micro-mechanical dust.

In active combat, the Weaver's reservoir operates under hard-science principles of programmable matter and high-frequency magnetic confinement fields. Billions of microscopic, multi-jointed alloy nodes rapidly interlock at the molecular level, allowing the Weaver to fabricate tools, forge weapons, or reinforce structural bulkheads in mere seconds. The experience of commanding the swarm is intensely tactile; operatives describe a distinct hum along their nerve endings as high-density telemetry streams back through their shunt port, conveying the structural integrity, surface tension, and molecular density of their creations in real time. Rather than carrying cumbersome bags of situational gear, the Weaver carries raw structural potential, reshaping their metallic fluid to solve logistical hurdles or tactical crises as they unfold.

The modular versatility of the Weaver MANTLE chassis makes it the ultimate force multiplier across all operational theaters. An operative can tune their nanite suspension into solid-state ballistic shields and physical armor to anchor a defensive perimeter, forge high-leverage melee blades or precision-calibrated long guns, weave aerodynamic aerofoils for effortless 3D flight, or flood allied tissue with cellular micro-sutures and clotting agents for emergency field triage. In technical environments, the nanite swarm can infiltrate sealed data ports and electronic consoles, short-circuiting security networks from the inside out. Whatever the mission demands, the Weaver ensures the cell is never without the right tool for the job.

Attributes

A Weaver's capabilities are governed by three Class Attributes:

  • Create: Molecular structural synthesis, solidifying dormant nanite fluid into functional physical tools, shields, and armaments.
  • Control: Telemetric dynamic manipulation, guiding active swarms and directing forged constructs across spatial vectors.
  • Modify: Precision lattice bonding, infusing existing equipment, physical structures, and organic tissue with adaptive nanite layers.

Basic Mechanics

When utilizing nanite capabilities, you roll tests using your Weaver Attributes. By default, your raw nanites can only create, modify, or manipulate simple objects that do not possess combat statistics or specialized properties—such as basic prybars, ropes, lockpicks, simple furniture, or utilitarian covers. Forging functional weapons, ballistic armor, medical consumables, or electronic viruses requires unlocking the corresponding Nanite Weaves.

To execute a nanite test, you declare your narrative action and pair your chosen Weaver Attribute with an appropriate Core Attribute (Class + Core), or with another Weaver Attribute (Class + Class). For example, combine Create + Strength to forge a heavy prybar and wrench open a jammed hatch, pair Create + Finesse to fabricate a micro-lockpick and align security tumblers, roll Control + Instinct to thread a nanite sensory cable beneath a blast door, or use Modify + Presence to subtly alter the identification text and seal on an executive credential.

The Nanite Reservoir

Every Weaver maintains a physical reserve of programmable matter, measured in Reserve:

  • Baseline Capacity: Operatives start with a capacity of 4 Reserve.
  • Physical Volume: A single point of Reserve corresponds to roughly 1 liter of concentrated nanite fluid held within the operative's pressurized dorsal canisters and subcutaneous manifold lines.
  • Material Scale & Density: Stretched to its structural limits, 1 Reserve can produce a 24-meter cable, a 1-square-meter rigid surface, or handheld items equivalent to Load 1–2:
    • 1 Reserve: Handheld tools, cables, grappling hooks, 1H weapons, simple containers (Load 1–2).
    • 2 Reserve: Ladders, chairs, full hazard suits, 2H firearms, deployable barriers (Load 3–4).
    • 3 Reserve: Reinforced benches, heavy 1m × 1m blast walls, pressurized airlocks (Load 5–6).
  • Workspace Limitation: You deploy nanites in the space you currently occupy, unless an ability extends range.
  • Load Invariant: All items forged purely from nanites have an effective Load of 0. Modifications applied to existing equipment preserve that equipment's original Load.
  • Instant Disassembly [Free Action]: Decouple, liquefy, and siphon creations back into your reservoir at any time for 0 AP, instantly recovering spent Reserve.
  • Reserve Cycles: When spent Reserve recharges at the start of next round or end of turn, it is actively circulating and cannot be allocated until the duration elapses.

Class Abilities

Weaver abilities are grouped into thematic packages called Nanite Weaves. Each Weave is a tripartite suite containing three specialized applications—one for Create, one for Modify, and one for Control. Operatives select one starting Weave at character creation, and unlock an additional Weave at Levels 2, 3, and 4.

HardWeave

HardWeave configures your nanite swarm into high-density interlocking crystalline lattices engineered to absorb catastrophic kinetic shock and deflect incoming fire.

HardWeave Shield [Action]

  • Attribute: Create
  • Cost: 1 Reserve, 1 AP
  • Effect: Micro-crystallized plates interlock across your forearm to form a rigid ballistic barrier. You forge a physical shield in your space for yourself or an ally. It functions as a 1H shield (Min STR 0, Load 0) granting a base +1 Physical Protection Bonus. Roll an Action Roll using Create. Each Hit spent increases the shield's Protection Bonus by +1.

HardWeave Armor [Action]

  • Attribute: Modify
  • Cost: 1 Reserve, 1 AP
  • Effect: Adaptive fluid seeps into seams and weave layers, hardening upon impact to distribute concussive force. You reinforce worn armor on yourself or an ally in your space. Roll an Action Roll using Modify. Each Hit spent increases the armor's Physical Protection by +1 until the end of the round.

HardWeave Block [Reaction]

  • Attribute: Control
  • Cost: 1 Reserve (recharges at the start of the next round)
  • Trigger: When you or an ally in your space are targeted by an attack.
  • Effect: A reactive burst of pressurized nanites flashes outward to deflect an incoming projectile or blade. You can contest the incoming attack for 0 AP, rolling your Contest Roll using your Control Attribute instead of your standard physical Attributes.

SliceWeave

SliceWeave aligns your nanite swarm into high-density cutting geometries, honing edges to monomolecular sharpness capable of parting composite armor and bone with equal ease.

SliceWeave Blade [Action]

  • Attribute: Create
  • Cost: 1 Reserve, 1 AP
  • Effect: Metallic fluid coalesces into an ergonomic hilt and extends a razor-honed blade of solid carbide alloy. You forge a melee weapon in your space for yourself or an ally. It functions as a 1H melee weapon (Min STR 0, Load 0, base Damage Bonus +1). When forged, declare its damage property: Piercing (favored pair Agility + Finesse), Slashing (favored pair Agility + Strength), or Striking (favored pair Strength + Finesse). Roll an Action Roll using Create. Each Hit spent increases the weapon's flat Damage Bonus by +1.

SliceWeave Edge [Action]

  • Attribute: Modify
  • Cost: 1 Reserve, 1 AP
  • Effect: A vibrating monomolecular ribbon bonds along the striking edge of an existing weapon. You reinforce a melee weapon in your space. Roll an Action Roll using Modify. Each Hit spent increases the weapon's flat Damage Bonus by +1 until the end of the round.

SliceWeave Slash [Action]

  • Attribute: Control
  • Cost: 1 Reserve (recharges at the start of the next round), 1 AP
  • Effect: A scything ribbon of high-velocity nanites lashes across the air in a lethal, sweeping arc. Select your current space or one adjacent space and roll a Control Success Test. You inflict Slashing damage equal to your Success Points + your Control Attribute to all designated targets in the chosen space. You may spend additional available Reserve (which recharges at the start of the next round) to include one additional space per Reserve spent.

BoltWeave

BoltWeave structures nanites into high-tolerance magnetic accelerator barrels, tailored propellant chambers, and aerodynamically perfected kinetic sabots.

BoltWeave Weapon [Action]

  • Attribute: Create
  • Cost: 1 Reserve, 1 AP
  • Effect: Fluid alloy solidifies into a shoulder-fired long gun with calibrated magnetic rails and an optical rail. You forge a firearm in your space for yourself or an ally. It functions as a 2H firearm (Min STR 0, Load 0) with a base weapon profile: Acc: [3 / 2 / 1 / 0], RoF: 1, Dmg: +1. Roll an Action Roll using Create. Each Hit spent allows you to calibrate the firearm by selecting one of the following options (you cannot choose the same option twice; different Accuracy band pairs count as separate options):
    • Increase flat Damage Bonus by +2.
    • Increase Rate of Fire (RoF) by +2.
    • Increase two adjacent Accuracy bands by +1 each (cannot increase a single band by +2).

BoltWeave Ammo [Action]

  • Attribute: Modify
  • Cost: 1 Reserve (recharges at the start of the next round), 1 AP
  • Effect: Chambered ammunition is coated in high-mass sabot jackets and terminal guidance fins. You infuse and calibrate the ammunition of an ally occupying your space. Roll an Action Roll using Modify. Each Hit spent grants that ally's firearm attacks your choice of the following benefits until the end of the round:
    • +1 Damage Bonus.
    • +1 Rate of Fire (RoF).
    • +1 Accuracy in a single range band (this cannot grant the ability to fire at a range band the weapon cannot naturally engage, marked -).
    The spent Reserve recharges at the start of the next round.

BoltWeave Bullet [Action]

  • Attribute: Control
  • Cost: 1 Reserve (recharges at the start of the next round), 1 AP
  • Effect: Pressurized reservoir coils launch a dense, hyper-velocity slug directly from your harness. Make a ranged attack against a creature or object within Short range (0–5 spaces), rolling a Control Success Test. You inflict kinetic damage equal to your Success Points + your Control Attribute.

FloatWeave

FloatWeave integrates aerostatic micro-turbines, magnetic repulsion baffles, and aerodynamic surface foils into your constructs to conquer vertical terrain and master 3D movement.

FloatWeave Infusion [Passive]

  • Attribute: Create
  • Effect: Internal counter-grav nodes and micro-thrusters allow forged items to hover weightlessly. Any item or weapon you create with your nanites gains integrated levitation. These creations hover freely within your space, move alongside you without requiring hands to carry, and can be wielded or operated hands-free using your Control or Create Attribute in place of the normal required Core Attribute (such as firing a hovering BoltWeave rifle or striking with a floating SliceWeave blade).

FloatWeave Wings [Action]

  • Attribute: Modify
  • Cost: 1 Reserve, 1 AP
  • Effect: Articulated aerofoil wings of layered composite feather-plates unfold from the recipient's shoulders. You attach wings to yourself or an ally in your space. The recipient gains Flight (base speed 4 meters, ascending costs double, ignores Difficult Terrain, and hovers in place). The recipient is immune to falling damage while the wings remain attached. When the recipient takes a movement action, they may execute their movement by rolling a Success Test using your Modify or Control Attribute.

FloatWeave Wind [Action]

  • Attribute: Control

  • Effect: A howling micro-vortex of directed air and swirling nanites lifts you bodily into the air. Until the end of your turn, you gain Flight and can use your Control Attribute on all Movement Actions.

LifeWeave

LifeWeave programs microscopic bio-medical assembler nodes capable of cellular reconstruction, accelerated tissue granulation, and synthetic neural bridging.

LifeWeave Elixir [Action]

  • Attribute: Create
  • Cost: 1 Reserve, 1 AP
  • Effect: Nanites suspend themselves in a concentrated metabolic nutrient fluid inside a sealed autoinjector. You synthesize a consumable injector (Load 0). When a creature consumes the elixir as an Action, the user rolls a Create Success Test using your Create rating. For every 2 Success Points, the user heals 1 Light Wound or 1 Light Stress.

LifeWeave Heal [Action]

  • Attribute: Modify
  • Cost: 1 Reserve (recharges at the start of the next round), 1 AP
  • Effect: Active medical micro-swarms flood an open wound to cauterize vessels and knit torn flesh. You inject medical nanites directly into an injured creature occupying your space. Roll a Modify Success Test. For every 3 Success Points, you heal 2 Light Wounds on the target.

LifeWeave Enhancement [Passive]

  • Attribute: Control
  • Effect: Subdermal nanite filaments weave through muscle fibers and neural pathways to augment physical and mental performance. Any creature affected by your other LifeWeave abilities can substitute your Control Attribute in place of any Core Attribute on their tests until the end of the round.

MirageWeave

MirageWeave commands nanites to align their surfaces into microscopic refractive lenses, sound-absorbing baffles, and adaptive pigments that bend light, alter physical appearances, and deaden acoustic signatures.

MirageWeave Infusion [Passive]

  • Attribute: Create
  • Effect: Adaptive photonic metamaterials and micro-pigments reshape the surface of your fabrications. You can alter the visual texture, markings, and finish of any item you create to make it appear authentic, legitimate, or disguised. You may use your Create Attribute on any tests made while utilizing the fabricated item. Furthermore, whenever you roll to Create an object, you may sacrifice Hits from your Action Roll to establish a Sustained Blocker Effect (1 Blocker die per 2 Hits sacrificed) that opposes any attempts by hostile creatures to visually spot, detect, or identify the item.

MirageWeave Coating [Action]

  • Attribute: Modify
  • Cost: 1 Reserve, 1 AP
  • Effect: A shimmering, light-refracting film crawls across skin, armor, and gear to reshape silhouettes or mimic identities. You coat a creature or object in your space. You can cosmetically alter the target's physical appearance, colors, uniforms, insignia, or facial features to disguise them. You can also roll Modify on tests relying on the coated creature or object to remain unseen or relying on other creatures to believe your disguise is authentic. Additionally, you may roll an Action Roll using Modify; for every 2 Hits sacrificed, you establish a Sustained Blocker Effect (1 Blocker die per 2 Hits sacrificed) opposing all attempts to perceive, detect, or target the coated subject from outside its space.

MirageWeave Veil [Action]

  • Attribute: Control
  • Cost: 1 Reserve (recharges at the start of the next round), 1 AP
  • Effect: An obscuring aerosol cloud of blackout nanites and optical chaff billows outward to shield your cell. You disperse a mobile veil of refractive nanites that moves with you and covers your current space, shielding everyone within it. Roll an Action Roll using Control. For every 2 Hits sacrificed, you establish a Blocker Effect (1 Blocker die per 2 Hits sacrificed) against incoming ranged attacks and visual detection from outside the space. This veil lasts until the beginning of the next Player Turn, but instantly breaks and dissipates if any creature makes an attack from within the veil.

CyberWeave

CyberWeave configures nanites into conductive micro-circuitry, logic gates, and sensor diodes capable of infiltrating and dominating electronic systems.

CyberWeave Drone [Action]

  • Attribute: Create
  • Cost: 1 Reserve, 1 AP
  • Effect: You assemble a palm-sized hexapod crawler drone in your space. Roll a Create Success Test. For every 2 Success Points, allocate a +1 bonus between its Interfacing or Sensors ratings. The drone moves up to 20 meters per round and can slice terminals or transmit telemetry. It disassembles if damaged or moving beyond 40 meters.

CyberWeave Virus [Action]

  • Attribute: Modify
  • Cost: 1 Reserve, 1 AP
  • Effect: You infect an electronic terminal, locked blast door, or sensor node in your space. Rolling Modify, you force the device to execute a standard function, display a false error, or short-circuit its primary bus. You can re-trigger this effect remotely within 40 meters.

CyberWeave Swarm [Action]

  • Attribute: Control
  • Cost: 1 Reserve, 1 AP
  • Effect: You deploy an atmospheric sensor cloud (4-meter radius within 20 meters). Roll a Control Success Test: every 2 Success Points grants +1 Upgrade to all allied ranged attacks made against targets within the cloud. The swarm can be repositioned as an Action for 0 Reserve.

Class Mods

Weaver Class Mods cost 25,000 Credits each (socketed at Levels 3 and 5, max 2 active):

  • Extra Reserve: Gain +1 Reserve (increasing baseline reservoir capacity from 4 to 5).
  • Reborn Weave: When rolling a Success Roll on Weaver tests, disassemble an active Creation/Modification enhanced with Hits to score those Hits and 8 Success Points automatically.
  • Revitalizing Weave: When recycling a Creation/Modification, also regain a spent Reserve that would have recharged at the end of the turn.
  • Woven Bond: Gain +1 Upgrade on any test made while using equipment or weapons you have Created or Modified.
  • Weave Infusion: Spend 1 additional Reserve on a Weave test to add +3 Success Points or score an additional Hit.

  • The Armament Smith: Invests in Create paired with BoltWeave and HardWeave. Forges customized sniper rifles and ballistic barriers on demand, arming the entire cell from a single reservoir.
