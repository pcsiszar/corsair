# Open Play Contracts

These are contracts that are meant to be resolved quickly without too many rolls. You can sprinkle these in whenever you
want to give your players options to choose from.

---

**Sanctioned Contract:** *Landfall - Find the Survey Team in the Exclusion Zone - 7,500 CR*

*Landfall and the Flotilla have a standing agreement that they regularly send survey teams to study the Exclusion Zone.
The latest team has not returned. Enter the Exclusion Zone and find the missing survey team.*

---

**Sanctioned Contract:** *Landfall - Arrest the Poachers in the Wildlife Reserve - 5,500 CR*

*Landfall detected unknown spacecraft entering the Wildlife Reserve. High likelihood of poachers. Extra payment for
capturing the hunters alive, but the primary objective is the preservation of wildlife.*

---

**Sanctioned Contract:** *Nimbus - Investigate for Evidence of Corporate Espionage - 7,500 CR*

*The authorities on Nimbus are keen to overlook wrongdoings on the corporations' part. We have received intel that a
local subsidiary of Redfeather Aerospace is being targeted by a local rival. Find out if that's really the case, and the
ones who are responsible.*

---

**Sanctioned Contract:** *Nimbus - Capture Gun Shipment, then pose as Smugglers - 12,500 CR*

*The Nimbus authorities learned of a local gang arranging a shipment of weapons to be smuggled to Nimbus. The details of
the meet are known only a select few, one of which is onboard the ship, for which we know the transponder code for.
Intercept the vessel and interrogate the smugglers. Then force them to give the go-ahead for the meetup and make contact
with the local authorities. Further instructions will be relayed later, but expect to play the role of the smugglers as
long as necessary to draw the target out.*

---

**Sanctioned Contract:** *Pelagia - Explore underwater Precursor site - 15,000 CR*

*The local SRC team meant to dive underwater to explore the site received threats from a local cult known as the
Children of the Leviathan. The SRC requests a Corsair escort both for potential Precursor security installations and
attacks from cultists.*

---

**Sanctioned Contract:** *Pelagia - Arrest Rayalana Cer'xi - 6,500 CR*

*Rayalein Cer'xi is the daughter of the land baron Numaraia Cer'xi. He has been charged with manslaughter and driving
under the influence on Irul'loa. All accounts indicate he has fled back to his father's island. Arrest him and bring him
to Irul'loa for trial and sentencing.*

---

**Sanctioned Contract:** *Suncatcher - Save Duneburrow from Raiders - 10,000 CR*

*Duneburrow sits near the halfway point between the Near and Far pole of Suncatcher near the equator. Recently a group
of bandits have been terrorizing the settlement, and we are too far away from Dustwall to receive help from the Rovers.
We require a fighting force to deal with the issue permanently.*

---

**Sanctioned Contract:** *Suncatcher - Arbitrate between the locals and the corporations- 6,000 CR*

*AgroBloom has been trying to establish themselves on Suncatcher but has been facing fierce local opposition. The
corporation claims that the locals are being unreasonable, while the Tanners claim that their concerns aren't being
heard. Hear out both sides and try to reach a resolution.*
