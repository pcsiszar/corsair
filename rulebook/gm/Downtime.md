# Downtime & Day-to-Day Operations

Not every hour of a Corsair's career is defined by pressurized breach-charges, howling decompression sirens, or high-stakes firefights through zero-gravity bulkheads. Corsairs are flesh-and-blood operatives who must decompress, treat physical trauma, overhaul battered starships, shop for high-grade ordnance, and scrounge up freelance income to pay dockyard berthing fees. 

Between operational contracts, play shifts into **Day-to-Day pacing**. This macro-pacing scope gives players the creative freedom to pursue personal projects, train new competencies, and manage their ship's logistics, while providing Game Masters with a structured engine to track operational runway, simulate a living station economy, and pace campaign arcs.

---

## 1. The 24-Hour Downtime Round & Shift Structure

Day-to-Day pacing operates on a unified, macro-scale round structure. A single round represents **one full 24-hour day** in the game world. 

At the start of each Day-to-Day round, every player character receives **3 Day-to-Day Action Points (AP)**. Each Action Point represents an **8-hour operational shift**:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                          THE 24-HOUR DOWNTIME CYCLE                                         |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------+-------------------------------------------+
| Shift             | Timeframe          | Operational Focus      | Example Downtime Actions                  |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------+-------------------------------------------+
| Shift 1: Morning  | 08:00 – 16:00      | Primary Operations     | Medbay recovery, hull repairs, market run |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------+-------------------------------------------+
| Shift 2: Afternoon| 16:00 – 24:00      | Secondary Endeavors    | Freelance gigs, tactical training, study  |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------+-------------------------------------------+
| Shift 3: Night    | 00:00 – 08:00      | Rest, Drills, or Gigs  | Evening freelance, simulators, relaxation |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------+-------------------------------------------+
```

### Free Allocation & Sustenance
Characters may allocate their 3 Day-to-Day AP across any combination of downtime activities (such as training, taking freelance gigs, or shopping). Consuming 1 Supply at the End of the Round covers standard meals, personal hygiene, and healthy sleep patterns distributed naturally across the 24-hour cycle. Operatives do not need to spend an AP on sleep unless they are actively seeking medical recovery from **Wounds** or **Stress** through a **Long Rest**.

## 2. Supplies & Operating Runway

A crew's remaining operational runway is measured in **Supplies**. Rather than burdening the table with separate bookkeeping for protein packs, hydroponic greens, reactor coolant cartridges, and life-support filter scrubbing chemicals, all vessel consumption is unified into a single daily burn rate.

### Consuming Supplies: The End-of-Round Heartbeat
At the **End of the Round** (every 24 hours of in-game time), deduct **1 Supply** from the starship’s cargo hold. Paying this 1 Supply sustains environmental life support and refills every crew member's pool back to **3 Day-to-Day AP** for the upcoming day.

### The Consequences of Zero Supply
If the starship enters a new day with **0 Supplies remaining**, the vessel's auxiliary reserves shut down, imposing severe immediate penalties starting that morning:
* **Auxiliary Systems Offline:** All passive internal module benefits (medical bays, fabrication workshops, advanced sensors) power down.
* **Repairs Blocked:** Starship hull damage cannot be repaired by crew or automation.
* **Crew Deprivation:** Life-support scrubbers falter and food rations run dry. Every crew member immediately suffers **1 Light Wound** and **1 Light Stress** at the start of each unsupplied day.
* **Advancement Frozen:** Physical exhaustion and hunger prevent effective practice; characters **cannot spend Day-to-Day AP to gain XP** or train attributes.

### Purchasing Supplies
Supplies can be procured at any civilized port, orbital station, or mining colony. Bulk provisioning offers substantial discounts to cells planning extended interplanetary burns:

| Provisioning Option | Amount of Supply | Total Credit Cost | Effective Cost Per Supply |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Emergency Rations** | 1 Supply | $\$2,000 \text{ Credits}$ | $\$2,000 \text{ Credits}$ |
| **Standard Crate** | 5 Supply | $\$8,000 \text{ Credits}$ | $\$1,600 \text{ Credits}$ *(Save $\$2,000$)* |
| **Sponsor Cargo Pallet** | 10 Supply | $\$12,000 \text{ Credits}$ | $\$1,200 \text{ Credits}$ *(Save $\$8,000$)* |
| **Industrial Haul** | 20 Supply | $\$20,000 \text{ Credits}$ | $\$1,000 \text{ Credits}$ *(Half Price!)* |

### Cargo Capacities by Ship Size
A vessel’s internal cargo volume limits the maximum number of Supplies it can haul:
* **Small Ships (Interceptors, Couriers):** Maximum **10 Supply** (10 days runway)
* **Medium Ships (Corvettes, Cutters):** Maximum **20 Supply** (20 days runway)
* **Large Ships (Frigates, Heavy Transports):** Maximum **30 Supply** (30 days runway)

## 3. Recovery, Long Rest & Healthcare Services

Combat injuries and psychological strain are inevitable hazards of the trade. While field dressings and stim-injectors provide rapid triage during tactical engagements, deep healing requires quiet rest and clinical support.

### The Long Rest Action
A character who spends **1 Day-to-Day AP** resting quietly in their quarters or undergoing treatment in a medical facility executes a **Long Rest**. 

To resolve a Long Rest, the player makes a **Success Test** using **Endurance + Willpower**. Because attribute pairs always use the lower rating as the base dice pool, an operative with Endurance 4 and Willpower 2 rolls 2 base dice.

Every Success Point (SP) generated on the roll is freely allocated to restore the character's Condition tracks:
* **Light Wounds & Light Stress:** Restoring 1 Light Wound or 1 Light Stress costs **1 Success Point**.
* **Aggravated Wounds & Aggravated Stress:** Clearing 1 Aggravated Wound or 1 Aggravated Stress costs **2 Success Points**.
* **Clearing Impaired & Crippled Conditions:** The moment a filled Condition track is cleared of all damage segments, any **Impaired** (1 Downgrade) or **Crippled** (2 Downgrades) condition tied to that track is instantly removed.

### Medical Facility Tiers & Contract Healthcare
Contract payouts and official Corsair sponsorships cover routine and emergency healthcare costs for commissioned cells. When resting in a civilized port or aboard a properly outfitted vessel, characters gain **Upgrades** to their Long Rest Success Test based on the quality of the facility:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                           HEALTHCARE FACILITY TIERS                                         |
+-------------------+----------+-------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Facility Tier     | Upgrades | Standard Availability   | Healthcare Coverage Notes                          |
+-------------------+----------+-------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Crew Bunk / Field | +0       | Ship quarters, tents    | Baseline self-care; roll base Endurance + Willpower.|
+-------------------+----------+-------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Port Clinic       | +1       | Station med-bays, berths| Covered by standard contract berthing & port fees. |
+-------------------+----------+-------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Trauma Center     | +2       | Specialized hospitals   | Covered by sanctioned mission pay & medical riders.|
+-------------------+----------+-------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| Biosculpting Ward | +3       | Elite corporate suites  | Covered by high-tier Corsair sponsor underwriting. |
+-------------------+----------+-------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
```

* **Shipboard MedBays:** A starship equipped with an internal **MedBay Module** provides permanent, autonomous medical care wherever the vessel travels, granting **+1, +2, or +3 Upgrades** to all Long Rest tests performed aboard (see [Starship Internal Modules](../ships/Internal_Modules.md)).

## 4. Training & Character Progression

When operatives have downtime to spare, they can hone their physiological reflexes, technical expertise, or academic mastery.

* **Training AP Cost & Reward:** Spending **1 Day-to-Day AP** on focused physical conditioning, target shooting, flight simulations, or cyber-warfare theory awards **1 Experience Point (1 XP)**.
* **Strict Daily Limit:** To reflect the biological and cognitive limits of daily conditioning, an operative can earn a **maximum of 1 XP from Training per 24-hour round** (spending at most 1 Day-to-Day AP on training per day). The remaining 2 AP must be allocated to other activities (such as medical rest, starship repairs, freelance odd jobs, shopping, or personal projects).
* **Closing the Gap:** Downtime training serves primarily to close the small remaining XP gap (typically 1 to 2 XP) needed to learn a new Skill rank, purchase an appealing Trait, or increase an Attribute after completing an operational mission.
* **Spending XP:** Accumulated Experience Points can be spent at any time during downtime to increase Skills (2 XP per skill rank), acquire new Traits (costing 2 to 8 XP), or improve Attributes (costing $3 \times \text{target level}$) according to the character advancement guidelines in [Advancement](../character/Advancement.md) and [Traits](../character/Traits.md).

## 5. Starship Maintenance & The Ship Fund

Starships endure tremendous strain from orbital maneuvers, atmospheric reentry friction, and kinetic point-defense fire. Maintaining a space-worthy hull requires both vigilant daily labor from the crew and access to industrial drydock gantries.

### The Ship Fund (250,000 Credits Baseline)
Every Corsair cell begins play with a dedicated **250,000 Credits Ship Fund**. This shared treasury is maintained in an escrow account managed by the ship's comms console, earmarked specifically for vessel drydock repairs, hull reconstruction, docking fees, and shared vessel upgrades. Mission bounties and salvage sales can be deposited into the fund to keep the vessel operational.

### Self-Repair (Field Maintenance)
When operating far from civilized drydocks, the crew can perform patch repairs to restore structural integrity:
* **Cost:** Spending **1 Day-to-Day AP** and consuming **1 Supply** (representing structural patch composites, welding rods, sealant resins, and cooling fluids).
* **Requirements:** An engineer equipped with a **Mechanic's Kit** (**Tinkering 2**, **Locksmith 2**).
* **Test:** The engineer rolls a **Knowledge + Tinkering** (or **Finesse + Tinkering**) Simple Test.
* **Outcome:** Each Hit scored restores **1 Light Hull segment**. 
* **Limitation:** Self-repairs **cannot clear Aggravated Hull damage**. Warped structural spars, cracked keel bulkheads, and punctured radiation baffles require industrial heavy drydocks.

### Professional Drydock Overhauls
When the vessel is berthed at an orbital station, naval shipyard, or planetary spaceport with heavy drydock gantries, the crew can contract a full overhaul:
* **Time Required:** **1 Day-to-Day round** (24 hours berthed in drydock) per Hull track repaired.
* **Restoration Rate:** Restores **1 full Hull track** completely, clearing all Light Wounds and all **Aggravated Wounds**.
* **Financial Cost:** Costs **25,000 Credits per track repaired**, paid directly out of the crew's **Ship Fund**.

> **Equipment Upgrades Note:** As established in standard equipment guidelines, installing, calibrating, or swapping mastercrafted weapon and armor upgrade kits takes place naturally during Day-to-Day downtime and costs **0 AP**, provided the operative is aboard ship or has access to a workbench.

## 6. Shopping & Commercial Bazaars

Every civilized spaceport, orbital aerostat, and domed mining settlement hosts bustling commercial concourses where Corsairs can spend their contract earnings.

Spending **1 Day-to-Day AP** on a shopping excursion allows an operative to browse local vendor stalls, military surplus depots, and tech bazaars, purchasing any weapons, armor, ammunition, tool kits, and gear cataloged in the rulebook that falls within that port's commercial market tier.

## 7. Interplanetary Travel & Fuel Consumption

Travel across the Fume system is governed by Newtonian physics and constant fusion burn. Because vessels accelerate toward their midpoint and decelerate into orbital capture, travel times and fuel consumption scale directly with astronomical distances:

| Transit Scope | Time Required | Fuel Cost | Narrative Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Between Sibling Moons** | 1 Place-to-Place round (~1 hour) | **1 Supply** | Quick tactical transfers within binary orbit |
| **Between Binary Moons (Same Giant)** | 1 Day-to-Day round (~8 hours) | **2 Supplies** | Intra-system transfer under constant 1-g burn |
| **Between Adjacent Gas Giants** | 3 Day-to-Day rounds (72 hours) | **4 Supplies** | Long-haul transit through interplanetary void |

## 8. Contracts & Mission Overview

Contracts are the primary lifeblood of a Corsair cell, providing the capital and institutional reputation needed to upgrade weapons, commission ship modules, and maintain operational independence:

* **Sanctioned Contracts:** Missions commissioned by recognized commercial conglomerates, planetary governors, or municipal authorities and vetted by the Corsair Oversight Committee. These tasks represent legitimate law enforcement, pirate interdiction, and hazardous asset recovery. Completing sanctioned contracts is the only path to increasing cell **Reputation**, which unlocks larger ship hulls and military-grade hardware requisitions.
* **Unsanctioned Contracts:** High-paying black-ops tasks offered directly by private patrons who require absolute discretion. While payouts are double or triple those of legitimate work, getting caught performing illegal operations risks severe Oversight reprimands, hefty fines, or expulsion.
* **Freelance Contracts:** Publicly posted odd jobs, civilian bounties, and low-stakes auxiliary tasks available to any licensed operative.

### Mission Payout Scaling

| Contract Tier | Sanctioned Payout (Per Operative) | Base Rep Gain | Experience Award (Per Operative) | Unsanctioned Payout (Per Operative) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 (Local / Precinct)** | $\$5,000 – \$7,500 \text{ Credits}$ | **$+1 \text{ Rep}$** | **$+4 \text{ XP}$** | $\$15,000 – \$25,000 \text{ Credits}$ |
| **Tier 2 (System / Corporate)**| $\$15,000 – \$20,000 \text{ Credits}$ | **$+3 \text{ Rep}$** | **$+8 \text{ XP}$** | $\$35,000 – \$60,000 \text{ Credits}$ |
| **Tier 3 (High-Threat / Black-Site)**| $\$35,000 – \$50,000+ \text{ Credits}$ | **$+7 \text{ Rep}$** | **$+12 \text{ XP}$** | $\$75,000+ \text{ Credits}$ |

## 9. Freelance Work & The Master d100 Odd Jobs Table

Between major contracts, Corsairs frequently pick up local freelance work to pocket extra credits, learn station rumors, or pass the time during long layovers. Freelance gigs are mundane, colorful, and often humorous odd jobs posted on dockside bulletin boards, spacer taproom holoboards, or local municipal job networks. 

* **Closing the Gear Gap:** Freelance gigs are primarily designed to close the small financial gap remaining after a major contract payout—allowing an operative to pocket the final $1,000 to $2,000 Credits needed to purchase a high-end weapon upgrade kit, custom cyberdeck module, or piece of armored kit without waiting for another full mission deployment.

### Resolving a Freelance Gig
Accepting and completing an odd job takes **1 Day-to-Day AP** (one 8-hour shift). The player rolls a **Simple Test** using the Attribute Pair specified by the gig.

* **Payout Formula:**
  * **1 or More Hits Scored:** The character successfully completes the job and earns **500 Credits base pay**, plus **+250 Credits for each additional Hit scored** beyond the first.
  * **0 Hits Scored:** The character earns **0 Credits**. The gig fell through, the employer proved to be a deadbeat, or the job took longer than expected. There is no negative mechanical complication; the operative simply loses that 8-hour shift's earning potential.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                           FREELANCE PAYOUT MATRIX                                           |
+------------------+-----------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Hits on Test     | Credit Payout         | Narrative Outcome                                                |
+------------------+-----------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 0 Hits           | $0 \text{ Credits}$   | Shift wasted; gig falls through or client stiffs on payment.     |
+------------------+-----------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1 Hit            | $500 \text{ Credits}$ | Solid work; baseline contract completed without incident.        |
+------------------+-----------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2 Hits           | $750 \text{ Credits}$ | Great efficiency; employer adds a generous performance tip.      |
+------------------+-----------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3 Hits           | $1,000 \text{ Credits}$| Exceptional work; client is thrilled and offers a heavy bonus.  |
+------------------+-----------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4+ Hits          | $1,250+ \text{ Credits}$| Local legend; employer sings your praises across the concourse.|
+------------------+-----------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 10. Practical Downtime Example: Shore Leave at Port Zenith

> Following a tense pirate interdiction in the upper atmosphere of Landfall, Jax, Silas, and Tessa dock their cutter at **Port Zenith** for a **24-hour layover**.
>
> The GM opens the scene in **Day-to-Day pacing (Round 1)**. Each operative receives **3 Day-to-Day Action Points (AP)** for the 24-hour cycle.
>
> ```
> +-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
> |                                           PORT ZENITH LAYOVER (9 AP)                                        |
> +-------+-----------+--------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
> | Shift | Character | AP Cost| Operational Downtime Action                                                    |
> +-------+-----------+--------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
> | S1    | Jax       | 1 AP   | Medbay Long Rest (Endurance 3 + Willpower 2 test in Port Clinic, +1 Upgrade)   |
> |       | Silas     | 1 AP   | Hull Self-Repair (1 AP + 1 Supply, Mechanic's Kit, Knowledge 4 + Tinkering 2)  |
> |       | Tessa     | 1 AP   | Bazaar Shopping Spree (Standard Crate of Supplies: 5 Supply, 8,000 Credits)   |
> +-------+-----------+--------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
> | S2    | Jax       | 1 AP   | Physical Conditioning (Centrifuge gym workout; gains 1 XP)                     |
> |       | Silas     | 1 AP   | Freelance Gig #01: Lost Featherwing (Instinct 4 + Knowledge 4 Simple Test)     |
> |       | Tessa     | 1 AP   | Freelance Gig #05: Synthwave Bouncer (Strength 3 + Presence 4 Simple Test)     |
> +-------+-----------+--------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
> | S3    | Jax       | 1 AP   | Freelance Gig #02: Spacer Bar Stand-Up (Presence 4 + Presence 4 Simple Test)   |
> |       | Silas     | 1 AP   | Technical Study (Orbital navigation theory; gains 1 XP)                        |
> |       | Tessa     | 1 AP   | Flight Simulator Drills (Emergency thruster failure rehearsal; gains 1 XP)     |
> +-------+-----------+--------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
> ```
>
> ### Shift 1: Morning Operations (3 AP Spent)
> * **Medbay Long Rest (Jax, 1 AP):** Jax took heavy shrapnel during the interdiction, leaving Track I of his Physical Condition filled with 4 Light Wounds and 1 Aggravated Wound (inflicting **Impaired Strength**). Jax books a treatment bunk at Port Zenith's dockside clinic. Contract healthcare covers the clinic fee, granting **+1 Upgrade** to his Long Rest test. Jax pairs **Endurance 3** and **Willpower 2**; taking the lower attribute and applying the clinic's Upgrade gives him a pool of 1d10 and 1d12. Jax rolls a `10` on the d12—scoring **10 Success Points**!
>   * He spends **2 SP** to clear the 1 Aggravated Wound.
>   * He spends **4 SP** to heal all 4 Light Wounds.
>   * With Track I completely cleared, Jax immediately removes the **Impaired** condition from Strength, restoring his full combat capability.
> * **Starship Self-Repair (Silas, 1 AP):** Silas spends his morning shift repairing micro-meteorite hull scorches. He spends **1 Day-to-Day AP** and expends **1 Supply** from the cargo hold. Equipping his **Mechanic's Kit** (**Tinkering 2**), Silas rolls a **Knowledge (4) + Tinkering (2)** Simple Test using the higher attribute (4d10). Rolling `[3, 6, 8, 9]`, Silas scores two Hits (`8` and `9`), cleanly patching 2 Light Hull segments.
> * **Bazaar Requisitions (Tessa, 1 AP):** Tessa navigates the port’s commercial concourse, purchasing a **Standard Crate of Supplies** (5 Supply for 8,000 Credits) to replenish the cargo hold. (Installing upgrade kits onto gear takes place freely during downtime and costs 0 AP).
>
> ### Shift 2: Afternoon Endeavors (3 AP Spent)
> * **Physical Conditioning (Jax, 1 AP):** Jax heads to the station gym to run high-G centrifuge sprint drills. Spending 1 Day-to-Day AP awards Jax **1 Experience Point (1 XP)** (meeting his daily training limit of 1 XP/day).
> * **Freelance Gig #01: Lost Featherwing (Silas, 1 AP):** Silas accepts an afternoon freelance gig to catch an escaped songbird inside ventilation conduits. Silas rolls **Instinct (4) + Knowledge (4)** (4d10): `[5, 8, 8, 10]`—three Hits! Silas lures the creature safely into a cage and collects a top-tier payout of **1,000 Credits** ($500 base + 2×$250 bonus), pocketing cash to close the gap on a new weapon mod.
> * **Freelance Gig #05: Synthwave Bouncer (Tessa, 1 AP):** Tessa works security at an underground modular synthesizer gig, rolling **Strength (3) + Presence (4)** (4d10): `[2, 7, 8, 9]`—two Hits! Tessa escorts out rowdy hecklers without throwing a punch, earning **750 Credits** ($500 base + $250 bonus).
>
> ### Shift 3: Night Shift (3 AP Spent)
> * **Freelance Gig #02: Spacer Bar Stand-Up (Jax, 1 AP):** Jax hits the open-mic stage at a bustling dockside pub, rolling **Presence (4) + Presence (4)** (4d10): `[4, 7, 8, 9]`—two Hits! His dry military humor brings down the house, earning him **750 Credits** ($500 base + $250 bonus).
> * **Technical Study (Silas, 1 AP):** Silas reviews encrypted sensor telemetry and navigational star charts, earning **1 Experience Point (1 XP)** (meeting his daily training limit of 1 XP/day).
> * **Flight Simulator Drills (Tessa, 1 AP):** Tessa runs emergency orbital reentry drills, earning **1 Experience Point (1 XP)** (meeting her daily training limit of 1 XP/day).
>
> ### End of the Round: 24-Hour Heartbeat
> Exactly 24 hours elapse aboard Port Zenith as all 9 Day-to-Day Action Points are spent across the three shifts. The GM resolves the universal heartbeat:
> 1. **Consuming Supplies:** The vessel deducts **1 Supply** from its cargo hold to maintain environmental scrubbers and crew sustenance (reducing the hold from 5 to 4).
> 2. **Refilling Action Points:** Because the ship is fully provisioned, all three operatives refill their pools back to **3 Day-to-Day AP** for the next day.
> 3. **Advancing the World Clock:** The calendar advances by one full day. Port docking clearances renew, and new Tier 1–3 Corsair Oversight contracts populate on commlink consoles.
