# Bestiary: Threats of the Sphere

This catalog provides Game Masters with ready-to-run adversaries and specialized encounters across the Sphere. Rather than an exhaustive encyclopedia of bespoke subsystems, this bestiary is designed primarily as a **thematic location toolkit**: whenever your Corsair cell touches down on an orbital refinery, stalks through a megacity skywalk, or braves the frontier trenches, you can quickly pull appropriate adversaries suited to the environment.

All entries adhere strictly to the standardized framework in [NPC Guidelines](NPC_Guidelines.md). Standard combatants use **Uniform Attributes** (Physical and Mental), flat Health pools, and standard Action Points. Ranged combatants feature an **Accuracy Array** (rated across *Short / Medium / Long / Extreme* range bands) and a Damage Bonus, while pure melee combatants rely on their flat Physical rating and **Melee Damage Bonus**.

## 1. Megacities & Civil Settlements

From the vertical needle towers of Cloudspire to the dense, neon-washed transit hubs of New Babylon on Landfall, urban centers in the Sphere are governed by corporate charters, private security contracts, and the opportunistic predators who dwell in the gaps between security cameras.

### Street Thug / Alley Muscle

Every metropolis produces a surplus of desperate youth and unemployed dock laborers whose primary marketable asset is a willingness to break bones for pocket money. Armed with lead pipes, industrial spanners, and cheap polymer zip-guns that misfire as often as they chamber, street thugs rely entirely on superior numbers to overwhelm solitary couriers or intimidate shopkeepers who fall behind on their protection dues. When faced with professional military ordnance, their enthusiasm tends to evaporate with alarming speed.

**Category:** Standard | **Tier:** 1

* **Phys:** 2 | **Ment:** 2
* **Health (HP):** 9 | **Action Points (AP):** 3
* **Protection:** 0 *(Street Clothes)*
* **Combat:** Acc Array: 2 / 1 / - / - | **DMG Bonus:** +0

### Cloudsurfer

The skywalks of Cloudspire are perpetually plagued by loose confederations of thrill-seeking youths and aerial vandals who turn city transit corridors into illegal velocity courses. Riding stripped-down magnetic hoverboards—noisy, unshielded platforms utilizing ground-effect induction coils and miniature solid-fuel thrusters that smell strongly of ozone and hot lithium grease—they specialize in snatch-and-grab robberies and high-altitude graffiti. While rarely interested in protracted firefights, they are unmatched at hit-and-run harassment, diving off maintenance catwalks to fire off burst sidearms before catching an updraft into the smog.

## 2. The Void, Orbital Stations & Derelicts

Outside the thin bubbles of planetary atmosphere lies the deep void: cold, silent, and crowded with pressurized tin cans, derelict cargo hulls, and those who make a living cutting open airlocks.

### Scav Skitterer

The Scavs—cloned, degenerate biological cast-offs of the mysterious entity known as The Collector—infest the dead hulls and forgotten orbital graveyards of the frontier. Possessing four spindly arms, pale insectoid eyes behind crude welding goggles, and hooked magnetic gloves, Skitterers navigate zero-gravity corridors with unnerving speed. Armed with cut-down scrap carbines, pneumatic rivet guns, and jagged pry-bars, they attack in chattering packs, eager to harvest every scrap of copper, fuel cell, or living tissue to feed the Great Tithe.

**Category:** Standard | **Tier:** 1

## 3. Wastelands, Deserts & Industrial Badlands

On the arid desert surface of X'ara, the toxic sulfur flats of Fume's lower platforms, and the lawless excavation perimeter around the Dustwall, survival is an hourly calculation involving clean water, filter cartridges, and sufficient ammunition to keep the local scavengers at bay.

### Desert Wraith Nomad

Dwelling in the bleached sandstone canyons and baking salt flats of Suncatcher on X'ara, the Desert Wraiths are xenophobic nomadic tribes who have adapted to planetary conditions that would kill an unaugmented human in hours. Draped in camouflaged, moisture-recycling sand cloaks, they move across the dunes with ghost-like silence. They specialize in ambushing corporate survey convoys, using composite hunting bows firing pressurized needle darts and sudden dust veils to disorient outsiders before vanishing into the shimmering heat.

Recruited from veteran drifters and retired prospectors who know the terrain, they dress in dust-scoured duster coats lined with flexible ballistic inserts, wide-brimmed hats, and heavy filtration goggles. They carry lever-action rifles and heavy weighted bolas, preferring to trip up and apprehend fugitives for reward bounties rather than waste ammunition on corpses.

## 4. The Alien Frontier & The DMZ

Beyond the Wanabo buffer zone and along the jagged border trenches of the Kazahn sector lies the Orkish Empire. The Great Truce holds by a thread, and encounters along the frontier involve raw iron, overwhelming ballistic kinetic shock, and disciplined martial traditions.

### Ork Boy (Frontier Shocktrooper)

An Ork infantryman possesses an uncomplicated worldview: what cannot be solved with a high-caliber slug can almost certainly be solved with a three-foot iron cleaver. Towering over standard humanoids with massive bone density and thick, leathery hide, Ork Boyz advance aggressively through incoming fire with deafening battle roars. They carry crude, open-bolt slug-throwers that fire massive high-grain propellant cartridges, caring little for precision as long as the target space is filled with flying metal.

**Category:** Standard | **Tier:** 2

* **Phys:** 3 | **Ment:** 3
* **Health (HP):** 12 | **Action Points (AP):** 3
* **Protection:** 1 *(Layered Scrap Straps & Hide)*
* **Combat:** Acc Array: 3 / 2 / - / - | **DMG Bonus:** +1

Bulking twice the mass of a human in full kit, they encase themselves in heavy plates of riveted pig-iron stamped directly onto motorized hydraulic spine rigs. They carry two-handed rotary thumpers—heavy automatic cannons fed from drum magazines that fire explosive anti-materiel rounds—and use their motorized gauntlets to punch through reinforced airlocks and fortifications.

## 5. Dangerous Planetary Fauna

Not every hostile encounter in the Sphere involves sentients with transponders. Thousands of untamed kilometers across Landfall’s equatorial jungles, X'ara’s desert wastes, and Pelagia’s endless saltwater depths teem with predatory organisms that consider humanoids to be high-protein snacks.

### Dune Silica-Crawler

Native to the shifting red sands of X'ara, Silica-Crawlers are dog-sized multi-legged predatory arthropods with translucent, razor-sharp chitin carapaces formed from bonded quartz. Burrowing beneath the surface near water seepage lines or mining camps, they sense the vibrations of approaching boots. They hunt in swarming packs, erupting from the sand in sudden scuttling flurries to slice through flightsuits with diamond-hard mandibles.

**Category:** Standard | **Tier:** 1

* **Phys:** 2 | **Ment:** 2

## 6. Precursor Vault Guardians

Scattered across deep bedrock, hollowed moons, and ancient subterranean ruins lie the vaults of the Precursors. Built tens of thousands of years before Newcomer colony ships ever dropped into the system, these silent complexes are defended by autonomous constructs that have maintained their security protocols across millennia of isolation.

They emit a faint, high-frequency hum that sets dental fillings on edge. Upon detecting unauthorized biological entities, they unfold into articulated kinetic rings, firing coherent bursts of accelerated magnetic darts with mathematical accuracy.
