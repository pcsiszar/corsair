The Escape Action Economy

The Escape Attempt: Operatives break tactical engagement by committing macro time. Costs 1 Place-to-Place AP and ends your current tactical turn.

Vehicles & Starships: A single driver or pilot spends 1 Place-to-Place AP to maneuver the craft and crew. Passengers pay 0 AP.

Cell Escape on Foot: All fleeing operatives spend 1 Place-to-Place AP each and roll together using Teamwork Dice Swapping.

Pursuer Commitment: Pursuers who wish to give chase must spend 1 Place-to-Place AP to contest. Each pursuer rolls their own separate contest pool! If pursuers have 0 P2P AP or refuse, quarry escapes automatically.

The Guardrails of Disengagement

1. Tactical Clearance (Outside CQC): Must not occupy the same space (0 spaces) as an active hostile and cannot be grappled or pinned. Breaking away in Short Range (1–5 spaces) is permitted, but incurs severe penalties.

2. Viable Egress Vector: Must possess an unobstructed path out (doors unsealed, no dead ends; locked bulkheads must be breached first).

3. Operational Locomotion: Conscious, unrestrained, functional propulsion. Starships must execute Breakaway onto Macro-Grid outside Dogfight Mode first.

Resolution Outcomes: Clean Escape vs. Violent Interception

Outcome Dice Condition Operational Resolution & Stakes
Clean Escape
Success
 Quarry retains ≥1 Hit (8+) after contest dice elimination. Quarry breaks line-of-sight and sensor contact. Encounter zooms out to Place-to-Place exploration at a safe location. Both sides retain remaining broader AP.
Violent Interception
Failure
 All 8+ Hits eliminated by pursuer, or quarry rolled 0 Hits. Drops back to Moment-to-Moment combat where caught. All refill to 3 AP. Pursuers ACT FIRST and place themselves anywhere relative to quarry (surround, CQC, take high cover, or enter Dogfight Mode at Advantage Position 8–10 on tail). Quarry permanently loses spent P2P AP.

Situational Modifiers (GM Adjudication)

The GM awards situational Upgrades or Downgrades based on creative tactics and environmental conditions:

• Obscurance: Smoke canisters, fog, or cutting facility power impose 1–2 Downgrades on pursuers lacking thermal sensors.

• Barriers: Slamming blast doors, dropping crates, or caltrops impose Blocker Effects (1–3 set-aside Hits) or Shrouds.

• Class Features: Hazard smoke/chemical screens, Shadow optical cloaks, or Pilot burns grant Upgrades or Blocker Effects.

• Terrain: Labyrinthine alleys/crowds grant quarry +1 Upgrade with local knowledge; open tarmac grants pursuers +1 Upgrade.
