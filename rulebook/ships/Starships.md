# Chapter 05: Starships & Space Combat

In the airless void of the Sphere, your ship is more than transportation—it is your life support, your operational sanctuary, and your most lethal instrument of survival. Space combat in *Corsair* is an unforgiving dance of orbital mechanics, Newtonian momentum, heat rejection, and physiological endurance. The setting obeys strict hard science fiction constraints: there is no artificial gravity, no faster-than-light transit, and no deflector shielding. Every maneuver burns precious reaction mass, every high-G vector turn crushes human ribs against acceleration couches, and every penetrating round strikes pressurized titanium-composite bulkheads directly.

This chapter covers the complete operational architecture for starships, dual-scale space combat, naval ordnance, internal systems, and flight-ready vessel registries across the following core documents:

---

## 1. [Ship Rules & Space Combat Mechanics](Ship_Rules.md)

Detailed rules governing space flight and naval tactical engagements:
* **Dual-Scale Engagement:** Seamless transition between the **Macro-Grid** (1 hex = 1 km spherical volume, 500m / 2 Success Points per hex) and close-quarters **Dogfight Mode** (Positions 1 to 10 on the Dogfight Scale).
* **Newtonian Momentum & Vector Inertia:** Traversal commitments, vector bleed, and restrictions on reversing into vacated hexes.
* **G-Strain Physiology:** Biological trauma and blackout risks (Light Wounds and Light Stress) from burns exceeding 6 Success Points, unmitigated by armor or protection.
* **Starship Anatomy:** Hull Condition Tracks (`[Hull] -> [Hull] -> [Hull] -> [Hull]`), Armor (Protection), Acceleration, Maneuverability, and Systems attributes.
* **Bridge Command Stations:** Dedicated seats for Pilot, Gunner, and Engineer, including action economy bonuses, Evasive Maneuvers, Stay on Target, Target Locks, Point Defense reactions, and Reactor Power Shunting.
* **Tactical Communications, EWAR, & Combat Depressurization:** Radio silence protocols, passive surveillance, and venting atmosphere to prevent cabin fires.
* **Combat Walkthrough ("The Skirmish at Vesta Void"):** A complete round-by-round procedural example demonstrating macro approach, power shunting, missile salvoes, PDC reactions, dogfight positioning, laser strikes, and contested breakaways.

## 2. [Ship Weapons & Hardpoints](Ship_Weapons.md)

Naval ballistics, hardpoint architecture, and master weapons catalog:
* **Hardpoint Classifications:** Small, Medium, and Large external mounting collars and spinal keels.
* **Guided vs. Unguided Ordnance:** Lead vector calculation with Finesse + Accuracy vs. sensor-locked Tracking rolls.
* **Orbital Ballistics & Recoil Dynamics:** Light-speed sensory latency, overlapping point-defense umbrellas, automated counter-thruster recoil compensation, and armored blowout panel containment.
* **Master Starship Weapons Table:** Complete operational statistics for all 22 naval weapon systems deployed in the Sphere.
* **Detailed Weapon Profiles (1–22):** Individual writeups including physical mounting mechanisms, rates of fire, ammunition logistics, and tactical combat doctrines.
