# Starship Weapons

In the cold expanse of the void, starships engage in lethal exchanges using two primary classes of ordnance: **Unguided
** weapons, which rely on manual lead calculation and rapid tracking, and **Guided** weapons, which utilize
self-contained seeking thrusters and sensor locks to hunt down their prey.

For detailed rules on firing these systems, acquiring target locks, and managing point-defense, refer
to [Ships and Space Combat](file:///c:/Users/csisz/IdeaProjects/corsair/rulebook/ships/Ships.md).

## Special Properties

* **Long-Range Ballistics (Railgun)**: These weapons fire projectiles at such extreme velocities that their accuracy
  dropoff is measured in 10 km (10 hex) increments rather than 1 km (1 hex) increments on the Macro-Grid.

* **Point-Defense (Rating)**: These weapons are equipped with high-speed tracking and fire-control systems designed to
  intercept incoming missiles and drones. The rating indicates the Attribute used in Contest Rolls against guided
  weapon attacks. When used in point-defense, the Gunner uses **Accuracy + Point-Defense Rating**.
