# Starships of the Sphere (The Hangar Bay)

Across the orbital habitats, asteroid belt mining claims, and lawless fringes of the Sphere, starships represent the apex of industrial engineering. From cobbled-together scavenger tugs to military-grade interdictors and crushing dreadnoughts, ten distinct hull classifications dominate operational service.

## Shipyards of the Sphere

* **Grabowski Void Industries (GVI):** Headquartered throughout the lawless asteroid clusters of the Fringe, GVI builds unrefined, heavy-framed vessels assembled from pressurized cargo containers, salvaged ore haulers, and reinforced structural I-beams. Their designs (the *Rusty Hook*, *Ironbound*, and *Bastion*) are notoriously loud, uninsulated, and prone to hydraulic weeping, but their thick industrial armor belts and generous internal mounting volumes make them beloved by pragmatists who demand survivability over elegance.
* **Redfeather Aerospace (RF):** The primary commercial shipwright for colonial planetary defense forces, exploration guilds, and licensed corsairs. Redfeather produces balanced, dependable craft (the *Kestrel*, *Talon*, and *Falcon*) characterized by modular hardpoint rails, standardized avionic buses, and readily available spare parts in every civilized orbital berth.
* **Chidori-Qwei Voidworks (CQV):** Specializing in bleeding-edge sensor arrays, faceted stealth hulls, and ultra-high-efficiency microwave thrusters. The *Shinobi* represents their design philosophy: zero armor, paper-thin structural bulkheads, but extreme maneuverability and electronic invisibility that lets operatives strike and vanish.
* **Caldera Foundry (CF):** The industrial forge-masters of deep space. Caldera specializes in massive electromagnetic coil structures and reinforced naval keels. The *Cinder* frigate is built entirely around an unyielding longitudinal spine, designed to deliver relativistic slugs across interplanetary corridors.
* **Ker'Satz Korp (KSK):** State-contracted military shipwrights producing the apex of naval violence. KSK designs (the *Dagger* interdictor and the terrifying *Leviathan* dreadnought) boast hardened ceramic composite armor, quadruple-redundant avionics, and multi-station fire-control bridges capable of dominating entire orbital sectors.

### Naval Classification & Hull Tiers

Vessels in the Sphere are categorized into three broad structural tiers:
* **Small Craft:** Corvettes, light scouts, and fast interceptors under 80 meters. Agile, high thrust-to-weight ratios, but limited magazine and module volume.
* **Medium Craft:** Patrol boats, armed freighters, and long-range interdictors from 80 to 200 meters. Self-sufficient expeditionary hulls with bunk spaces, closed-loop hydro-recyclers, and heavy battery wells.
* **Large Capital Vessels:** Defensive monitors, strike frigates, and dreadnoughts exceeding 200 meters with multi-deck spinal keels. Strictly space-borne naval leviathans requiring orbital drydocks for maintenance.

### Docking Fees & Reaction Mass Logistics

Maintaining a starship in active flight status requires steady capital. Civilized orbital hubs charge standardized berthing fees:
* **Small Craft:** 500 Credits / day
* **Medium Craft:** 1,500 Credits / day
* **Large Vessels:** 5,000 Credits / day

Refueling reaction mass (purified water-slurry and deuterium fusion pellets) costs roughly **1,000 Credits per macro burn corridor**, ensuring crews must constantly seek profitable corsair contracts to stay space-worthy.
