# Corsair Style Guide

This document establishes the official writing style, formatting, and grammar guidelines for all Corsair rulebook materials. Future coding agents and writers should adhere strictly to this guide to ensure consistency across the codebase and rulebooks.

---

## 1. Spelling & Grammar Standards

- **American English**: Always use American English spelling conventions.
  - *Yes*: `maneuver`, `maneuverability`, `armor`, `specialized`, `defense`, `neighbor`.
  - *No*: `manoeuvre`, `manoeurability`, `armour`, `specialised`, `defence`, `neighbour`.
- **Possessive vs. Contraction**: Pay close attention to "its" vs. "it's".
  - `its`: Possessive pronoun (e.g., "its draconic form", "its special properties").
  - `it's`: Contraction of "it is" or "it has" (e.g., "it's a successful hit", "it's up to the Game Master").
- **Singular vs. Plural Dice**:
  - Use `die` for singular (e.g., "spend a Gambit die", "add a Boost die").
  - Use `dice` for plural (e.g., "your dice pool", "roll three dice").
- **Relative Pronouns**:
  - Use `who` or `whom` when referring to people/characters (e.g., "allies who are in range").
  - Use `that` or `which` when referring to inanimate objects, tools, or rules (e.g., "weapons that deal damage").

---

## 2. Tone and Voice

- **Style**: Direct, technical, and objective, yet engaging.
- **Formality**: Keep rules mechanics formal. Avoid overly casual asides (e.g., replace phrases like "Or not. It's up to them." with objective phrasing like "subject to the GM's discretion").
- **Narration vs. Mechanics**: Draw a clear boundary between flavor text/narration (italicized or clearly separated) and hard mechanics.

---

## 3. Capitalization & Formatting of Game Concepts

To make rules easy to scan and read, key mechanical terms must use standard title casing and formatting:

- **Attributes**: Capitalize and bold core Attributes when referring to them mechanically (e.g., **Strength**, **Finesse**, **Agility**, **Charisma**, **Knowledge**, **Instinct**).
- **Attribute Pairs**: Format as **Attribute + Attribute** (e.g., **Finesse + Agility**, **Presence + Knowledge**).
- **Test Types**: Capitalize and bold the specific test types: **Simple Test**, **Success Test**, **Complex Test**.
- **Roll Types**: Capitalize and bold specific rolls: **Action Roll**, **Success Roll**, **Contest Roll**.
- **Mechanics and Pools**: Capitalize key systems: **Gambit**, **Boost**, **Upgrade**, **Downgrade**, **Contest**, **Teamwork**.
- **Hits/Successes**: Capitalize **Hit** or **Success** when referring to rolling an 8 or higher on a die.

---

## 4. Specific Mechanics to Enforce

When writing or editing rules, ensure these specific design mechanics are consistently described:

- **Gambit Die Preservation**: When a player spends a Gambit die, and the dice pool has already been modified by **Upgrades** or **Downgrades**, the newly added Gambit die retains its original die type.
- **Teamwork in Contests**: In a **Teamwork Success Test**, each 8+ on a **Contest Roll** eliminates the highest die the contested character rolled.
- **Locked Segments & Greater Healing**: Beyond a **Long Rest**, there are currently no other specific examples or mechanisms for **Greater Healing** to clear locked (Aggravated) segments in the **Day to Day** healing section.
