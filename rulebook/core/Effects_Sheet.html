Quick Reference: Effects

Tactical Maneuvers & Narrative Influence

Effects represent tactical advantages, narrative twists, or scene alterations. To activate an effect, you must sacrifice Hits (dice showing 8+) scored during either a Success Roll or an Action Roll. Your declared narration must align logically with the effect and chosen Attribute Pair.

Combat & Performance Effects

Effect Name Activation & Mechanics
Instant Benefits
Crit Effect
Success Roll · Generic
 For every Hit spent on a Success Roll, add +2 Success Points to your total outcome (applied to the highest remaining die).
Chain Effect
Action Roll · Multi-Target
 Spend 1 Hit per additional target eligible for the test. Complex Tests use a single Success Roll applied to all targets. Must pay resource costs for each target. Cannot chain multi-target actions. Ranged attacks must target the same range band; combined Chain and Crit cannot exceed weapon Rate of Fire.
Push Effect
Success Roll · Melee / Physical
 Spend 1 Hit on a Success Roll to displace a target backward up to 4 meters (you may move along at no cost). Restricted to melee attacks and physical actions (cannot be used with ranged attacks or social interactions).
Persistent & Tactical Influences
Upgrade / Downgrade
Action Roll · Advantage / Disadvantage
 Spend Hits from an Action Roll to step up or step down a die on subsequent rolls. On Complex Tests, can apply immediately to step up dice on your upcoming Success Roll without specifying the created advantage.
Blocker Effect
Action Roll · Hindrance
 Set aside Hits from an Action Roll to hinder future actions. Each Hit negates the highest die scored on an opponent's test attempting the blocked action. If no test is normally required, forces a Simple Test first.
Persistent Blocker
Action Roll · Continuous
 Similar to Blocker, but set-aside Hits reset after each blocked attempt. Ends when an action succeeds despite the blocker or conditions expire (e.g., Magnetic Restraints, Smoke Screen).

General Effect Rules

Narrative Consistency: The GM's deciding factor for an effect is: "Is this effect possible using this Attribute Pair and this narration?"

Removing Effects: A creature in a position to remove an ongoing effect can spend 1 Action Point to make a Simple Test, eliminating 1 set-aside Hit per Hit scored on the test.
