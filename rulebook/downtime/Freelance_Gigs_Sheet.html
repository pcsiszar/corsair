Freelance Rules & Tiered Payout Formula

COST: 1 DAY-TO-DAY AP (8 HOURS)
Resolution: Operative rolls a Simple Test using the gig's Attribute Pair (higher attribute is base pool, 8+ is a Hit).
Closing the Gap: Freelance gigs close small credit gaps for gear/mods between missions. 0 Hits = $0 Credits (no penalty).
• 1 Hit: $500 Credits base pay.
• Bonus Hits: +$250 Credits per extra Hit.
• 0 Hits: $0 Credits (shift lost).

GM Advice: Running Freelance Gigs at the Table

• Populating Job Boards: Roll 3 or 4 gigs at the start of layover to post on the comm terminal.
• Teamwork on Gigs: Two operatives can collaborate on the same gig (spending 1 AP each); one assists to grant Boost dice.
• NPC Hooks: Even a simple gig can introduce colorful local contacts, rivals, or patrons.
• Fast Resolution: Keep rolls swift; describe the fun 8-hour shift and award earned Credits!
