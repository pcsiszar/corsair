The 24-Hour Downtime Cycle

Macro Pacing Tier

1 Round = 1 Full Day (24 Hours): Every operative receives 3 Day-to-Day AP representing three 8-hour operational shifts.

Shift 1: Morning (08:00–16:00) · Medbay rest, hull patch repairs, commercial bazaar shopping.
Shift 2: Afternoon (16:00–24:00) · Freelance gigs, physical training, tactical weapon drills.
Shift 3: Night (00:00–08:00) · Rest, cyber-warfare study, flight simulator runs, evening odd jobs.

Sustenance & Sleep: Consuming 1 Supply at the End of the Round covers standard meals, hygiene, and healthy sleep distributed naturally. Operatives only spend an AP on rest when actively treating injuries.

Healthcare & Long Rest

Cost: 1 DtD AP

Long Rest Success Test: Roll Endurance + Willpower (base dice pool = lower attribute). Success Points (SP) are freely allocated across tracks:

• 1 SP: Heals 1 Light Wound or 1 Light Stress.
• 2 SP: Clears 1 Aggravated Wound or 1 Aggravated Stress.
• Clearing a full track removes Impaired (-1 Downgrade) or Crippled (-2 Downgrades).

Starship Maintenance & Upkeep

Ship Fund: 250k Cr Baseline

The Ship Fund: Every cell starts with a dedicated 250,000 Credits escrow account to finance vessel drydocks, repairs, and hull overhauls.

Self-Repair (Field Maintenance):
• Cost: 1 Day-to-Day AP + 1 Supply · Requires Mechanic's Kit (Tinkering 2).
• Test: Knowledge + Tinkering Simple Test.
• Outcome: 1 Light Hull segment restored per Hit. Cannot heal Aggravated damage.
Professional Drydock Overhauls:
• Time: 1 Day-to-Day round (24 hours berthed) per track.
• Outcome: Completely heals 1 full Hull track (including Aggravated).
• Cost: 25,000 Credits per track, paid directly from the Ship Fund.

Training & Upgrades: Spending 1 Day-to-Day AP on training grants 1 XP (Max 1 XP / 24-hr day) to close small skill/trait gaps. Installing/swapping upgrade kits takes 0 AP.

Freelance Work & Mundane Gigs

Cost: 1 DtD AP · Simple Test

Resolution: Roll a Simple Test with the gig's Attribute Pair. Each Hit scores credit earnings:

• 0 Hits: $0 Credits (shift fell through; no complication).
• 1 Hit: $500 Credits base payout.
• 2 Hits: $750 Credits ($500 + $250 tip).
• 3 Hits: $1,000 Credits ($500 + $500 tip).
• 4+ Hits: $1,250+ Credits (exceptional work).
