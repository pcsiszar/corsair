# Melee Weapons

In the close-quarters chaos of boarding actions and urban skirmishes, a reliable melee weapon is often the difference
between life and death. Melee weapons are defined by their **Reach**, **Damage Bonus**, **Penetration**, and **Load**.

## Weapon Statistics Explained

- **Reach**: The effective length and reach of the weapon.
    * **0-2**: These weapons can only target enemies in the **same space**.
    * **3-4**: These weapons can target enemies in the **same space** or **adjacent spaces**.
    * **Reach Advantage**: If you attack a target in melee and your weapon's Reach is lower than your opponent's, you
      suffer **Downgrades** to your attack test equal to the difference in Reach.
- **Damage Bonus**: The flat amount of damage added to the success points of a successful attack. Often calculated based
  on attributes (e.g., `Str/2`).
- **Load**: The weight and bulk of the weapon.
- **Cost**: The average market price in Credits.
