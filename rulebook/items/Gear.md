### Special Properties

* **Jetpack (Xm):** Once per round, you can use the Jetpack to move, using the Jetpack's Thrust attribute to roll. When
  you do, you can buy 4 meters of movement with every Success Point and your base movement becomes X meters. You can
  move in any direction, ignore difficult terrain, and you can hover, but you must descend to solid ground at the end of
  the round. As long as you can activate the jetpack, you can damage from falling.

* **Zipline (Xm):** A Cable Launcher that comes with a built-in cable with a length of X meters. You can deploy it
  between
  two fixed surfaces by using Success Test using Finesse. Once in place, creatures can traverse it via motorized pulleys
  as part of their Movement Action, as long as they move to either fixed end of the cable. You can pick up the Zipline
  from either end, reeling the cable in for 1 AP.

* Gecko Gear: While wearing this equipment, you can stick to any surface. You can also climb surfaces, but your base
  movement speed is 8 meters and you can only buy 1 meter's worth of movement using success points.

### Special Properties

* Area Attack Defense (X): Once per round, this device can intercept an Area Attack. It contests the first incoming Area
  Attack targeting the space it's deployed in, or an adjacent one. If the after the contest there are no dice with an 8+
  value remain, the Area Attack is dispersed entirely.

* Remote Weapon (X): Once per round, this device can intercept an Area Attack. It contests the first incoming Area
  Attack targeting the space it's deployed in, or an adjacent one. If the after the contest there are no dice with an 8+
  value remain, the Area Attack is dispersed entirely. It requires 1 AP to set up and 1 AP to pack up.

* Smartpod (X): This is a deployable smart weapon mount equipped with a motors, joints, and a camera. Once deployed, it
  can take any ranged weapon and fire it remotely, using the AP of you or any ally. It fires using its Smartpod
  attribute instead of Finesse. It takes 1 AP to deploy it, including mounting a weapon, and 1 AP to pack up, including
  dismounting the weapon. When it takes damage, or when you command it to, it locks up and fixes itself into the ground,
  unable to fire, but making it nigh impossible to move the pod or dismount the weapon, so as to protect
  it from falling into the wrong hands.

  Volatile substances in your system.

  day-to-day.
